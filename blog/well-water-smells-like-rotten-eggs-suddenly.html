Well Water Suddenly Smells Like Rotten Eggs

Has your well water developed a sudden sulfur smell, akin to rotten eggs? This is often caused by the presence of sulfur bacteria in the water system.

Causes of Sulfur Smell

This smell is typically due to bacteria that feed on naturally occurring sulfates in the water, converting them to hydrogen sulfide gas. Changes in the aquifer can also contribute to this issue.

Treatment Options

There are several solutions, including shock chlorination, installing a carbon filter, or using an aeration system to remove the sulfur compounds from the water.

FAQs

  • Is the smell harmful? The hydrogen sulfide causing the smell is usually not harmful, but it can be unpalatable and affect water quality.
  • How can I ensure my water is safe? Regular water testing is recommended to monitor and maintain safe water quality.
