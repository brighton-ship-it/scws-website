Frequently Asked Questions

How many water wells are in Wyoming?

Wyoming has approximately 95,000 registered water wells. Laramie County (Cheyenne) has the most with about 14,250.

What is the average well depth in Wyoming?

Average is 250-400 feet. Eastern WY (Ogallala) averages 150-350 feet; western WY 200-600 feet.

How much does it cost to drill a well in Wyoming?

A complete residential well costs $12,000-$28,000. Jackson area is most expensive due to rock formations.

Do you need a permit for a well in Wyoming?

Yes, all wells require permits from the State Engineer's Office. Domestic and stock wells have simplified processes but are still required.
