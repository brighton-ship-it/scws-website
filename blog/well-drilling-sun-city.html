Sun City's Well Systems: Built to Last, Ready for Service

Many Sun City properties were developed with wells drilled in the 1960s through 1980s—systems that were built with the quality materials and construction practices of their era. These wells can continue producing excellent water for many more decades with proper maintenance and periodic updates to pumping equipment.

  • Original Construction: Most Sun City wells were professionally constructed with proper casing and seals, providing a solid foundation for continued service.
  • Aging Equipment: Pumps, pressure tanks, and controls from the original installation may be ready for modern replacements.
  • Mineral Accumulation: Decades of use can allow mineral scale to build up in casings and screens, restricting water flow.
  • Production Changes: Regional groundwater changes may affect production, sometimes requiring well deepening or other modifications.

Well Rehabilitation Services

Before recommending a new well, SCWS thoroughly evaluates existing systems to determine if rehabilitation can restore performance. Many Sun City wells can be brought back to excellent production through targeted rehabilitation techniques that cost a fraction of new well drilling.

Comprehensive Well Evaluation

Our evaluation begins with a complete review of your well's history and current performance. We use downhole video inspection to examine casing condition, screen integrity, and identify any obstructions or damage. This information guides our recommendations and ensures we address actual problems rather than guessing.

Well Cleaning and Rehabilitation

For wells experiencing reduced production, professional cleaning can often restore flow rates dramatically. Over decades, mineral scale, iron bacteria, and sediment can accumulate in casings, screens, and the surrounding formation, restricting water movement. Our cleaning processes remove these deposits and restore the well's ability to produce water.

  • Chemical Cleaning: Specialized solutions dissolve mineral scale and biological deposits.
  • Mechanical Cleaning: Brushing and surging to remove loosened deposits and sediment.
  • Development: Techniques to clear formation materials and improve water flow into the well.
  • Testing: Production testing to document improved performance.

Well Deepening

When cleaning alone doesn't restore adequate production, well deepening may access additional water-bearing zones below the original well bottom. This is often more economical than drilling an entirely new well, particularly when the existing casing and upper construction remain in good condition.

Pump Replacement and System Upgrades

Even when wells remain in excellent condition, pumping equipment wears out over time. Sun City's original well systems likely have pumps that are decades old—well beyond typical service life. SCWS provides complete pump replacement services that modernize your system with current, efficient equipment.

Signs Your Pump May Need Replacement

  • Higher Electric Bills: Aging pumps lose efficiency, consuming more power for the same water production.
  • Reduced Pressure: Worn pump components can't maintain pressure as effectively as new equipment.
  • Frequent Cycling: Short pump cycles indicate pressure tank or pump problems.
  • Noisy Operation: New sounds often indicate bearing wear or other mechanical problems.
  • Water Quality Changes: Sediment in water may indicate pump deterioration.

Modern Pump Technology

Today's submersible pumps are significantly more efficient and reliable than equipment from decades past. Modern stainless steel construction resists corrosion, high-efficiency motors reduce operating costs, and advanced designs provide longer service life. SCWS installs properly sized equipment matched to your well's production capacity and your household's water demands.

Pressure System Services

Your pressure tank and controls work together with your pump to deliver consistent water pressure throughout your home. Sun City systems with original pressure tanks—particularly older steel tanks—may benefit significantly from replacement with modern equipment.

  • Pressure Tank Replacement: Modern bladder tanks provide more consistent pressure and longer life than older designs.
  • Pressure Switch Service: Adjustment or replacement of pressure controls for optimal system operation.
  • System Optimization: Comprehensive evaluation and adjustment for best performance and efficiency.

Water Quality Services

Common water quality services for Sun City properties include water softening for hard water, filtration for sediment or odors, and periodic testing to monitor changes. We provide honest recommendations based on actual test results, never suggesting treatment you don't need.
