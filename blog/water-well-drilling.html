The Drilling Process

  1. Site evaluation - We assess your property, research local geology, and identify the best drilling location
  2. Permitting - We handle all county permit applications and requirements
  3. Mobilization - Our drilling rig arrives and sets up on your property
  4. Drilling - We drill through various formations until reaching a productive aquifer
  5. Casing installation - Steel or PVC casing is installed to protect the well
  6. Well development - We clean and develop the well to maximize water production
  7. Pump installation - Submersible pump, pressure tank, and controls are installed
  8. Water testing - Final flow rate and water quality testing

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does water well drilling cost in California?

Water well drilling in Southern California typically costs $15,000-$50,000 depending on depth, geology, and location. Average cost is $35-$85 per foot for drilling, plus pump installation ($2,000-$5,000) and permits ($500-$2,000).

How deep do you have to drill for a water well in California?

Well depth in Southern California varies by location: coastal areas often reach water at 100-300 feet, while mountain and desert regions may require 400-800+ feet. We research your area's geology before drilling to estimate depth.

How long does it take to drill a water well?

Most residential wells take 1-3 days to drill, plus additional time for pump installation and development. The entire process from permit to flowing water typically takes 2-6 weeks depending on permit processing times.

Do I need a permit to drill a water well in California?

Yes, California requires well permits from your county's environmental health or water resources department. We handle all permit applications as part of our service.

What type of drilling method do you use?

We primarily use mud rotary drilling, which is ideal for Southern California's varied geology including granite, decomposed granite, clay, and sand formations. We also offer air rotary drilling for specific conditions.
