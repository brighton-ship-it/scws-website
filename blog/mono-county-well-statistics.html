Groundwater

Unique geology: Volcanic formations create productive but variable aquifers. Long Valley Caldera area has geothermal influence.

  • Mammoth Basin: Volcanic aquifer with good yields
  • Bridgeport Valley: Alluvial basin with excellent water
  • Water quality: Generally excellent; some areas have elevated minerals
  • Recharge: Sierra Nevada snowmelt
