Why Consider a Well in El Cajon?

Water Cost Reduction

Helix Water District rates include tiered pricing that makes high-volume irrigation expensive. Properties with substantial landscape water needs can often achieve good returns on well investments.

Drought Independence

During drought restrictions, outdoor water allocations may be limited. Well owners maintain control over their irrigation, protecting valuable landscaping.

Property Types Best Suited

Not all El Cajon properties are good well candidates. The best prospects include:

  • Large lots (1+ acres) with extensive landscaping
  • Horse properties and equestrian facilities
  • Properties in eastern El Cajon toward Alpine
  • Hillside properties with adequate lot size

El Cajon Groundwater

El Cajon's valley setting provides varied groundwater conditions depending on location.

Valley Floor

The central valley floor has mixed conditions. Alluvial deposits near drainages may provide groundwater access, though urban development has impacted some areas.

Eastern Areas

Properties in eastern El Cajon, transitioning toward Alpine, often have better conditions—decomposed granite geology similar to successful well areas throughout the region.

Hillside Properties

Properties on the valley's surrounding hills may access fractured rock aquifers, though depths can vary significantly.

Well Services for El Cajon

Irrigation Wells

Properties with significant landscape water consumption can reduce costs with dedicated irrigation wells. Systems integrate with existing landscape controllers.

Horse Property Wells

Equestrian properties need reliable water for pastures, arenas, and livestock. We design systems sized for these demands.

Pool and Landscape Supply

Pools and extensive landscaping consume significant water. Well water avoids premium municipal rates for these uses.

Well Rehabilitation

Existing wells showing declining production may benefit from rehabilitation:

  • Hydrofracturing: Opening new water-bearing fractures
  • Well Cleaning: Removing mineral deposits
  • Pump Upgrades: Modern, efficient equipment

FAQs

Is my El Cajon property a good candidate?

It depends on lot size, location, and water usage. Properties in eastern areas and on larger lots are generally better candidates. We provide free site evaluations.

Do I need permits?

Yes, San Diego County requires well permits. We handle permitting as part of our service.

How long does installation take?

Drilling takes 3-5 days typically. Total project time including permits is usually 4-6 weeks.
