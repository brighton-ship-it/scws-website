About Olivenhain

Olivenhain ("olive grove" in German) was founded in 1884 by German settlers who established a farming community in these rolling hills. Today, the community maintains its rural character through deed restrictions that preserve minimum lot sizes and keep development density low. The historic Olivenhain Town Hall, built in 1895, still serves as a community gathering place.

The area is known for its equestrian community, with many properties featuring stables and riding facilities. The Elfin Forest Recreational Reserve provides over 11 miles of trails on the community's eastern border.

Well Maintenance in Olivenhain

Regular well service in Olivenhain helps prevent costly emergency repairs. With local wells drilled through the Peninsular Ranges batholith, primarily granitic and metamorphic rock, we recommend annual checkups that include water level measurement, pump performance testing, pressure tank inspection, and water quality screening.

Olivenhain well owners should watch for signs of trouble: reduced water pressure, pump cycling frequently, discolored water, or unusual noises from the pressure tank. These often indicate issues that are much cheaper to fix early than after a complete system failure.
