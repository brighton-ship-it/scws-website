About Olivenhain

Olivenhain ("olive grove" in German) was founded in 1884 by German settlers who established a farming community in these rolling hills. Today, the community maintains its rural character through deed restrictions that preserve minimum lot sizes and keep development density low. The historic Olivenhain Town Hall, built in 1895, still serves as a community gathering place.

The area is known for its equestrian community, with many properties featuring stables and riding facilities. The Elfin Forest Recreational Reserve provides over 11 miles of trails on the community's eastern border.

Olivenhain Well Tip

Many Olivenhain wells tap into fractured granite aquifers. Well depths typically range from 300-500 feet, with some properties needing deeper drilling to find adequate production.
