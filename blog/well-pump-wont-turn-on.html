Well Pump Won't Turn On

Dealing with a well pump that won’t turn on can be a major inconvenience for homeowners relying on a private well for their water supply. Understanding why your well pump isn't functioning is critical to restoring your home's water flow efficiently.

Causes

Several issues can prevent your well pump from turning on, including electrical problems, a malfunctioning pressure switch, or a depleted pressure tank. Understanding the root cause can guide the appropriate repairs.

Symptoms

The most obvious symptom is the absence of water when you turn on your faucets. You might also notice no sound from the pump or irregularities like flickering lights if there's an electrical issue.

Solutions

First, check for tripped circuit breakers and inspect wiring connections. If the issue persists, the pressure switch might need adjustment or replacement. For tank issues, a recharge or replacement may be required.

When to Call SCWS
