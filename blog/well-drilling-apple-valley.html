Understanding Apple Valley's High Desert Hydrogeology

Apple Valley sits within the Mojave River Groundwater Basin, one of the most important aquifer systems in the high desert region. Understanding this unique hydrogeological environment is essential for successful well development.

  • Deep Aquifer Systems: Apple Valley wells often need to reach 400-800 feet or deeper to access quality water below mineral-laden shallow zones.
  • Alluvial Basin Fill: The valley contains deep deposits of sand, gravel, and sediments that form productive aquifer materials.
  • Water Quality Considerations: Shallow groundwater in some areas may contain elevated minerals; deeper zones typically offer better quality.
  • Regional Management: The Mojave Water Agency manages groundwater rights in the region, and SCWS maintains familiarity with applicable regulations.

Proper Well Construction

Deep wells require meticulous attention to construction details. Proper casing installation, appropriate screen selection, and quality sealing are critical for well longevity and water quality protection. SCWS follows best practices for deep well construction, ensuring your well will provide reliable service for decades.

  • Quality Casing Materials: Heavy-wall steel casing appropriate for deep installations.
  • Proper Screen Selection: Screens matched to formation conditions for optimal production and sand control.
  • Sanitary Seal: Proper surface seal and annular sealing to protect water quality.
  • Well Development: Thorough development procedures to maximize production.

Horse properties require water for drinking (10-15 gallons per horse daily), wash facilities, pasture irrigation, and arena maintenance. Our systems are sized for peak demand periods, ensuring adequate water during hot summer months when consumption is highest.

Agricultural operations benefit from our experience with high-capacity wells and efficient pumping systems. We understand that reliable irrigation water is essential for crop success and property value, and design accordingly.

Well Rehabilitation Services

Water Quality Considerations

Water quality in Apple Valley wells varies based on depth and location. Shallow groundwater in some areas may contain elevated levels of naturally occurring minerals, including arsenic, fluoride, or total dissolved solids. Deeper wells typically access better quality water below these shallow zones.

SCWS provides comprehensive water testing and can recommend treatment solutions when needed. We believe in honest recommendations—many Apple Valley wells produce excellent water requiring minimal treatment, and we'll tell you if that's your situation.
