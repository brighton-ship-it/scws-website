Common Well Pump Problems in San Marcos

Our San Marcos service experience has revealed the most frequent issues:

  • Motor failures: Heat and heavy use wear out pump motors.
  • Electrical problems: Control boxes, capacitors, and switches fail over time.
  • Pressure tank issues: Bladder failures cause pump short cycling.
  • Scale buildup: Hard water deposits reduce pump efficiency.
  • Impeller erosion: Sand and sediment wear down pump internals.
  • Check valve failures: Worn valves allow backflow and reduce efficiency.
  • Low water conditions: Drought can affect pump performance.

Signs Your Well Pump Needs Attention

San Marcos well owners should watch for these warning signs:

  • Declining water pressure over time
  • Pump running longer to fill the pressure tank
  • Rapid on-off cycling
  • Air spurting from faucets
  • Unusual sounds from equipment
  • Higher electricity bills
  • Sandy or discolored water
  • Complete loss of water
