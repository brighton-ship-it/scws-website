Well Drilling Services Near Coronado

Coronado is San Diego's crown jewel—a picturesque peninsula community known for the iconic Hotel del Coronado, pristine beaches, and historic naval presence. As a fully developed urban area served by the City of Coronado's municipal water system, private well drilling isn't typical within Coronado itself. However, Southern California Well Service (SCWS) serves the greater San Diego County region, including the East County and backcountry areas where private wells are essential.

If you own property in San Diego County's rural areas—from Alpine to Ramona, Julian to Jamul—we're the region's trusted well drilling specialists.
