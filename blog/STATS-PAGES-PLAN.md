# SCWS Stats Pages Plan — 500 Pages

## Strategy
Create "[topic] statistics 2026" pages that journalists/bloggers cite when writing articles. Each page = passive backlink magnet.

---

## Categories & Targets

### 1. State-Level Water/Well Stats (50 pages)
Every US state + DC:
- `water-well-statistics-[state].html`
- Focus on well counts, permits, groundwater, regulations

### 2. California County Stats (58 pages)  
Every CA county:
- `well-statistics-[county]-county-california.html`
- Permits, water table, drilling activity, costs

### 3. Major Topic Stats (100 pages)
Core well/water topics:
- well-drilling-statistics, well-pump-statistics, groundwater-statistics
- water-quality-statistics, water-testing-statistics
- drought-statistics, water-shortage-statistics
- residential-well-statistics, agricultural-well-statistics
- Variations: by year, by region, by type

### 4. Industry Stats (75 pages)
- well-drilling-industry-statistics
- water-well-contractor-statistics  
- pump-industry-statistics
- water-treatment-industry-statistics
- plumbing-industry-statistics
- Variations by year, region, specialization

### 5. Problem/Issue Stats (75 pages)
- well-contamination-statistics
- well-failure-statistics
- groundwater-depletion-statistics
- water-scarcity-statistics
- well-maintenance-statistics
- Variations by cause, region, severity

### 6. Cost/Financial Stats (50 pages)
- well-drilling-cost-statistics-[state]
- well-pump-replacement-cost-statistics
- water-treatment-cost-statistics
- well-inspection-cost-statistics

### 7. Demographic/Usage Stats (50 pages)
- rural-water-statistics
- homeowner-well-statistics
- farm-water-statistics
- off-grid-water-statistics

### 8. Comparison Stats (42 pages)
- well-water-vs-city-water-statistics
- submersible-vs-jet-pump-statistics
- private-well-vs-municipal-statistics

---

## Total: 500 pages

### Execution Plan
- 3 Opus agents at a time
- ~25 pages per agent batch
- 20 batches total
- Estimated completion: 2-3 days at steady pace
