Riverside County's Varied Groundwater Conditions

Riverside County's geology spans from coastal-influenced alluvial basins to desert formations, creating dramatically different groundwater conditions depending on location. Understanding these variations is essential for successful well drilling.

  • San Jacinto Basin: One of the region's most productive groundwater basins, with alluvial deposits providing excellent water yields in many areas of Hemet, San Jacinto, and surrounding communities.
  • Perris Basin: This groundwater basin serves properties in Perris, Menifee, and surrounding areas with varying water quality and production potential.
  • Box Springs Mountain Area: Properties in the hills feature fractured crystalline bedrock requiring specialized drilling techniques.
  • Variable Depth Requirements: Well depths across Riverside County range from under 200 feet in productive basins to over 600 feet in hillside locations.

Our Well Drilling Process

At SCWS, we follow a comprehensive approach to well drilling that maximizes your chances of success while minimizing environmental impact and cost. Our process begins long before any drilling equipment arrives on your property.

First, we conduct a thorough site evaluation, reviewing geological maps, neighboring well logs, and conducting an on-site assessment to determine the optimal drilling location. We consider factors like proximity to potential contamination sources, accessibility for drilling equipment, and the property's topography.

Once we've identified the best location, our experienced drilling crews arrive with state-of-the-art equipment designed to handle Riverside County's varied geology. We use air rotary drilling methods for most applications, which allows us to quickly penetrate rock formations while monitoring water production in real-time.

  • Site Preparation: We prepare the drilling site with minimal disturbance to your property.
  • Drilling Operations: Our crews work efficiently while maintaining the highest safety standards.
  • Well Development: After drilling, we thoroughly develop the well to maximize water production.
  • Water Testing: We collect samples for water quality analysis to ensure your water is safe.
  • Pump Installation: We install appropriately sized pumping equipment for your needs.

Agricultural and Rural Property Solutions

Riverside County's agricultural heritage continues in many areas, from citrus groves and nurseries to equestrian facilities and small farms. These operations depend on reliable water supplies that only properly designed well systems can provide.

For agricultural operations, we design wells and pumping systems capable of delivering the sustained flow rates needed for irrigation. We can install variable frequency drives that optimize energy efficiency and protect your pump from the wear and tear of constant cycling.

Equestrian properties throughout western Riverside County require reliable water for animal care, facility maintenance, and arena irrigation. We size systems appropriately to handle peak demand periods while ensuring consistent availability during hot summer months.

Well Rehabilitation and Deepening

Many Riverside County properties have existing wells that may be underperforming or showing signs of age. Before drilling a new well, it's often worth exploring rehabilitation options that can restore production at a fraction of the cost.

  • Well Cleaning: Removing mineral deposits and biological growth that restrict water flow.
  • Hydrofracturing: Using high-pressure water to open new fractures and improve production.
  • Well Deepening: Drilling deeper to access additional water-bearing zones.
  • Pump Upgrades: Installing more efficient modern pumping equipment.

Drilling Conditions in Riverside

Well drilling in Riverside typically encounters mixed alluvial deposits and crystalline basement rock of the Peninsular Ranges. Most wells can be completed in 1-3 days under normal conditions. The relatively moderate depths keep drilling costs reasonable, though rocky formations can slow progress.

Riverside County well permits are issued through the Environmental Health Department. Turnaround is typically 2-3 weeks. We handle the entire permit process for Riverside properties.
