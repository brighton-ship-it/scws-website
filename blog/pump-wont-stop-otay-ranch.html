Well Pump Runs Constantly in Otay Ranch?

Is your well pump running non-stop? A pump that won't shut off is wasting electricity, wearing out prematurely, and may indicate serious problems with your Otay Ranch well system.

📋 In This Guide

  • Why Pumps Run Constantly
  • Dangers of Continuous Running
  • Immediate Steps
  • Professional Diagnosis
  • Related Articles

Why Pumps Run Constantly

  • Water Leak: Leak somewhere in system preventing pressure buildup
  • Failed Pressure Tank: Waterlogged tank can't hold pressure
  • Pressure Switch Stuck: Switch contacts welded closed
  • Check Valve Failure: Water draining back down well
  • Well Running Low: Pump pulling air, can't build pressure
  • Pump Wear: Worn impellers can't build adequate pressure

Dangers of Continuous Running

  • Electric bills skyrocket
  • Pump motor burns out (expensive replacement)
  • Well can be damaged from over-pumping
  • Potential pipe damage from constant pressure

Immediate Steps

  1. Turn off pump at breaker to prevent further damage
  2. Check for obvious leaks (wet spots in yard, running toilets)
  3. Tap pressure tank—should sound hollow at top, solid at bottom
  4. Check pressure gauge—should read 40-60 PSI when stable
  5. Call for professional diagnosis

Professional Diagnosis

A pump that won't stop can have multiple causes. Our technicians systematically test each component to identify and fix the actual problem, not just the symptoms.
