Well Drilling in Bonita

Bonita — Spanish for "beautiful" — is an unincorporated community known for estate properties, horse ranches, and rural character. Many Bonita properties rely on private wells for domestic, irrigation, and equestrian needs.

Southern California Well Service provides well drilling throughout Bonita and the Sweetwater area. We specialize in estate and equestrian property wells.

Why Bonita Properties Need Wells

  • Estate properties — Large lots, significant water needs
  • Equestrian facilities — Horse country
  • Rural character — Many off municipal water
  • Cost savings — Reduce Sweetwater Authority bills
  • Drought protection — Reliable supply

Well Applications

Bonita wells serve estate needs:

Estate Properties

  • Primary domestic — Sole water source
  • Landscape irrigation — Large grounds
  • Pool/spa — Recreational features
  • Gardens — Food production

Equestrian

  • Arena watering — Dust control
  • Paddock irrigation — Pasture maintenance
  • Barn facilities — Wash racks, drinking
  • Livestock — Horse water supply

Agricultural

  • Small orchards — Citrus, avocados
  • Vineyards — Hobby and small production
  • Gardens — Vegetables, flowers

Local Geology

Bonita benefits from Sweetwater River influence:

Geological Features

  • Alluvium: Sweetwater River area
  • Decomposed granite: Hillsides
  • Stadium Conglomerate: Some areas
  • Fractured granite: Higher elevations

Sweetwater River Influence

  • Alluvial aquifer near river
  • Recharge from Sweetwater Reservoir
  • Generally productive area

Area Typical Depth
