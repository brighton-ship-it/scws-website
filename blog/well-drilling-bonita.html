Well Drilling in Bonita

Bonita — Spanish for "beautiful" — is an unincorporated community known for estate properties, horse ranches, and rural character. Many Bonita properties rely on private wells for domestic, irrigation, and equestrian needs.

Southern California Well Service provides well drilling throughout Bonita and the Sweetwater area. We specialize in estate and equestrian property wells.

Based on California DWR well completion reports. Bonita's average well depth is 275 feet shallower than the San Diego County average of 450 feet.

With 33 wells on record, Bonita has a growing well infrastructure. The wide depth range of 14 to 800 feet reflects the varied terrain and geology across Bonita's landscape. Shallower wells typically tap into alluvial aquifers near drainages, while deeper wells penetrate the Peninsular Ranges batholith, primarily granitic and metamorphic rock to reach more reliable water sources.

Drilling Conditions in Bonita

Well drilling in Bonita typically encounters the Peninsular Ranges batholith, primarily granitic and metamorphic rock. Most wells can be completed in 1-3 days under normal conditions. The relatively moderate depths keep drilling costs reasonable, though rocky formations can slow progress.

San Diego County requires a well permit from the Department of Environmental Health (DEH) before drilling can begin. The permit process typically takes 2-4 weeks and costs $800-$1,500 depending on the parcel. We handle the entire permit process for Bonita properties.
