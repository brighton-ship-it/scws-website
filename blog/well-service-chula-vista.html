Well Maintenance in Chula Vista

Regular well service in Chula Vista helps prevent costly emergency repairs. With local wells drilled through the Peninsular Ranges batholith, primarily granitic and metamorphic rock, we recommend annual checkups that include water level measurement, pump performance testing, pressure tank inspection, and water quality screening.

Chula Vista well owners should watch for signs of trouble: reduced water pressure, pump cycling frequently, discolored water, or unusual noises from the pressure tank. These often indicate issues that are much cheaper to fix early than after a complete system failure.
