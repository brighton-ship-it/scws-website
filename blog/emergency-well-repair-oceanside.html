Warning Signs Oceanside Well Owners Should Know

Watch for these indicators of developing problems:

  • Declining water pressure
  • Pump running longer than normal
  • Frequent pump cycling
  • Air in water lines
  • Sandy or discolored water
  • Changes in taste or smell
  • Higher electric bills
  • Unusual sounds from equipment

Early attention to these signs often prevents complete failures and reduces repair costs.
