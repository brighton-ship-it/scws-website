Well Pump Tripping Breaker

If your well pump keeps tripping the circuit breaker, it disrupts your water supply and indicates electrical or mechanical issues that need immediate attention.

Causes

Potential causes include electrical overloads, short circuits in wiring, or mechanical jams within the pump itself. Each poses a risk to both the pump and your electrical system.

Symptoms

Repeated instances of tripping breakers are common symptoms, along with flickering lights or other electrical anomalies when the pump operates.

Solutions

Examine the wiring for damage, check for obstructions in the pump, and ensure the breaker isn't overloaded. Replacing worn components may resolve this issue.
