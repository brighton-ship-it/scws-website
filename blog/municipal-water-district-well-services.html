Services for Municipal Agencies

Production Well Drilling

New municipal production wells for water supply systems:

  • Test holes and pilot wells: Hydrogeological investigation to site production wells
  • Production well construction: All diameters and depths per specifications
  • Well development: Achieving maximum sustainable yield
  • Pump testing: Comprehensive yield and efficiency testing
  • Video inspection: Downhole camera for construction verification
  • Compliance documentation: All required reports and certifications

Well Rehabilitation

Restoring capacity to existing municipal production wells:

  • Pre-rehabilitation assessment: Video inspection, pump testing, water quality analysis
  • Chemical rehabilitation: Acid treatment, dispersants, biocides for scale and biofouling
  • Mechanical rehabilitation: Brushing, surging, jetting, swabbing
  • Airlift development: High-volume development to restore specific capacity
  • Post-rehabilitation testing: Document improvements, verify success

Pump Installation and Replacement

Municipal pump systems require specialized expertise:

  • Line shaft turbine pumps: Bowl assembly, column pipe, head assembly installation
  • Large submersible pumps: High-HP submersible motor and pump sets
  • VFD integration: Variable frequency drive installation and programming
  • Controls and SCADA: Integration with existing monitoring systems
  • Start-up and commissioning: Performance verification and documentation

Emergency Repairs

24/7 emergency response for municipal well emergencies:

  • Pump failures: Rapid diagnosis and repair/replacement
  • Well collapses: Assessment and remediation
  • Contamination events: Disinfection and return-to-service support
  • Electrical failures: Motor, controls, and VFD emergencies
  • After-hours availability: Critical infrastructure requires 24/7 support

Well Abandonment

Proper destruction of decommissioned wells per California regulations:

  • Assessment: Well condition evaluation, permit acquisition
  • Obstruction removal: Pump, casing, and debris removal as needed
  • Sealing: Cement/bentonite grout per DWR standards
  • Documentation: Well destruction report filed with county

Water District Services

Wellfield Maintenance Programs

Ongoing maintenance contracts for district wellfields:

  • Scheduled inspections: Monthly, quarterly, or annual per contract
  • Performance monitoring: Track specific capacity, pump efficiency, water levels
  • Preventive maintenance: Lubrication, adjustment, minor repairs
  • Trend analysis: Identify declining wells before failures occur
  • Budget planning: Multi-year rehabilitation and replacement scheduling

Video Inspection Services

Downhole camera inspection for well assessment:

  • Condition assessment: Casing corrosion, screen plugging, biofilm growth
  • Obstruction location: Identify depth and nature of blockages
  • Pre/post rehabilitation: Document conditions before and after work
  • New well inspection: Verify construction quality
  • High-definition recording: DVD or digital file for district records

Pump Testing and Efficiency Analysis

Comprehensive testing per AWWA standards:

  • Step-drawdown tests: Determine well efficiency and optimal pumping rate
  • Constant-rate tests: Sustainable yield determination
  • Recovery tests: Aquifer characteristics
  • Pump efficiency tests: Wire-to-water efficiency, energy optimization
  • Recommendations: Optimize pumping schedules, identify upgrade opportunities

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my well pump needs repair?

Common signs include: no water or low pressure, pump cycling on and off rapidly (short cycling), unusual noises, dirty or sandy water, and higher-than-normal electric bills. Any of these warrant a professional inspection.

Can I repair my well pump myself?

Surface-level issues like pressure switch adjustment or breaker resets are safe DIY tasks. However, anything involving pulling the pump from the well requires specialized equipment and should be handled by a licensed contractor to avoid damaging the well casing.
