Heritage Well Service Joins Southern California Well Service

We're proud to announce that Heritage Well Service has officially joined the Southern California Well Service family. This partnership brings together decades of well service expertise to better serve San Diego County.

For Heritage Well Service Customers

If you're a former Heritage customer, nothing changes for you except access to more resources. Your service records are safe, your warranties are honored, and we're here to help.

Call Us: (760) 440-8520 Request Service Online

Why This Matters

Heritage Well Service built a reputation on quality workmanship and honest service over decades serving San Diego County. When they approached us about joining forces, we knew it was a perfect fit.

Both companies share the same values: show up on time, be honest about what's wrong, charge fair prices, and stand behind your work. That foundation doesn't change—it gets stronger.

What's Changed

✓ More Technicians

Expanded team means faster response times across San Diego County

✓ More Equipment

Additional rigs and service trucks for simultaneous jobs

✓ 24/7 Emergency Service

Round-the-clock availability for urgent well and pump repairs

✓ Broader Service Area

Covering more of Riverside and San Bernardino counties

What Hasn't Changed

  • Quality standards: Same commitment to doing the job right the first time
  • Honest pricing: Transparent estimates, no surprises
  • Customer service: Real people answering the phone, not a call center
  • Local ownership: Family-owned business rooted in San Diego County
  • Experienced techs: Many of the same technicians you've worked with

About Southern California Well Service

SCWS was founded in 2020 by Brighton Scala and Travis Sego, but our roots in well drilling run much deeper. Brighton learned the trade working with Joe Fain of Fain Drilling—a company with over 60 years of history in Southern California. Travis started at age 14 with his uncle at Sam Crum Well Drilling.

When we say "60+ years of combined family heritage," we mean it. Acquiring Heritage Well Service added another layer of expertise and history to our team.
