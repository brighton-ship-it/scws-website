Well Pressure Drops During Shower

The frustration of losing water pressure during a shower is not uncommon among well users. This issue often stems from an undersized water system or pipe restrictions.

Causes and Solutions

One major cause of low water pressure during showers is an inadequate well pump system that cannot handle multiple fixtures running simultaneously. Regular maintenance and proper system sizing can prevent this inconvenience.
