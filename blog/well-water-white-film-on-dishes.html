White Film on Dishes from Well Water

Have you noticed a white film on your dishes after washing them? This is a common sign of hard water deposits.

Understanding Hard Water

Hard water contains high concentrations of calcium and magnesium, which can leave behind unsightly deposits on dishes and fixtures.

Solutions for Hard Water

Installing a water softener can reduce mineral content, preventing the formation of film and making home cleaning tasks easier.

FAQs

  • Is hard water harmful? While generally not harmful, it can cause buildup and reduce the efficiency of cleaning supplies.
  • How do water softeners work? Softeners work by replacing hard minerals with sodium through an ion exchange process.
