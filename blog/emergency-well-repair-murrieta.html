Warning Signs Murrieta Well Owners Should Know

Watch for these indicators of developing problems:

  • Declining water pressure
  • Pump running longer than normal
  • Rapid on-off cycling
  • Air in water lines
  • Sandy or discolored water
  • Taste or smell changes
  • Higher electric bills
  • Unusual equipment sounds

Early response to warning signs often prevents full emergencies and reduces repair costs.
