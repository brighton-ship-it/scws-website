Average Cost for Well: $15,000-$50,000 (2026 Pricing Guide)

Updated February 2026 | By Southern California Well Service

Quick Answer: The average cost for a well is $15,000-$50,000 in Southern California. Most homeowners pay $20,000-$35,000 for a complete water well including drilling, casing, pump system, electrical work, and permits.

What's Included in Average Well Cost

Drilling (40-50% of total)

  • $25-$65 per foot depending on geology
  • Well development (cleaning/flushing)
  • Rig mobilization/demobilization

Casing & Screen (10-15%)

  • PVC or steel casing
  • Well screen section
  • Gravel pack and sealing

Pump System (15-20%)

  • Submersible pump and motor
  • Drop pipe and wiring
  • Control box

Pressure System (5-8%)

  • Pressure tank
  • Pressure switch and gauges
  • Check valve and fittings

Electrical & Permits (10-15%)

  • Dedicated circuit installation
  • Well permit fees
  • Water quality testing

What Affects Your Well Cost

  1. Depth: Every 50 feet adds $1,500-$4,000
  2. Geology: Hard rock costs 30-50% more than soft formations
  3. Location: Remote sites cost more for equipment access
  4. Pump selection: Premium brands cost more but last longer
  5. County: Permit fees vary $500-$2,000

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average cost for a well?

$15,000-$50,000 in Southern California, with most paying $20,000-$35,000 for a complete residential well.

What affects the cost of a well?

Depth is the biggest factor, followed by geology, pump size, location, and permit requirements.

How do I know how deep my well needs to be?

Check with neighbors, review county well logs, or get estimates from local drillers who know area conditions.

Is a well worth the cost?

For most rural properties, yes. Wells typically pay for themselves in 10-15 years through eliminated water bills and add value to property.
