Jurupa Valley • Riverside County's Rural Character

Well Service in Jurupa Valley, CA

Expert Well Drilling & Pump Repair for Riverside County's Newest City

SC By SCWS Team | January 30, 2026 • 10 min read

California's youngest city has deep roots in the land. Incorporated in 2011, Jurupa Valley brought together the unincorporated communities of Mira Loma, Rubidoux, Glen Avon, Pedley, and Sunnyslope—areas that have maintained their rural character even as the Inland Empire developed around them. Today, Jurupa Valley remains a haven for horse properties, small farms, and families who value space and independence. Many of these properties depend on private wells for their water supply, and Southern California Well Service has been keeping these wells running for decades.

🐴 Serving Jurupa Valley's Rural & Equestrian Properties

Well drilling, pump repair, and maintenance for residential, agricultural, and equestrian properties throughout Jurupa Valley.

Why Jurupa Valley Properties Need Well Experts

Jurupa Valley's unique character creates specific well service needs. Unlike newer developments where municipal water is standard, many Jurupa Valley properties were established long before city services reached the area:

  • Legacy wells from agricultural era – Many date to the citrus and dairy farming days
  • Equestrian property demands – Horses and livestock need reliable, high-volume water
  • Agricultural irrigation – Small farms and hobby ranches throughout the area
  • Properties without municipal access – Some areas still aren't served by water districts
  • Cost-conscious homeowners – Avoiding monthly water bills for large-lot irrigation

Whether your well is 50 years old or you're planning a new installation, our team understands Jurupa Valley's unique conditions and needs.

Complete Well Services for Jurupa Valley

Well Pump Repair & Replacement

When your pump fails, water stops—and for equestrian properties, that's an emergency. Our pump repair services provide fast response throughout Jurupa Valley. We service all pump types and brands:

  • Submersible pump motor failures
  • Jet pump problems and replacements
  • Pressure tank issues and waterlogged tanks
  • Control box and electrical troubleshooting
  • Low pressure diagnosis and correction
  • Pump upgrades for increased capacity

Many Jurupa Valley wells have pumps that are 20-30+ years old. If your pump is struggling, consider proactive replacement before complete failure leaves you without water.

Well Drilling for New & Replacement Wells

Whether you're developing a new property or your existing well has failed beyond repair, we provide complete well drilling services. Jurupa Valley's favorable geology—alluvial deposits from the Santa Ana River—often allows for productive wells at moderate depths.

We handle all Riverside County permits, drill to productive water zones, install casing and pumps, and leave you with a fully functioning water system.

Well Rehabilitation

Many older Jurupa Valley wells have declining production due to mineral buildup, sediment accumulation, or deteriorating casings. Before drilling a replacement well, let us evaluate whether rehabilitation can restore your existing well. This can save significant money compared to new well construction.

Water Quality Testing & Treatment

Jurupa Valley's agricultural history means some wells have elevated nitrates or other concerns. Our comprehensive water quality testing identifies any issues, and we can recommend or install appropriate treatment systems. Regular testing ensures your water remains safe and suitable for all uses.

Jurupa Valley's Groundwater & Geology

Jurupa Valley benefits from its location along the Santa Ana River corridor, which has deposited thick layers of sand and gravel over millennia. This creates favorable conditions for groundwater storage and well production.

Jurupa Valley Well Depth Ranges

  • Mira Loma area: 150-280 feet
  • Rubidoux / Mission Boulevard: 180-300 feet
  • Glen Avon: 200-350 feet
  • Pedley: 150-250 feet
  • Jurupa Hills foothills: 250-400 feet
  • Sunnyslope: 200-320 feet

Depths vary by specific location. Our site assessment provides accurate estimates for your property.

Water Quality Considerations

Jurupa Valley groundwater quality is generally good, though some areas face challenges:

  • Nitrates – Some areas have elevated nitrates from historical dairy and agricultural operations
  • Hard water – Moderate hardness is common throughout the area
  • Iron – Some wells produce water with noticeable iron content
  • TDS – Total dissolved solids vary by location

All of these concerns can be addressed with appropriate treatment systems. We test all new wells and can perform testing on existing wells to identify any water quality issues.

Jurupa Valley Local Tip

If you're buying an equestrian property in Jurupa Valley, always get a professional well inspection before closing. Evaluate pump condition, flow rate, and water quality—replacing a failed pump or drilling a new well after purchase can be costly surprises.

Special Services for Equestrian Properties

Jurupa Valley is horse country, and horses need a lot of water. A single horse drinks 5-10 gallons daily, plus water for washing, arena watering, and pasture irrigation. Equestrian properties have specific well requirements:

💧 Higher Capacity Pumps

Equestrian properties often need 15-30+ GPM to meet peak demand. We size pump systems for your actual usage patterns.

🔋 Larger Pressure Tanks

Bigger tanks reduce pump cycling and provide reserve capacity for simultaneous water users across your property.

🛡️ Backup Systems

For properties where water failure would be critical, we can install backup generators or secondary pump systems.

🔧 Preventive Maintenance

Annual inspections catch problems before they become emergencies. Essential for properties where animals depend on the water supply.

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep are wells in Jurupa Valley, CA?

Wells in Jurupa Valley typically range from 150 to 350 feet deep. Properties in the Santa Ana River corridor often find water shallower (150-250 feet), while hillside properties toward the Jurupa Mountains may require drilling 300-400 feet.

Do many Jurupa Valley homes have private wells?

Yes, many Jurupa Valley properties—especially in the older agricultural areas, equestrian zones, and rural pockets—rely on private wells. Some areas have never been connected to municipal water, while others maintain wells for irrigation and livestock.

How much does it cost to drill a well in Jurupa Valley?

Well drilling in Jurupa Valley typically costs between $15,000 and $32,000 for a complete residential installation. The relatively shallow water table keeps costs lower than foothill communities. Equestrian facility wells may cost more depending on capacity requirements.

Is Jurupa Valley groundwater safe to drink?

Most Jurupa Valley groundwater is safe for drinking with proper well construction. Some areas have elevated nitrates from historical agricultural use. We recommend water testing for all wells, and treatment systems can address any concerns.

What areas around Jurupa Valley do you serve?

We serve all of Jurupa Valley plus Riverside, Norco, Corona, Eastvale, Ontario, and throughout western Riverside and eastern San Bernardino counties. Our team knows the local geology and conditions.
