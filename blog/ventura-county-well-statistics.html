Frequently Asked Questions

How many wells are in Ventura County?

Ventura County has approximately 12,500 registered water wells.

What is the average well depth?

Residential wells average 250-400 feet.

How much does it cost to drill a well?

A complete residential well costs $28,000-$45,000.

What is the Fox Canyon GMA?

The Fox Canyon Groundwater Management Agency manages several critically overdrafted basins in southern Ventura County, implementing pumping allocations and regulations.
