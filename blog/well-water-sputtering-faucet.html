Why Is My Well Water Sputtering?

Sputtering, spitting, or air-mixed water from your faucets indicates air in your well system. Here's what causes it and how to fix it.

Common Causes

  • Low water level in well: Pump drawing air
  • Failed check valve: Allows water to drain back
  • Waterlogged pressure tank: No air cushion
  • Leak in drop pipe: Air enters at connection
  • Pump set too shallow: Near water surface

Solutions

  • Lower pump deeper in well
  • Replace faulty check valve
  • Replace waterlogged pressure tank
  • Repair drop pipe connections
  • Wait for well to recover (if temporarily low)
