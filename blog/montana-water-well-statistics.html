Frequently Asked Questions

How many water wells are in Montana?

Montana has approximately 175,000 registered water wells. Gallatin County (Bozeman) has the most with about 21,000.

What is the average well depth in Montana?

Average is 150-300 feet. Western valley wells average 65-150 feet; eastern Montana 200-400 feet.

How much does it cost to drill a well in Montana?

A complete residential well costs $10,000-$25,000. Missoula Valley offers lowest costs ($8,000-$18,000) due to shallow productive aquifers.

Do you need a permit for a well in Montana?

Montana allows exempt wells for domestic use up to 35 GPM and 10 acre-feet/year. All wells must be registered and drilled by licensed drillers.
