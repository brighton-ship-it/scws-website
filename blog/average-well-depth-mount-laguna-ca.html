Geology and Groundwater in Mount Laguna

Wells in Mount Laguna reach moderate depths, averaging 262 feet. The area features a mix of alluvial deposits and bedrock formations, with depth varying based on specific location and proximity to fracture zones. San Diego County geology in this region typically involves decomposed granite overlaying harder rock, with groundwater found in fractures and weathered zones.
