What Affects Well Drilling Cost?

Every property is different. Here are the main factors that affect your well drilling quote:

  • Depth required: Based on local water table depth (typically $60-$150/ft in our area)
  • Geology: Rock drilling costs more than soft formations
  • Casing requirements: Steel vs. PVC, diameter needed
  • Pump system: Based on depth and water demand
  • Access: Difficult terrain may require additional equipment
  • Permits: Varies by county and municipality

💡 Typical Cost Ranges in Our Service Area

  • Shallow wells (under 200ft): $12,000 - $25,000
  • Medium wells (200-400ft): $25,000 - $50,000
  • Deep wells (400-800ft): $50,000 - $100,000
  • Extra deep wells (800ft+): $100,000+

These are rough ranges. Your actual estimate will be specific to your property and needs.

Why Get Multiple Estimates?

We always recommend getting 2-3 estimates for well drilling projects. Here's why:

  • Prices can vary 20-40% between contractors
  • Different drillers may recommend different depths or locations
  • You'll learn more about your options
  • You can compare experience and credentials

We're confident in our pricing and service — we welcome the comparison. Our estimates are detailed, honest, and we don't use high-pressure sales tactics.

Questions to Ask When Getting Estimates

  1. What's included in the quoted price?
  2. What happens if you have to drill deeper than estimated?
  3. Is the pump included in the price?
  4. How long has the company been drilling wells?
  5. Are they licensed and insured? (C-57 license required in CA)
  6. What's their warranty on the well?
  7. Can they provide references?

⚠️ Watch Out For

  • • Quotes that seem too good to be true (they usually are)
  • • Contractors who won't put the estimate in writing
  • • High-pressure sales tactics or "today only" pricing
  • • Unlicensed contractors (verify at CSLB)
