Well Water Smells Like Bleach

Experiencing a bleach-like smell in your well water can be concerning, especially after conducting a shock treatment. If the odor persists, it may simply require more thorough flushing.

Why Does This Happen?

A lingering bleach odor often results from incomplete flushing after treating the well water. While it's common, the smell should dissipate with more water usage over time.
