Well Drilling in Oceanside

Oceanside is San Diego County's third-largest city — a diverse coastal community ranging from beach neighborhoods to inland agricultural areas. Many Oceanside properties benefit from private wells, particularly in the eastern agricultural areas and larger residential properties.

Southern California Well Service provides well drilling throughout Oceanside. We serve residential properties, agricultural operations, commercial facilities, and military-adjacent areas.

Why Oceanside Properties Need Wells

  • Agricultural operations — Eastern Oceanside farming
  • Large residential — Estate property irrigation
  • Commercial — Business park landscaping
  • Cost savings — Reduce water district bills
  • Drought protection — Maintain landscapes during restrictions

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep are wells in Oceanside?

Wells typically range from 150-400 feet deep depending on location.

What about coastal properties?

Properties very close to the ocean face saltwater intrusion risks. Inland properties east of I-5 are better candidates.

Do you work on agricultural wells?

Yes, we specialize in agricultural wells for orchards, nurseries, and farming operations.

Is the San Luis Rey valley good for wells?

Yes, the San Luis Rey River valley has productive alluvial aquifers.
