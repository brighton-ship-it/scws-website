Fresno County Well Overview

72,000 Total registered water wells - California's largest well population

Source: California DWR, Fresno County Environmental Health

Fresno County is California's top agricultural producer and has the state's largest water well population. Located in the heart of the San Joaquin Valley, the county relies heavily on groundwater for both agricultural and domestic use.

Key Fresno County Well Facts

  • 72,000+ registered wells - #1 in California
  • 280,000+ residents rely on private wells
  • $7.5 billion annual agricultural production
  • 1,247 new well permits issued in 2025 (highest in CA)
  • Agriculture uses 80%+ of groundwater
  • Multiple SGMA subbasins within county
  • Subsidence areas in western Fresno County

Frequently Asked Questions

How many wells are in Fresno County?

Fresno County has approximately 72,000 registered water wells, the most of any county in California.

What is the average well depth?

Residential wells average 350-450 feet. Western Fresno County requires deeper wells (500-800+ feet).

How much does it cost to drill a well in Fresno County?

A complete residential well costs $32,000-$50,000. Western county costs are higher due to depth requirements.

What is SGMA's impact on Fresno County?

Multiple subbasins are critically overdrafted. GSAs are implementing sustainability plans that may limit new wells and pumping allocations.
