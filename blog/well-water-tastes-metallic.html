Well Water Tastes Metallic

A metallic taste in well water is frequently due to iron, manganese, or copper leaching into the supply, often from plumbing or natural sources.

Identifying the Source

Testing the water can help determine the exact cause of the metallic taste. High levels of these metals are not only unpleasant but can also have long-term impacts on health.

Treatment Options

Treatment systems such as water softeners or iron filters can effectively reduce or remove the presence of metals, improving taste and safety.

FAQs

  • Is metallic-tasting water safe? While often safe, it’s advisable to address the issue to avoid possible health effects from prolonged exposure.
