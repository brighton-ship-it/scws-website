Well Water Tastes Metallic

A metallic taste in your well water is usually caused by iron, manganese, or other dissolved metals. While often not dangerous, it's unpleasant and can indicate changes in your well or groundwater.

Quick Answer

Metallic-tasting water typically means elevated iron or manganese levels. A water test will confirm the cause, and treatment options include oxidation filters, water softeners, or iron removal systems.

Common Causes

!

High iron content (most common)

!

Elevated manganese levels

!

Corroding pipes or well casing

!

Low pH water dissolving metals

!

Changes in groundwater chemistry

What You Can Check

Before calling a professional, here are safe things to inspect:

  • ✓ Check if the taste is in cold water only (well issue) or hot water too (water heater)
  • ✓ Look for rust-colored staining in sinks or toilets
  • ✓ Note if the taste is worse in the morning (sitting water)
  • ✓ Check your water heater anode rod

When to Call a Professional

Call a well professional immediately if:

  • ⚠ Taste appeared suddenly (possible contamination)
  • ⚠ Visible rust particles in water
  • ⚠ Staining is getting worse
  • ⚠ Other water quality changes noticed
