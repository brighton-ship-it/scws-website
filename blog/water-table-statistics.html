Water Table Trends

Long-term Statistics

  • 64% of wells show declining trends over 25 years
  • 22% stable or minor fluctuations
  • 14% increasing (often due to management or wet periods)
  • Average decline: 0.5-2 ft/year in stressed areas
  • Extreme areas: 3-5+ ft/year decline (High Plains, Central Valley)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average water table depth?

Varies by region: 10-50 ft in the East, 100-500 ft in the West.

Are water tables declining?

Yes, 64% of monitored wells show declining trends over 25 years.

Typically 5-20 ft in humid areas, 20-50 ft in arid regions.
