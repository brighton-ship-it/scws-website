Warning Signs to Watch For

Encinitas well owners should be alert to:

  • Declining water pressure
  • Pump running excessively
  • Rapid cycling
  • Air in lines
  • Discolored water
  • Taste or odor changes
  • Higher electric bills
  • Equipment noise changes

Early attention often prevents emergencies.
