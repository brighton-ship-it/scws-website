Emergency FAQs

What should I do while waiting?

Turn off your pump breaker to prevent motor damage. If you have a pressure tank, you may have some water remaining. Avoid running faucets to conserve what's left.

Is there an after-hours fee?

Weekend and evening calls may include a service call fee. We always provide pricing upfront before beginning work.

Can you fix my pump today?

In most cases, yes. Our trucks carry common pumps and parts. Complex situations may require ordering specific equipment.
