About Myers

Company Background

  • Founded: 1870 in Ashland, Ohio
  • Current owner: Pentair (acquired 1999)
  • Market position: Professional-grade, mid-to-upper tier
  • Distribution: Primarily through plumbing/well supply houses

Product Lines

  • Submersible pumps — 4" residential and commercial
  • Jet pumps — Shallow and convertible
  • Sump/sewage pumps — Their original specialty
  • Pressure systems — Tanks and boosters

Myers Submersible Pumps

Strengths

  • Solid construction — Stainless steel components
  • Good efficiency — Competitive performance curves
  • Professional support — Dealer/contractor network
  • Reliable motors — Often Franklin Electric motors
  • Parts availability — Through professional channels

Considerations

  • Not budget-priced — More than big-box brands
  • Less common than Goulds/Grundfos — Fewer installers stock them
  • Residential focus — Commercial options more limited

Typical Lifespan

Myers submersible pumps generally last:

  • Average: 8-15 years
  • Best case: 15-20+ years (ideal conditions)
  • Worst case: 5-8 years (challenging conditions)

This is comparable to other professional-grade brands.

Myers Jet Pumps

Myers also manufactures jet pumps for shallow and convertible applications:

Shallow Well Jet Pumps

  • Water levels up to 25 feet
  • Cast iron bodies with stainless impellers
  • 1/2 to 1 HP typical residential sizes
  • Good value in the professional jet pump market

Convertible Jet Pumps

  • Can operate as shallow or deep well pump
  • Deep well mode uses ejector assembly in well
  • Useful for moderate depth wells (to ~90 feet)

When to Choose Myers

Good Choice If:

  • You want professional-grade without premium pricing
  • Your installer recommends and stocks them
  • Residential or light commercial application
  • Looking for good value in quality pumps

Consider Alternatives If:

  • Heavy commercial or industrial application
  • Your installer prefers other brands they know well
  • Local parts availability is limited
  • Need very high efficiency (Grundfos may be better)

Installation Considerations

What Matters More Than Brand

Pump longevity depends heavily on:

  • Proper sizing — Matched to well and system
  • Correct installation — Wire sizing, torque arrestors, splices
  • Electrical supply — Adequate voltage, proper grounding
  • Water quality — Sand, minerals, pH
  • Usage patterns — Cycling frequency

A well-installed Myers will outlast a poorly-installed premium brand.

Warranty Information

  • Typical manufacturer warranty: 1-3 years
  • Extended warranties sometimes available
  • Professional installation often required for warranty
  • Keep documentation for warranty claims

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Myers well pumps good quality?

Yes, Myers is a solid professional-grade pump brand with over 100 years of manufacturing history. They're now part of Pentair (a major water equipment company) and offer reliable products positioned between budget retail brands and premium options like Grundfos. Myers pumps are commonly used by well contractors and generally provide good value for residential and light commercial applications. They're not as widely stocked as Goulds or Franklin-equipped pumps but offer comparable quality.

How long do Myers pumps last?

Myers submersible pumps typically last 8-15 years with proper installation and favorable well conditions. This lifespan is comparable to other professional-grade brands like Goulds. Actual longevity depends significantly on water quality (sand and minerals are hard on pumps), proper sizing for the application, electrical supply quality, and usage patterns (frequent cycling wears pumps faster). Well-maintained systems in good conditions can exceed 15-20 years.

Is Myers better than Goulds or Grundfos?

All three are quality professional-grade brands that will serve well in residential applications. Myers is generally priced between budget and premium options—offering professional quality without premium pricing. Goulds (Xylem) and Grundfos are often considered slightly higher-end, particularly for commercial applications, with corresponding higher prices. For most residential wells, all three will provide excellent service. The "best" choice often depends on local availability, your installer's familiarity with the brand, and specific application requirements.

Where can I buy Myers pumps?

Myers pumps are sold primarily through plumbing supply houses, well supply distributors, and professional contractor channels—not typically at big-box retail stores. This is actually a quality indicator: professional-grade pumps are distributed through professional channels. Your well contractor can source Myers pumps, or you can find authorized distributors through the Pentair/Myers website. Buying through professional channels also ensures proper support and warranty coverage.
