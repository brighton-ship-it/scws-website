Well Drilling Cost Per Foot: 2025 Pricing Guide for San Diego & Riverside Counties

Published February 2, 2025 | 14 min read

Understanding well drilling cost per foot is essential for budgeting your water well project. In San Diego and Riverside Counties, drilling costs range from $35 to $85 per foot depending on geology and conditions.
