Well Drilling Services in San Bernardino

Residential Well Drilling

For homeowners in San Bernardino seeking water independence, we design and install complete residential well systems. This includes drilling, casing, pump installation, pressure tank, and all electrical connections. Most homes need 5-10 GPM (gallons per minute) for comfortable water supply.

Agricultural & Irrigation Wells

San Bernardino County has a strong agricultural heritage. We drill high-capacity wells for farms, ranches, orchards, and vineyards. Agricultural wells often require higher flow rates (20-100+ GPM) and larger diameter casings to support irrigation demands.

Commercial Well Drilling

Businesses in San Bernardino requiring independent water supply can rely on SCWS for commercial well installation. We work with developers, contractors, and business owners to design systems that meet commercial demand requirements.

Well Drilling Cost in San Bernardino

Well drilling costs in San Bernardino typically range from $15,000 to $45,000 for a complete residential system. Key cost factors include:

  • Drilling depth: $35-$55 per foot depending on geology
  • Casing: Steel or PVC, based on well depth and conditions
  • Pump system: $1,500-$4,500 depending on depth and flow needs
  • Pressure tank: $500-$1,200 for quality bladder tanks
  • Electrical: $800-$1,500 for wiring and controls
  • Permits: San Bernardino County permit fees

We provide detailed written quotes before any work begins—no surprises, no hidden fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to drill a well in San Bernardino?

Most residential wells in San Bernardino cost between $15,000 and $45,000 for a complete system. The price depends on required depth, geological conditions, and pump system needs. Agricultural and commercial wells may cost more due to higher capacity requirements.

How deep are wells in San Bernardino, CA?

Well depths in San Bernardino vary based on location, typically ranging from 200 to 500 feet. Valley properties often find water at shallower depths, while hillside locations may require deeper drilling. We review local well logs during your free site evaluation to provide accurate depth estimates.

Do I need a permit to drill a well in San Bernardino County?

Yes, all new well construction in San Bernardino County requires permits. SCWS handles the complete permitting process, including application preparation, setback verification, and inspection coordination.

How long does it take to drill a well?

Actual drilling typically takes 1-3 days for residential wells. The complete project—including permitting, drilling, pump installation, and testing—usually takes 1-2 weeks from permit approval to water flowing.
