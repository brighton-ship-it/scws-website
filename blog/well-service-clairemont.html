About Clairemont

Clairemont was developed beginning in the 1950s and was San Diego's first "master-planned" community. Developer Lou Burgener created a template for suburban development that would be replicated throughout the region. Today, Clairemont has a population of over 80,000 and includes diverse sub-neighborhoods like Bay Ho, Bay Park, Clairemont Mesa East and West, and North Clairemont.

Older single-family homes, particularly those on larger canyon-edge lots in the original development areas, are most likely to have or benefit from private wells for landscape irrigation.
