Frequently Asked Questions

How many well contractors are in the US?

There are approximately 28,500 licensed water well contractors in the United States, operating through about 8,500 drilling companies.

What is the average salary for a well driller?

Experienced well drillers earn $52,000-58,000 per year median, with driller/owners earning $75,000-150,000+ depending on business volume.

What license is needed in California?

California requires a C-57 Well Drilling (Water) contractor license from CSLB, requiring 4 years experience and examination.

How long to become a licensed driller?

Most states require 2-4 years of supervised experience to qualify for a license. California requires 4 years.

Is well drilling a good business?

Well drilling has an 85% five-year survival rate, above the construction industry average, attributed to consistent demand and high barriers to entry.
