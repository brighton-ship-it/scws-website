Well Pressure Switch Won't Turn Off Pump

If your well pump continues to run and won't turn off, the pressure switch might be stuck or misadjusted.

Diagnosing the Problem

Check the pressure switch for any visible issues like stuck contacts or improper settings. Professional evaluation might be required if these adjustments don't fix the issue.

Why won't my pump turn off?
The usual culprits are a stuck pressure switch or incorrect settings.
