Well Yield Testing (Flow Rate Test)

A well yield test measures how much water your well produces, typically expressed in gallons per minute (GPM). This is critical information for real estate transactions, permits, and system sizing.

When You Need a Yield Test

  • Buying or selling property with a well
  • Applying for building permits
  • Sizing a new pump system
  • Diagnosing low water problems

The Testing Process

  1. Measure static water level
  2. Pump well at known rate
  3. Monitor drawdown over time
  4. Calculate sustainable yield
  5. Document recovery rate

What Results Mean

  • 1-5 GPM: Adequate for small household with storage
  • 5-10 GPM: Good for typical household
  • 10+ GPM: Excellent, supports irrigation
