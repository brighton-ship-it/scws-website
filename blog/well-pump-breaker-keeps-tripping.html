Well Pump Breaker Keeps Tripping

Experiencing repeated tripping of your well pump breaker is a common sign of underlying electrical issues or a failing motor.

Common Causes

If your breaker trips consistently, it could be due to a short circuit, ground fault, or an overloading pump motor.

Safety Steps to Consider

Firstly, unplug any unnecessary devices on the same circuit to ensure the breaker isn't overloaded. Inspect the pump wiring and motor for signs of damage. If issues persist, contacting an electrician is strongly recommended.

When to Call a Professional

FAQs

  • Why do breakers trip? Breakers trip as a safety measure when they detect a fault or overload within the circuit.
  • Can a bad breaker trip frequently? Yes, a breaker itself may be faulty and could be the cause of frequent trips.
