Well Pump Relay Chattering

The chattering sound of a relay can be worrisome. Typically, it's caused by a voltage drop or poor electrical contacts in the relay system.

Identifying the Cause

If your relay is clicking frequently, it may be due to a temporary voltage drop or dirty contacts that need cleaning or replacement.
