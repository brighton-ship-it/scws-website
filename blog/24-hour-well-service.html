Why True 24-Hour Service Matters

Living with a private well means accepting that you're responsible for your own water supply. Unlike city water customers who can call the utility and wait for repairs, well owners face immediate consequences when their system fails. Without water, you can't:

  • Flush toilets or use bathrooms - The most immediate and pressing need
  • Prepare food or wash dishes - Essential for any household
  • Shower or bathe - Necessary for work, school, and hygiene
  • Water livestock or animals - Critical for farms and ranches
  • Run fire suppression systems - A safety concern in fire-prone areas
  • Operate medical equipment - Some devices require water

These aren't problems that can wait until Monday morning. True 24-hour service exists because well emergencies demand immediate attention.

When to Call for 24-Hour Emergency Service

Our 24-hour line is for genuine emergencies. Call us immediately if:

  • You have no water at all and cannot wait until business hours
  • You smell burning or see smoke from electrical components
  • You have vulnerable household members (infants, elderly, medical needs)
  • You have livestock or animals requiring water
  • You're experiencing a water quality emergency (contamination)
  • You have visible damage to wellhead components

For non-emergency issues like low pressure, occasional cycling, or water quality testing, we encourage scheduling a regular service appointment to avoid emergency rates.
