What Qualifies as a Well Emergency?

We consider these situations true emergencies warranting 24-hour response:

✅ YES - Emergency Service

  • Complete loss of water (no water from any fixture)
  • Well pump failure in summer heat with no backup water
  • Sewage-like smell suddenly appearing in water
  • Flooding from a burst pressure tank or line
  • Medical necessity requiring water access
  • Livestock or animals needing water

⏳ Can Usually Wait - Next Business Day

  • Low pressure but water still flowing
  • Minor discoloration that clears when running
  • Pump cycling more frequently than usual
  • Scheduled maintenance or inspection

What to Do While Waiting for Service

If Your Pump Won't Run:

  1. Check the circuit breaker — reset it once if tripped
  2. Check the pressure switch for debris or damage
  3. Listen at the wellhead for humming (motor trying to start)
  4. Don't repeatedly reset a tripping breaker

If You Have No Water:

  1. Confirm it's not a power outage affecting the area
  2. Check the pressure gauge (should read 30-50 PSI)
  3. Turn off the pump breaker to prevent damage
  4. Don't run faucets—save any water in your pressure tank

Temporary Water Solutions:

  • Bottled water for drinking and cooking
  • Fill containers at a neighbor's house
  • Water delivery services (we can recommend local providers)
  • Portable tank if you have livestock
