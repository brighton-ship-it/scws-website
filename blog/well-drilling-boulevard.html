Boulevard's Groundwater

Boulevard spans diverse terrain with variable groundwater conditions. Honest site assessment is essential for success in this area.

Variable Terrain

Boulevard covers a large area with conditions ranging from relatively favorable valleys to challenging rocky hillsides. Water availability varies significantly from property to property.

Fractured Bedrock

Most of Boulevard sits on fractured granite and metamorphic rock. Groundwater is stored in fracture systems, and well success depends on intersecting productive fractures.

Valley and Drainage Areas

Properties near seasonal drainages and valley areas sometimes find better conditions where alluvial deposits supplement fractured rock storage.

Backcountry Considerations

Remote Access

Boulevard properties often have challenging access via unpaved roads. Our equipment handles rough terrain—we evaluate access during free site consultations.

Temperature Extremes

Boulevard experiences freezing winter nights and hot summer days. Systems need protection for both—freeze protection for winter, heat shielding for summer.

Wind

Boulevard is known for wind. Above-ground equipment needs secure mounting and wind-resistant enclosures.

Fire Safety

Backcountry fire risk makes water storage important. We can integrate large tanks for firefighting reserves.

FAQs

Is solar pumping reliable in Boulevard?

Yes. Boulevard's abundant sunshine makes solar highly effective. With proper battery backup and storage, solar systems provide reliable water year-round.

How do you handle remote properties?

Our equipment is designed for backcountry access. We evaluate roads during free site consultations and have handled most Boulevard properties successfully.

What if you don't find water?

Drilling is inherently uncertain. We share what we know about local conditions beforehand. If drilling doesn't produce adequate water, we discuss options including deepening, hydrofracturing, or alternative locations.

Can you service existing wells?

Yes. We repair, rehabilitate, and upgrade existing wells throughout Boulevard. Sometimes rehabilitation can restore production more cost-effectively than new drilling.
