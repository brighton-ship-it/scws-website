Common Well Pump Repairs

Electrical Repairs (Often Same-Day)

  • Pressure switch replacement: $150-$300 — Contacts burn out over time
  • Capacitor replacement: $100-$200 — Failed capacitors prevent motor starting
  • Control box repair: $200-$500 — Common on deep well submersible systems
  • Wiring repairs: $200-$600 — Damaged or corroded connections

Pressure System Repairs

  • Pressure tank replacement: $400-$1,200 — Waterlogged tanks cause short-cycling
  • Pressure gauge replacement: $50-$150 — Accurate readings are essential
  • Pressure adjustment: $100-$200 — Optimize cut-in/cut-out settings

Pump Repairs Requiring Well Access

  • Check valve replacement: $300-$600 — Prevents water draining back
  • Torque arrestor installation: $200-$400 — Prevents pump twisting
  • Pump repositioning: $500-$1,500 — Move pump deeper if water level dropped
