Well Drilling Cost in Riverside County (2026)

Updated February 2026 • By SCWS

What does it cost to drill a well in Riverside County? Most residential wells in Riverside County cost between $15,000 and $45,000 for a complete, turnkey system including drilling, casing, pump, pressure tank, and electrical connections.

Riverside County spans diverse terrain—from the Temecula wine country to the desert communities near Palm Springs. This geological variety means well drilling costs can vary significantly from one property to another.

Riverside County Well Drilling Cost Breakdown

ComponentTypical Cost
Drilling (per foot)$35-$55
Steel Casing (per foot)$15-$28
Submersible Pump$1,500-$4,000
Pressure Tank$500-$1,200
Electrical & Controls$800-$1,500
Permits & Inspections$500-$1,200

Costs by Region

Temecula / Wine Country ($18,000-$40,000)

The Temecula Valley typically has moderate well depths of 250-450 feet. Hillside vineyard properties may require deeper wells and more expensive installations.

Hemet / San Jacinto Valley ($15,000-$35,000)

Valley floor properties often find water at shallower depths (150-300 feet), keeping costs on the lower end. Mountain foothill properties cost more.

Murrieta / Menifee ($20,000-$45,000)

These growing communities have variable geology. Newer developments may have deeper water tables requiring more expensive installations.

Desert Communities ($25,000-$55,000)

Palm Desert, Cathedral City, and eastern Riverside County often require deeper wells (400-600+ feet) and may have water quality issues requiring treatment.

What Affects Your Price?

  • Well Depth: The biggest cost driver—deeper wells require more drilling time, more casing, and larger pumps
  • Geological Conditions: Hard rock drilling costs more than soft alluvial soils
  • Property Access: Difficult access may require smaller equipment or longer setup time
  • Water Requirements: Agricultural wells needing higher GPM cost more than residential
  • Permit Complexity: Some areas have additional requirements adding to costs
