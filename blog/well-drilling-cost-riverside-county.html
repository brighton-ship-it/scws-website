Well Drilling Cost in Riverside County (2026)

What does it cost to drill a well in Riverside County? Most residential wells cost between $15,000 and $45,000 for a complete system including drilling, casing, pump, and pressure tank.

Cost Factors

  • Depth: $35-$55 per foot for drilling
  • Geology: Rocky conditions increase costs
  • Location: Valley vs hillside properties
  • Pump system: $1,500-$4,000 depending on depth

Riverside County Areas We Serve

Temecula, Murrieta, Hemet, Winchester, Menifee, Sun City, Wildomar, Anza, and throughout the county.
