Vista Groundwater Conditions

Vista's geology includes weathered and fractured crystalline bedrock with some areas of sedimentary formations. Groundwater conditions vary across the city's diverse terrain.

  • Fractured Granite: Water stored in fracture networks throughout much of Vista.
  • Weathered Zones: Decomposed rock providing near-surface storage.
  • Alluvial Areas: Valley bottoms may access productive unconsolidated deposits.
  • Santiago Formation: Sedimentary rocks in some areas with different water-bearing characteristics.
  • Variable Depths: Wells range from 200 to over 600 feet depending on location.
