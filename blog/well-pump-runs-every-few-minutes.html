Well Pump Runs Every Few Minutes

If your well pump runs every few minutes, it's likely experiencing a condition known as short cycling. This occurs when the pump repeatedly turns on and off in a short span of time. In this article, we'll explore common causes and solutions for short cycling.

Causes of Short Cycling

Short cycling can be caused by a waterlogged pressure tank, a leak in the plumbing, or an incorrectly adjusted pressure switch. Each of these issues can cause the pump to cycle more frequently than necessary.

How to Diagnose

Begin by checking the pressure tank for waterlogging by tapping on the tank's exterior. If it sounds full, it might be waterlogged. Inspect the plumbing for leaks by checking for water loss when all fixtures are off. The pressure switch settings should also be reviewed to ensure they are set correctly.

FAQs

  • Will short cycling damage my well pump? Yes, frequent cycling can lead to premature failure of the pump.
  • How do I know if the pressure tank is waterlogged? A waterlogged tank will sound full when you knock on it and remain heavy at the top.
