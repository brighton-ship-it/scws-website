About Del Mar

Del Mar (Spanish for "of the sea") was incorporated in 1959 and has carefully maintained its village character while becoming one of California's most desirable addresses. The city hosts the famous Del Mar Thoroughbred Club racing season each summer, and the San Diego County Fair at the Del Mar Fairgrounds draws millions annually.

Del Mar's geography includes coastal bluffs, the San Dieguito River valley, and the Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve to the south. Properties in the inland portions of Del Mar, particularly near Crest Canyon and toward the Carmel Valley border, are most likely to have or benefit from private wells.

Well Maintenance in Del Mar

Regular well service in Del Mar helps prevent costly emergency repairs. With local wells drilled through the Peninsular Ranges batholith, primarily granitic and metamorphic rock, we recommend annual checkups that include water level measurement, pump performance testing, pressure tank inspection, and water quality screening.

Del Mar well owners should watch for signs of trouble: reduced water pressure, pump cycling frequently, discolored water, or unusual noises from the pressure tank. These often indicate issues that are much cheaper to fix early than after a complete system failure.
