Questions we hear before this job

Do Palomar wells freeze?

The water in the borehole does not. Everything above grade does — pitless adapters, exposed poly, pump-house plumbing, and tanks. Winterization is part of this service, not an upsell.

Can you get up the mountain in winter?

We schedule around snow and chain control. If the road is closed, we will say so instead of promising a Tuesday that cannot happen.
