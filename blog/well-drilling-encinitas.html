Frequently Asked Questions

How deep are wells in Encinitas?

Wells typically range from 150-400 feet deep. Valley and lowland areas often find water shallower.

Can I drill a well for my nursery?

Yes, we specialize in agricultural and nursery wells. We size systems for commercial growing operations.

What about coastal properties?

Properties very close to the ocean face saltwater intrusion risks. Inland properties are generally better candidates.

Do you serve Olivenhain?

Yes, Olivenhain is excellent for wells. Larger rural lots and equestrian properties are ideal. See our Olivenhain page.
