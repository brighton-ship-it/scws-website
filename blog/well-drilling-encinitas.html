Olivenhain & Rural Encinitas

While coastal Encinitas relies on municipal water, Olivenhain and the rural inland areas have different characteristics:

Olivenhain

This historic community east of downtown Encinitas features larger estate properties, many with equestrian facilities. Some properties use private wells for irrigation to supplement district water.

Estate Properties

Large properties with substantial landscaping, horse facilities, or agricultural use may benefit from irrigation wells for cost savings.
