Communities We Serve in Riverside County

SCWS provides well services throughout Riverside County, with deep familiarity of each area's unique characteristics and water system needs.

Temecula and Murrieta

The Temecula Valley wine country region represents one of Southern California's most desirable areas, with rolling hills, vineyards, and upscale properties that often rely on private wells. SCWS serves both the established wine estates and newer developments throughout Temecula and neighboring Murrieta. We understand the irrigation demands of vineyard properties and the residential needs of custom home owners. The area's alluvial deposits often provide good water quality, though depth requirements vary significantly based on location relative to the Santa Margarita River watershed.

Hemet and San Jacinto

Hemet and San Jacinto sit in the San Jacinto Valley, an area with significant agricultural heritage and excellent groundwater resources. Many properties in this region have wells dating back decades, requiring ongoing maintenance and eventual rehabilitation or replacement. SCWS provides comprehensive services for these communities, from emergency pump repairs to complete well system upgrades. The valley's deep alluvial fill creates favorable conditions for productive wells when properly constructed.

Menifee and Sun City

Menifee has experienced tremendous growth while maintaining its rural character in many areas. Properties outside the municipal water system depend on wells for their water supply, and SCWS serves these homeowners with the same quality and professionalism we bring throughout Riverside County. Sun City, with its retirement community character, often has older wells that benefit from our rehabilitation services or may require pump system updates for modern efficiency.

Corona and Norco

Corona's eastern areas and the Temescal Valley region include numerous properties served by private wells. The equestrian community of Norco, known as "Horsetown USA," has unique water demands—horses require significant water, and many properties maintain pastures that need irrigation. SCWS has extensive experience serving horse properties throughout Norco, designing and maintaining well systems that reliably meet these elevated demands.

Lake Elsinore and Wildomar

The communities around Lake Elsinore, including Wildomar, include hillside properties and rural acreage where private wells provide water independence. These areas present varied geological conditions, from alluvial valleys to rocky hillsides, requiring experienced drillers who understand local formations. SCWS has successfully drilled and serviced wells throughout this region, providing reliable water solutions for homes, ranches, and small farms.

Perris and Surrounding Areas

Perris and the surrounding rural areas maintain significant agricultural activity, including the equestrian facilities and farming operations that depend on reliable well water. SCWS provides services throughout this region, understanding the importance of keeping agricultural operations running with dependable water supplies.

Well Pump Services

Pump problems are the most common well service call we receive, and SCWS responds quickly to restore your water supply. Our technicians are experienced with all pump types and configurations found in Riverside County wells.

  • Emergency Pump Repair: Rapid response to restore water service when pumps fail unexpectedly.
  • Submersible Pump Replacement: Professional pump pulling and installation with properly sized replacement equipment.
  • Jet Pump Service: Repair and replacement of above-ground jet pump systems common in shallower wells.
  • Variable Frequency Drives: Installation of VFD controllers for improved efficiency and pump longevity.
  • Pressure System Optimization: Tank replacement and system adjustment for consistent, reliable pressure.

Agricultural and Equestrian Well Systems

Riverside County's agricultural heritage and thriving equestrian community create specialized water system demands. SCWS has deep experience serving these customers with systems designed for high-volume, reliable operation.

For horse properties, we design systems that provide adequate flow for multiple watering stations, wash racks, and pasture irrigation. We understand that horses depend on constant water availability, and our systems are designed with reliability as the primary consideration.

Agricultural operations benefit from our expertise in high-production wells and efficient pumping systems. From small hobby farms to commercial operations, we size systems appropriately and design for energy efficiency to minimize operating costs.

Water Quality Services

Water quality varies throughout Riverside County, and many property owners require treatment systems to achieve optimal water quality. SCWS provides comprehensive water testing and treatment solutions.

  • Comprehensive Testing: Laboratory analysis for bacteria, minerals, and contaminants.
  • Water Softening: Treatment for hard water common in many Riverside County areas.
  • Filtration Systems: Sediment removal and whole-house filtration solutions.
  • Treatment Recommendations: Honest assessment of treatment needs based on test results.
