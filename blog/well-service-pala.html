Local notes (not filler)

The San Luis Rey River feeds a shallower alluvial strip. Those wells can move with a dry year. Hillside granite wells are a different animal — more like the rest of North County bedrock. We treat them as two problems. Hard water and sediment from decomposed granite are common; we test before we hang treatment.

Questions we hear before this job

Do you work on tribal trust land?

Private fee-land wells in and around Pala, yes. Wells on Pala Band trust land can need tribal coordination in addition to county rules. Tell us which you have when you call.

How deep are Pala wells?

It depends on the river corridor versus the hillside. Valley properties often sit shallower in alluvium; granite hillsides go deeper. We read your log instead of quoting a county-wide number as if it were yours.
