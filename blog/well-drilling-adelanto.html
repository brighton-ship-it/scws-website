Adelanto's Growing Need for Private Wells

Adelanto has seen remarkable growth over the past decade, driven by affordable land, business-friendly policies, and new industries including renewable energy and licensed cultivation facilities. This growth has created significant demand for private water wells:

  • New development on city outskirts – Properties beyond municipal water reach
  • Commercial operations – Cultivation facilities, warehouses, and manufacturing
  • Agricultural projects – Small farms, greenhouses, and livestock operations
  • Backup water supply – Insurance against municipal restrictions or outages
  • Cost savings – Avoiding expensive connection fees and monthly bills

The Mojave River Basin aquifer beneath Adelanto provides adequate groundwater for properly constructed wells, making private water supply a viable option for both residential and commercial users.

For homeowners and those developing residential properties, a private well provides independence from municipal water systems and the freedom to use water without restrictions.

Residential Well Costs in Adelanto

Residential wells in Adelanto typically cost between $18,000 and $35,000 for complete installation. The final cost depends primarily on depth required and site accessibility. Most residential wells are drilled to 250-400 feet and produce 10-30 GPM—plenty for a single-family home with landscaping.

Commercial Well Drilling in Adelanto

Adelanto has become a significant center for licensed cannabis cultivation and other water-intensive industries. These operations require wells capable of sustained high-volume production with reliable performance.

Commercial Well Requirements

Commercial wells differ from residential wells in several important ways:

  • Higher production rates – 50-200+ GPM depending on operation size
  • Larger diameter casing – 8" to 12" typical for commercial wells
  • More robust pump systems – Sized for continuous or near-continuous operation
  • Additional permits – Commercial water use permits beyond standard well permits
  • Water rights considerations – Mojave Water Agency allocation requirements

Commercial Well Costs

Commercial well drilling in Adelanto ranges from $35,000 to $75,000+ depending on production requirements. Larger operations may require multiple wells or deeper drilling to access sufficient water. We provide detailed assessments for commercial projects, including production estimates based on neighboring well data and hydrogeological analysis.

Adelanto Commercial Tip

Commercial water users in the Mojave Water Agency service area must secure water production rights or purchase "make-up water" for production exceeding base allocations. We can help you navigate these requirements as part of your commercial well project planning.

Understanding Adelanto's Groundwater

Adelanto sits above the Alto Subbasin of the Mojave River Basin, one of the more productive groundwater basins in the high desert region. Understanding this resource helps explain what to expect from well drilling in the area.

The Mojave River Basin Aquifer

The aquifer beneath Adelanto consists primarily of alluvial deposits—sand, gravel, and sediment washed down from the San Gabriel and San Bernardino Mountains over millennia. This material creates relatively good conditions for groundwater storage and production. The Mojave River, though often dry on the surface, recharges the aquifer during wet years.

Adelanto Well Depth Ranges

  • Central Adelanto: 250-350 feet
  • North Adelanto / Air Force base area: 300-400 feet
  • South Adelanto / Victorville border: 250-350 feet
  • West Adelanto / Industrial areas: 300-450 feet
  • East Adelanto / El Mirage border: 300-400 feet

Actual depths vary by specific location. Our site assessment provides accurate projections for your property.

Water Quality Considerations

Adelanto groundwater is generally suitable for most uses, though some areas have elevated total dissolved solids (TDS) or other mineral content. We test all new wells and can recommend treatment systems if needed. For cultivation operations, we can perform specialized water analysis to ensure compatibility with your specific requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep are wells in Adelanto, CA?

Wells in Adelanto typically range from 250 to 450 feet deep. The Mojave River Basin aquifer provides good water production for properly constructed wells. Some commercial operations may drill deeper to access additional water-bearing zones.

How much does it cost to drill a well in Adelanto?

Residential well drilling in Adelanto typically costs between $18,000 and $35,000 for a complete installation. Commercial wells range from $35,000 to $75,000+ depending on production requirements and depth.

Does Adelanto have good groundwater?

Yes, Adelanto sits above the Mojave River Basin, one of the larger aquifers in the high desert. Properly constructed wells typically produce 10-50 GPM for residential use and higher volumes for commercial applications.

Can you drill commercial wells in Adelanto?

Yes, we drill both residential and commercial wells in Adelanto. Commercial wells require larger equipment and higher-capacity pump systems but are well-suited for the area's growing industrial operations.

What areas near Adelanto do you serve?

We serve all of Adelanto plus Victorville, Hesperia, Apple Valley, Phelan, Oak Hills, Lucerne Valley, and throughout the Victor Valley. Our team has extensive experience with high desert geology.
