Well Water Smells Like Cabbage or Vegetables

Experiencing a cabbage-like smell in your well water can be quite disturbing. This odor is usually indicative of sulfur compounds present in the water, similar to the well-known rotten egg smell. In this article, we will explore the common causes of this odor and how it can be treated effectively.

Understanding the Sulfur Smell

The smell of cabbage in well water is often due to hydrogen sulfide gas, which forms from decaying organic matter. This can occur naturally in certain aquifers or can be due to bacteria that use sulfur as a food source.

Is It Harmful?

Though unpleasant, the sulfur smell is not typically harmful. However, it's important to ensure that sulfur bacteria have not contaminated your water system.
