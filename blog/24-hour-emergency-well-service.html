What Qualifies as a Well Emergency?

We prioritize true emergencies to ensure families aren't left without water:

  • ✅ Complete loss of water – No water from any faucet
  • ✅ Pump running continuously – Risk of burnout or flooding
  • ✅ Water flooding from well – Burst pipe or seal failure
  • ✅ Electrical fire smell from pump – Safety hazard
  • ✅ Livestock or medical need – Critical water requirements

What to Do While Waiting for Help

If you have NO water:

  • Check circuit breaker – Reset if tripped
  • Check pressure gauge – Note the reading
  • Turn off water heater – Prevents damage to heating element
  • Gather water for immediate needs (neighbors, store)

If pump is RUNNING CONSTANTLY:

  • Turn off power to pump at breaker
  • Check for visible leaks
  • Close main water valve to house

If you smell BURNING:

  • Turn off power immediately
  • Do not attempt to inspect yourself
  • Wait for professional assessment
