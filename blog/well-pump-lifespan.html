How Long Do Well Pumps Last?

Most well pumps last 8-15 years, though some last 20+ years with good water quality and proper sizing.

Average Pump Lifespan

  • Submersible pumps: 8-15 years
  • Jet pumps: 10-15 years
  • Pressure tanks: 10-15 years

Factors That Shorten Pump Life

  • Sandy or sediment-laden water
  • Frequent cycling (short-cycling)
  • Oversized or undersized pump
  • Power surges and electrical issues
  • Hard water and mineral deposits

Signs Your Pump Is Failing

  • Decreased water pressure
  • Air spurts from faucets
  • Higher electric bills
  • Pump runs constantly
  • Clicking sounds from pressure switch
