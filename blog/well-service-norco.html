Well Maintenance in Norco

Regular well service in Norco helps prevent costly emergency repairs. With local wells drilled through mixed alluvial deposits and crystalline basement rock of the Peninsular Ranges, we recommend annual checkups that include water level measurement, pump performance testing, pressure tank inspection, and water quality screening.

Norco well owners should watch for signs of trouble: reduced water pressure, pump cycling frequently, discolored water, or unusual noises from the pressure tank. These often indicate issues that are much cheaper to fix early than after a complete system failure.
