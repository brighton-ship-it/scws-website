Well Maintenance Checklist for Homeowners

Regular maintenance extends your well's life and prevents costly emergencies. Use this checklist to keep your well system running smoothly.

Monthly Tasks

  • Check pressure gauge readings (should be 40-60 PSI)
  • Listen for unusual pump noises
  • Note any changes in water taste, color, or smell
  • Check for leaks around pressure tank and fittings

Annual Tasks

  • Professional well inspection
  • Water quality testing (bacteria, nitrates)
  • Check wellhead for damage or settling
  • Inspect electrical connections
  • Test pressure switch operation

Every 3-5 Years

  • Full water chemistry analysis
  • Flow rate testing
  • Video inspection if problems suspected
