Well Water Pressure Fluctuates Constantly

If you're experiencing fluctuations in your water pressure, it often points to issues with the pressure tank or switch.

Understanding Pressure Fluctuations

Pressure surges can be caused by a waterlogged tank that isn't able to maintain steady pressure, or a failing pressure switch that isn't regulating the pump properly.

How to Fix the Issue

Inspecting the pressure tank for signs of waterlogging and testing the pressure switch's functionality may reveal the root cause. Both components might need replacement if they are unable to perform properly.

FAQs

  • What causes a waterlogged tank? A failed bladder or air charge can lead to waterlogging.
  • Should I attempt to adjust the pressure switch myself? It's possible, but if you're unsure, professional help is best.
