When to Schedule a Free Well Inspection

Wondering if you should schedule an inspection? Here are common situations where a free assessment is valuable:

Warning Signs That Warrant Inspection

⚠️ Pressure Changes

Water pressure fluctuates or has dropped

⚠️ Strange Pump Behavior

Pump cycles on/off frequently or runs constantly

⚠️ Unusual Sounds

Clicking, humming, or grinding noises

⚠️ Water Quality Changes

Discoloration, sediment, or new odors

⚠️ Higher Electric Bills

Unexplained increase in power usage

⚠️ Air in Lines

Sputtering or spitting from faucets

Proactive Inspection Situations

  • Annual checkup: Just like your car, wells benefit from regular inspection
  • System is 10+ years old: Components have finite lifespans
  • Before summer: Heavy irrigation season stresses systems
  • After power outages: Surges can damage equipment
  • New to well ownership: Understand what you have
  • Planning home improvements: Ensure capacity for additions

Common Issues We Find During Free Inspections

Over thousands of inspections, here are the most common problems we discover:

Waterlogged Pressure Tank

The bladder has failed, causing rapid pump cycling. Usually $800-$1,500 to replace—much less than the pump damage it can cause.

Improper Pressure Settings

Pressure switch set incorrectly, causing poor performance. Often a simple adjustment at no charge.

Compromised Well Seal

Cracked or missing well cap allows contamination. Easy fix that protects water quality.

Worn Pump Approaching Failure

High amp draw or reduced flow indicates pump wear. Replacement on your schedule beats emergency calls.

Corroded Wiring or Connections

Electrical issues that could cause failure or safety hazards. Important to address promptly.

Learn more about common problems in our guides on pressure tank issues and pump troubleshooting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in a free well inspection in San Diego?

Our free well inspection includes: visual inspection of wellhead and cap, pressure tank assessment, pressure switch evaluation, flow rate check, pump amp draw test (if accessible), water pressure measurement, and identification of any visible issues. This helps determine if your well system is functioning properly.

Is there really no cost or obligation for the well inspection?

Yes, our basic well system assessment is completely free with no obligation. We'll inspect your system and tell you what we find. If repairs or services are needed, we'll provide a quote, but you're never obligated to proceed. We believe in earning your business through expertise and honesty.

How long does a free well inspection take?

A free well inspection typically takes 30-60 minutes depending on your system's complexity and accessibility. We take the time needed to thoroughly evaluate your system and answer your questions.

What areas qualify for free well inspections?

We offer free well inspections throughout San Diego County including Ramona, Valley Center, Escondido, Fallbrook, Alpine, Julian, Poway, and surrounding areas. We also serve parts of Riverside County. Call to confirm service in your area.

What's the difference between a free inspection and a paid well inspection?

A free inspection is a basic system check to identify obvious issues and assess overall condition. Paid inspections (for real estate transactions, for example) include detailed documentation, water quality testing, flow rate testing, and a formal written report suitable for lenders or buyers.

When should I schedule a free well inspection?

Schedule a free inspection if you notice: changes in water pressure, unusual pump cycling, strange noises, discolored water, higher electric bills, or if your system is over 10 years old. Annual inspections help catch problems before they become emergencies.

Can you inspect my well if I'm buying a home?

Yes, but home buyers typically need a formal paid inspection with documentation for their lender or transaction. We offer comprehensive well inspections for home purchases that include water testing, flow rate certification, and detailed reports. Free inspections are better suited for existing well owners wanting a system check.

How do I schedule a free well inspection in San Diego?

Call Southern California Well Service at (760) 440-8520 or request an appointment through our website. We'll schedule a convenient time and send a technician to evaluate your well system at no cost.
