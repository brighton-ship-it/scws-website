When Your Well Needs Chlorination

Well chlorination (shock chlorination) is the process of disinfecting your entire well system with chlorine to kill bacteria and other microorganisms. San Marcos well owners may need chlorination when:

  • Positive bacteria test — Coliform or E. coli detected
  • New well — Disinfect before first use
  • After repairs — Pump replacement or well work
  • After flooding — Surface water contamination
  • Rotten egg smell — Sulfur bacteria
  • Slime buildup — Iron or sulfur bacteria

Our Chlorination Process

  1. Calculate chlorine dose — Based on well depth and diameter
  2. Add chlorine solution — Food-grade calcium hypochlorite
  3. Circulate through system — Run to all fixtures until chlorine smell
  4. Contact time — Let stand 12-24 hours
  5. Flush system — Remove all chlorinated water
  6. Re-test — Verify bacteria eliminated

Chlorination Cost

Professional well chlorination in San Marcos typically costs $200-$400 depending on well depth and system complexity. This includes proper chlorine dosing, circulation, and follow-up testing recommendations.

DIY vs Professional

While homeowners can attempt chlorination, professional service ensures:

  • Correct chlorine concentration (too little won't work, too much damages equipment)
  • Proper circulation through entire system
  • Safe disposal of chlorinated water
  • Follow-up testing to confirm success

Ongoing Protection

If bacteria returns repeatedly, you may need continuous disinfection:

  • UV disinfection — Kills bacteria without chemicals
  • Chlorine injection — Continuous low-dose treatment
  • Well rehabilitation — Address the source of contamination
