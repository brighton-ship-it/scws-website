Borrego Springs Groundwater

The Borrego Valley aquifer is a desert basin that has supplied the community for generations, but faces unique challenges.

Valley Aquifer

Borrego Springs sits atop alluvial deposits that form a productive aquifer. Most residential wells find adequate water at moderate depths (100-300 feet), with generally good water access throughout the developed area.

Water Sustainability

The Borrego Valley is classified as a critically overdrafted groundwater basin under California's Sustainable Groundwater Management Act (SGMA). The Borrego Valley Groundwater Sustainability Agency is implementing a plan to bring the basin into balance over time. New wells may be subject to permitting requirements and allocation systems.

Water Quality Considerations

Desert groundwater often contains elevated minerals. Borrego Springs water quality varies by location and depth—some areas have excellent water while others require treatment for hardness or other dissolved minerals.

Extreme Heat

Summer temperatures exceed 115°F regularly. All above-ground equipment needs protection—shaded enclosures, adequate ventilation, and heat-resistant components.

Water Treatment

Desert water often requires treatment for household use. We can recommend appropriate solutions based on your well's water quality testing.

FAQs

Can I still drill a new well in Borrego Springs?

Generally yes, though new wells may be subject to permits and allocations under the Groundwater Sustainability Plan. We can help you understand current requirements for your specific situation.

What about the aquifer overdraft?

The basin is working toward sustainability over a 20-year timeline. Existing residential wells remain viable; the plan focuses on reducing agricultural pumping. Efficient, appropriately sized wells for residential use remain practical.

Is solar pumping practical in Borrego?

Very much so. Borrego Springs has some of California's most abundant sunshine, making solar pumping highly effective. Combined with storage, it provides reliable water with minimal ongoing costs.

What about water quality?

Quality varies by location. We test every well and can recommend treatment for any issues. Many Borrego wells produce water that's fine with basic treatment for hardness.
