Well Maintenance in Big Bear City

Regular well service in Big Bear City helps prevent costly emergency repairs. With local wells drilled through Mojave Desert alluvial basins and Transverse Ranges crystalline rock, we recommend annual checkups that include water level measurement, pump performance testing, pressure tank inspection, and water quality screening.

Big Bear City well owners should watch for signs of trouble: reduced water pressure, pump cycling frequently, discolored water, or unusual noises from the pressure tank. These often indicate issues that are much cheaper to fix early than after a complete system failure.
