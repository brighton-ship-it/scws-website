Beaumont sits in the San Gorgonio Pass, the corridor between the San Bernardino and San Jacinto Mountains that has been Southern California's eastern gateway for generations. The pass's unique geography creates productive groundwater resources that have supported agriculture and, increasingly, residential development. At Southern California Well Service, we provide expert well services throughout the Beaumont-Cherry Valley area.

Beaumont Area Well Conditions

Pass Groundwater Basin

The San Gorgonio Pass contains significant groundwater resources, recharged by mountain runoff from both the San Bernardino and San Jacinto ranges. Well depths vary but typically range from 200 to 450 feet.

Variable Geology

The pass includes both alluvial deposits and areas closer to bedrock. Drilling conditions and well productivity can vary significantly across short distances.

Growing Demand

Beaumont's rapid residential growth increases demand on groundwater resources. Efficient well systems and proper maintenance help ensure sustainable water supply.

Beaumont Well Characteristics

  • Typical depths: 200-450 feet
  • Elevation: ~2,500-3,000 feet
  • Flow rates: 15-60+ GPM
  • Water quality: Generally good
