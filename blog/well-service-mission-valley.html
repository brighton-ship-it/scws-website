About Mission Valley

Mission Valley follows the San Diego River from Old Town to SDSU's new Mission Valley campus (the former Qualcomm/SDCCU Stadium site). The valley has been a major commercial area since the 1960s and continues to evolve with new mixed-use developments. Mission San Diego de Alcalá, California's first mission, is located in the eastern portion of the valley.

Hotels, golf courses (like the Riverwalk Golf Club), and commercial landscape operations may benefit from dedicated well water for irrigation purposes.
