Artesian Well Costs

  • Standard artesian: $40,000-65,000 (15-25% premium)
  • Flowing artesian: $50,000-85,000+
  • Control valve: $500-2,500
  • Enhanced casing: $3,000-8,000 additional
  • Pressure grouting: Required, adds $2,000-5,000

Frequently Asked Questions

What percentage of wells are artesian?

Approximately 5-8% are artesian; only 1-2% are true flowing wells.

Do artesian wells need pumps?

Flowing artesian wells may not need pumps; others still require pumping.

How much more do they cost?

Artesian wells cost 15-30% more due to special construction requirements.
