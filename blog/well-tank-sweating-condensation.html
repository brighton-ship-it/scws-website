Well Pressure Tank Sweating or Condensation

Noticing condensation or sweating on your well pressure tank? In many cases, this is a normal result of humid weather.

Understanding Tank Condensation

Cold water inside the tank can cool the metal, causing perspiration when humid air contacts the surface. This is typically harmless unless it leads to pooling water.

When to Be Concerned

If you see water pooling around the tank, ensure it’s not due to a leak. Persistent condensation might indicate insufficient ventilation and insulation.

FAQs

  • Is tank condensation normal? Yes, especially in high humidity. Proper ventilation can minimize it.
