Well Drilling in Rancho Bernardo

Well Depths in Rancho Bernardo

Well depths vary across RB based on location and geology:

Typical Depth Ranges

Area Typical Depth
Valley/lower areas 200-300 ft
Central RB 250-350 ft
Higher elevations 300-450 ft
Near 4S Ranch 250-400 ft

Yield Expectations

  • Lower producers: 5-15 GPM
  • Average: 15-40 GPM
  • Good producers: 45-80+ GPM

Local Geology

  • Alluvium: Valley areas, productive
  • Friars Formation: Sandstone layers
  • Stadium Conglomerate: Deeper zones
  • Granite: In some hillside areas
