Benefits of Wells in Rancho Bernardo

Significant Cost Savings

San Diego municipal water rates continue rising, with tiered pricing that dramatically increases costs for high-volume users. A well providing irrigation water can reduce your water bill by thousands annually—often paying for itself within a few years.

Drought Independence

During drought restrictions, municipal water allocations may limit outdoor watering. Well owners maintain control over their irrigation schedule, protecting landscape investments through dry periods.

Landscape Health

Many plants actually prefer well water to treated municipal water. Without chlorine and with natural mineral content, well water often supports healthier plant growth.

Property Value

A working well system adds value to properties by demonstrating water security and reduced operating costs—attractive features for future buyers.

Rancho Bernardo Groundwater Conditions

RB's location along the San Dieguito River drainage and its varied topography create diverse groundwater conditions.

Valley and Lower Elevations

Properties near the San Dieguito River corridor and lower elevations often encounter alluvial aquifers at moderate depths (150-300 feet). These areas typically offer good production potential for irrigation wells.

Hillside Properties

Higher elevation areas in RB transition to decomposed granite and fractured bedrock. Wells in these zones typically range 250-400 feet and require techniques suited to hard rock drilling.

Property Requirements

Not every RB property can accommodate a well. Key considerations include:

  • Lot Size: Larger lots generally have more flexibility for well placement with proper setbacks
  • Setbacks: County requires minimum distances from septic systems, property lines, and structures
  • Access: Drilling equipment needs reasonable access to the drill site
  • HOA Rules: Some associations may have restrictions on well equipment

FAQs

Can I drill a well on a typical RB lot?

It depends on lot size, configuration, and setback requirements. Many larger RB lots (0.5 acre+) can accommodate wells. Our free evaluation determines feasibility for your specific property.

Will my HOA allow a well?

Most HOAs don't prohibit wells, though some may have requirements for equipment screening. We can help you understand requirements and design compliant installations.

How much will I save on water bills?

Savings depend on your irrigation usage. Properties spending $300-600+ monthly on irrigation often see substantial savings. We can estimate potential savings based on your water bills.

Can I use well water for my pool?

Yes. Many RB pool owners use well water for filling and maintenance, avoiding municipal water costs for these high-volume uses.
