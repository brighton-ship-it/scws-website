Well Drilling in San Ysidro

San Ysidro is San Diego's southernmost community, located directly at the US-Mexico border. The area includes the busiest land border crossing in the Western Hemisphere, making it a hub for commercial activity, logistics, and international trade.

Southern California Well Service provides well drilling for commercial, industrial, and agricultural applications throughout San Ysidro and the greater border region. The area's commercial activity and remaining agricultural operations create ongoing demand for private water supplies.

San Ysidro Service Area

San Ysidro Geology

The San Ysidro area features the Otay Formation and related sediments:

Geological Layers

Formation Depth Characteristics
Alluvium 0-50 ft Valley fill, near Tijuana River
Otay Formation 50-300 ft Sandstone/conglomerate, productive

Water Quality Considerations

  • TDS: 500-2,000 mg/L (varies by zone)
  • Hardness: Moderate to hard
  • Tijuana River influence: Avoid alluvium near river
  • Industrial areas: Testing essential

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep are wells in San Ysidro?

Wells in San Ysidro typically range from 200-500 feet deep depending on location and target aquifer.

Is the water quality good?

Water quality varies. Most wells produce water suitable for irrigation and industrial use. Potable applications may require treatment. Avoid shallow alluvium near the Tijuana River.

Do you serve Otay Mesa?

Yes, we serve the entire border region including Otay Mesa, San Ysidro, Nestor, and Imperial Beach areas.

What permits are required?

San Diego County DEH well permit required. We handle the permitting process.
