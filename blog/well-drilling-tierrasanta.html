Well Drilling in Tierrasanta

Tierrasanta — "The Island in the Hills" — is an upscale planned community in eastern San Diego, surrounded by Mission Trails Regional Park and military land. Known for its family-friendly neighborhoods and natural setting, Tierrasanta properties often feature larger lots that benefit from private wells.

Southern California Well Service provides well drilling throughout Tierrasanta. While the community is served by the City of San Diego water system, many properties drill irrigation wells to reduce water costs and gain independence from drought restrictions.

Tierrasanta Character

  • Larger lots — Many properties 0.25-0.5+ acres
  • Natural setting — Adjacent to Mission Trails Park
  • Mature landscaping — Established gardens and grounds
  • HOA communities — Common area irrigation needs

Well Applications

Tierrasanta property owners drill wells for various purposes:

Residential Irrigation

  • Landscape watering — Lawns, gardens, trees
  • Drought protection — Unrestricted irrigation
  • Cost savings — Reduce water bills
  • Pool/spa support — Fill and makeup water

HOA Common Areas

  • Entry landscaping — Community aesthetics
  • Parks and greenbelts — Shared spaces
  • Athletic facilities — Playing fields

Mission Trails Area

Properties near Mission Trails Regional Park have unique considerations:

Natural Setting Benefits

  • Larger lots — More well location options
  • Native landscaping — Lower water needs possible

Environmental Considerations

  • Sensitive habitat setbacks may apply
  • Permit review may include environmental check
  • Generally not a barrier to drilling

Fire Protection

  • Independent water supply for fire suppression
  • Backup irrigation during emergencies
  • Landscape maintenance during restrictions

Well Depths in Tierrasanta

Well depths vary across Tierrasanta based on elevation and geology:

Typical Depth Ranges

Location Typical Depth
Lower areas 200-300 ft
Central Tierrasanta 250-350 ft
Hillside properties 300-400+ ft

Yield Expectations

  • Lower producers: 5-15 GPM
  • Average: 15-30 GPM
  • Good producers: 30-50+ GPM

Local Geology

Tierrasanta features typical San Diego mesa geology:

  • Surface: Linda Vista Formation (red-brown sand)
  • Below: Friars Formation (sandstone)
  • Deeper: Stadium Conglomerate, fractured bedrock
