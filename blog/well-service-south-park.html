About South Park

South Park is named for its location south of Balboa Park. The neighborhood was developed primarily in the 1910s-1930s and has become one of San Diego's most desirable urban villages. The commercial district along 30th Street and Fern Street features local restaurants, cafes, and boutiques that give the neighborhood its distinctive character.

Properties from the original development era may have wells that predate municipal water service.
