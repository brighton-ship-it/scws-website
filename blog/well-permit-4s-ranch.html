San Diego County Well Permit Requirements

In San Diego County, well permits are required for:

  • Drilling a new domestic or agricultural well
  • Deepening an existing well
  • Repairing or reconstructing well casing
  • Decommissioning (destroying) an unused well
  • Converting a well to a different use

The Permit Process

  1. Site Evaluation: We assess your property for optimal well location
  2. Application: Submit permit application with site plans
  3. Review: County reviews for setback requirements and regulations
  4. Approval: Permit issued (typically 2-4 weeks)
  5. Construction: Well work proceeds per permit specifications
  6. Inspection: County may inspect completed work
  7. Completion Report: We file required documentation

Setback Requirements

Wells must be located minimum distances from:

  • Septic tanks: 50-100 feet
  • Leach fields: 100-150 feet
  • Property lines: 5-10 feet
  • Buildings: 5 feet minimum
  • Other wells: varies

Exact requirements depend on San Diego County regulations and local conditions.

Well Permit Process in 4s Ranch

Before drilling or modifying a well in 4s Ranch, you need a permit from San Diego County. The permitting process ensures wells are constructed to California standards, protecting both your water supply and neighboring wells.

What Requires a Permit in San Diego County

  • New well drilling — residential, agricultural, or commercial
  • Well deepening — extending an existing well to reach more water
  • Well destruction/abandonment — sealing an unused well to prevent contamination
  • Pump testing — extended yield tests on new or existing wells
  • Well repair involving casing modifications

Typical Permit Timeline for 4s Ranch

San Diego County well permits typically take 2-4 weeks for standard residential wells. Environmental review or proximity to protected waterways can extend this. We handle all permitting as part of our drilling contracts — you don't have to navigate county offices yourself.

Permit Costs

Permit fees in San Diego County range from $500-$1,500 depending on well type and location. Additional fees may apply for environmental review, health department clearance, or setback variance requests. These are included in our drilling quotes.

Setback Requirements

California well standards require minimum distances from potential contamination sources. In 4s Ranch, standard setbacks include:

  • 50 feet from septic tanks and leach fields
  • 100 feet from sewage disposal areas
  • 50 feet from property lines (varies by county)
  • Varies from streams, drainage, and flood zones

If your 4s Ranch property can't meet standard setbacks, we can help you apply for a variance — we've successfully obtained variances throughout San Diego County.
