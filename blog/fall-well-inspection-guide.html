Prepare your well system for winter with this fall inspection checklist. Assess summer wear, prepare for rainy season, and ensure reliable water supply through cooler months.

Assessing Summer Wear

Summer's heavy use takes a toll on well systems. Check your pump's runtime—if it's running longer to maintain pressure, efficiency has dropped. Listen for new sounds that might indicate bearing wear. Review your summer electricity bills; significant increases suggest pump strain. Document any performance changes for comparison with spring readings.

Preparing for Wet Weather

San Diego's rainy season brings flood risks and contamination concerns. Inspect your wellhead's seal and ensure the sanitary well cap is secure. Check that surface drainage directs water away from your well. If your well is in a low area, consider installing a protective barrier or regrading to prevent flooding.

Freeze Protection for Mountain Properties

Properties above 3,000 feet need freeze protection. Insulate exposed pipes, pressure tanks, and any above-ground components. Verify heat tape is functional if installed. Consider installing a pump house or insulated enclosure for exposed equipment. Drain and winterize any irrigation lines that won't be used.

Water Quality Check

Fall testing catches any contamination from summer's higher water table use. Test for bacteria especially if you experienced any flooding or unusual rainfall. This is also a good time to service water treatment equipment before winter, when access may be more difficult. Clean or replace filters, check softener salt levels, and inspect UV systems.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why inspect my well in fall?

Fall inspection catches wear from heavy summer use before winter. It's also the time to prepare for potential flooding, ensure proper drainage, and verify your system can handle reduced maintenance access during wet weather.

Should I worry about freezing in San Diego County?

Mountain areas like Julian, Palomar, and Laguna Mountains can experience freezing. Insulate exposed pipes and pressure tanks in these zones. Coastal and valley areas rarely freeze but benefit from fall preparation nonetheless.

How do I prepare my wellhead for rainy season?

Ensure the well cap is sealed tight, the concrete pad is intact without cracks, and ground slopes away from the wellhead. Clear any debris that could trap water. Consider a well cap with a screened vent if yours is solid.
