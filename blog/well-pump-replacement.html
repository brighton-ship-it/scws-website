Well Pump Replacement Guide

Need to replace your well pump? Complete pump replacement typically costs $1,200-$4,500 depending on well depth and pump size.

Replacement Cost Factors

  • Well depth: Deeper wells cost more to service
  • Pump horsepower: Larger pumps cost more
  • Pump brand: Grundfos, Franklin, etc.
  • Additional repairs: Wiring, drop pipe, etc.

The Replacement Process

  1. Diagnose current pump failure
  2. Pull existing pump from well
  3. Inspect drop pipe and wiring
  4. Install new pump
  5. Test system operation
