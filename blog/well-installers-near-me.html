How to Find Well Installers Near You

Follow these steps to find qualified local well installers:

Step 1: Search and Research

  • Search "well installers near me" plus your city name
  • Check Google Maps for well drilling contractors in your area
  • Ask neighbors with wells who they used
  • Check local Facebook groups for recommendations
  • Contact your county health department for licensed contractor lists

Step 2: Verify Licensing

Every well installer in California must have a C-57 Water Well Contractor license:

  • Visit cslb.ca.gov
  • Search by contractor name or license number
  • Verify active status and C-57 classification
  • Check for any complaints or disciplinary actions

⚠️ Warning: Never hire unlicensed well installers. It's illegal in California and puts your investment at risk. Unlicensed work may fail inspection, void warranties, and create liability for groundwater contamination.

Step 3: Check Insurance

Verify well installers carry proper coverage:

  • General liability insurance — minimum $1 million
  • Workers' compensation — covering all employees
  • Equipment insurance — for the drilling rig

Step 4: Review Reputation

  • Read Google and Yelp reviews carefully
  • Look for reviews mentioning your area
  • Check BBB accreditation and complaints
  • Ask for references from recent local installations

Step 5: Get Multiple Estimates

Contact at least three well installers for written estimates including:

  • Estimated depth range and cost per foot
  • Casing specifications and materials
  • Pump brand and specifications
  • Pressure tank size and type
  • Permit costs and handling
  • Timeline and warranty terms
  • Dry hole policy

Drilling and Construction
