About 4S Ranch

4S Ranch takes its name from the four "S" cattle brands of historic ranches in the area. Today it's a thriving community known for excellent schools (including Design39Campus and 4S Ranch Elementary), beautiful parks, and family-friendly neighborhoods. The community is bordered by the Los Peñasquitos Canyon Preserve to the south and Rancho Bernardo to the north.

While newer developments primarily use city water, older ranches and large-lot properties in the surrounding unincorporated areas often rely on wells for irrigation or domestic use.
