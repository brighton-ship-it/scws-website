Know the Warning Signs

Identifying early signs of trouble can prevent emergencies. Watch for:

  • Irregular water pressure in your system.
  • Pump making unexpected noises.
  • Intermittent spurts of water delivery.
  • Strange smells or tastes in drinking water.
