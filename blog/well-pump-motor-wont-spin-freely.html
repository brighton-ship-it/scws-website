Well Pump Motor Won't Spin Freely

A well pump motor that refuses to spin can be the result of various mechanical issues such as sand clogging, rust, or failed components like bearings.

Reasons and Resolutions

The buildup of sand or rust can cause significant wear on pump components, eventually leading to obstruction. Regular inspection and cleaning are recommended to combat this issue.
