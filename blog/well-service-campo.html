Understanding Campo's Water Situation

Campo sits at approximately 2,600 feet elevation in eastern San Diego County, just a few miles from the Mexican border. This remote community has no municipal water system—every home, ranch, and business relies on groundwater wells. Understanding the local hydrogeology is essential for successful well drilling and maintenance.

Campo's Geology and Groundwater

The Campo area is characterized by:

  • Fractured granite bedrock: Water is found in cracks and fissures rather than porous aquifers
  • Decomposed granite surface: Upper layers may hold some water but often need deeper drilling
  • Variable water tables: Depths can vary significantly even between neighboring properties
  • Seasonal fluctuations: Water levels may drop during extended drought periods
  • Hard water: High mineral content is common due to granite geology

These geological conditions require specific drilling techniques and equipment—experience in the Campo area makes a significant difference in success rates.

Well Pump Services for Campo Properties

Well pumps in Campo face challenging conditions—hard water, deep installations, and temperature extremes can all affect pump lifespan. We provide complete pump services to keep your water flowing.

Pump Repair Services

When your well pump fails, you need fast, reliable service. Common pump problems we diagnose and repair in Campo include:

  • No water production: Failed motor, broken shaft, or electrical issues
  • Low pressure: Worn impellers, check valve problems, or dropping water levels
  • Short cycling: Pressure tank issues or switch problems
  • Air in lines: Failing pump seals or dropping water table
  • High electric bills: Pump running continuously due to leaks or inefficiency
  • Unusual noises: Bearing failure or debris in pump

Water Quality in Campo

Campo's granite geology affects water quality in several ways. Understanding these issues helps you maintain safe, pleasant water for your home.

Common Water Quality Issues

Hard Water

High calcium and magnesium from granite. Causes scale buildup in pipes and appliances.

Iron & Manganese

Can cause staining and metallic taste. Common in fractured rock aquifers.

Low pH (Acidic)

Some wells produce slightly acidic water that can corrode pipes.

Bacteria Risk

Surface contamination possible if well seal is compromised.

Water Testing Services

We recommend comprehensive water testing for all Campo wells, including:

  • Bacterial testing: Total coliform and E. coli
  • Mineral analysis: Hardness, iron, manganese, pH
  • Nitrate testing: Important for drinking water safety
  • Complete panel: Full analysis for new wells or real estate transactions

Based on test results, we can recommend appropriate water treatment solutions for your specific water chemistry.

Well Rehabilitation

If your Campo well has declining production, rehabilitation may restore flow without drilling a new well. Options include:

  • Chemical treatment: Dissolves mineral scale and bacterial buildup
  • Mechanical cleaning: Removes sediment and debris from well screen
  • Hydrofracturing: Opens new fractures in rock formations
  • Well deepening: Extends existing well to reach more water
