Frequently Asked Questions

What is hydrofracturing and how does it work?

Hydrofracturing (hydrofracking) uses high-pressure water to open existing fractures in bedrock, increasing water flow into your well. It's effective for wells in granite, sandstone, and other hard rock formations common in San Diego County.

How much does hydrofracturing cost?

Hydrofracturing typically costs $3,000-$8,000 depending on well depth and conditions. Many homeowners see 2-10x improvement in water yield, making it more cost-effective than drilling a new well.

Is hydrofracturing guaranteed to work?

While success rates are high (70-80%), results vary based on geology. We perform a thorough assessment first and only recommend hydrofracturing when conditions are favorable. If it doesn't improve yield significantly, drilling deeper or a new well may be needed.
