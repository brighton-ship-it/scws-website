Equestrian Property Wells

Canyon Lake is known for its equestrian community. Horse properties have unique water demands:

Water Requirements

  • Horses need 10-15 gallons/day — More in summer heat
  • Pasture irrigation — Significant water for turnout areas
  • Wash racks and arenas — Additional demand
  • Multiple watering stations — Barn, paddocks, pastures

What We Recommend

  • Higher-capacity pumps (10-20+ GPM)
  • Larger pressure tanks
  • Backup power considerations
  • Multiple water points

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does drilling cost in Canyon Lake?

Residential wells typically cost $18,000-$45,000. Equestrian properties with high-capacity systems can run $30,000-$60,000+.

How deep are Canyon Lake wells?

Most wells range from 200-400+ feet. Properties near the lake often have better water availability.

Can I drill in a gated community?

Yes, we work with HOAs regularly. We coordinate access, meet community requirements, and respect property and neighbor concerns.

What size well do I need for horses?

Equestrian properties typically need 10-20+ GPM capacity, depending on the number of horses and irrigation needs.
