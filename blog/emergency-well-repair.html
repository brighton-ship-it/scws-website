What to Do While Waiting for Emergency Well Repair

You've called for help—now what? Here's how to manage the situation while waiting for your technician to arrive:

⚠️ Immediate Safety Steps

  • If you smell burning or see smoke: Turn off the well pump breaker immediately. This is a fire hazard.
  • If the breaker keeps tripping: Leave it OFF. Repeated resets can cause electrical fires.
  • Stop trying to run water: Repeated attempts with a failing pump can cause additional damage.

Getting Water in the Meantime

  • Neighbors with city water: Fill buckets and containers for flushing toilets and basic washing.
  • Bottled water: Grocery stores and convenience stores for drinking and cooking. Budget $1-2 per gallon.
  • Water delivery services: Many deliver same-day, including 5-gallon dispensers.
  • Gym/fitness center: If you have a membership, use their shower facilities.

For Livestock Emergencies

  • Water trucking services: Agricultural water haulers can deliver quickly.
  • Local fire department: Many rural fire departments provide emergency water for livestock—call the non-emergency number.
  • Neighboring farms: Fellow farmers understand the urgency and often have equipment to help.

Information to Gather for the Technician

Help us diagnose faster by noting:

  • When did the problem start?
  • Did it happen suddenly or gradually?
  • Any unusual sounds before the failure (grinding, clicking, humming)?
  • Any recent power outages or electrical storms?
  • Has the pump been short-cycling recently?
  • How old is your current pump (if known)?
