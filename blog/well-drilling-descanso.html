Descanso's Groundwater

Descanso benefits from its valley setting along the Sweetwater River drainage, creating generally favorable groundwater conditions compared to surrounding mountain areas.

Valley Floor

Properties along the valley floor near the Sweetwater River often find water at relatively shallow depths (150-250 feet) due to alluvial deposits and favorable recharge conditions.

Decomposed Granite

Much of Descanso sits on decomposed granite—weathered bedrock that often stores groundwater in its fractures and weathered zones. This geology typically produces reliable wells.

Hillside Properties

Properties on the surrounding hills may require deeper drilling (300-400+ feet) to reach productive fracture zones in the bedrock.

Location Typical Depth Geology Production

Well Services for Descanso

Residential Well Drilling

Mountain homes need reliable water systems designed for rural conditions. We design systems with adequate storage, freeze protection, and backup capabilities appropriate for Descanso's mountain environment.

Ranch and Agricultural Wells

Descanso's ranches and small farms need water for livestock, gardens, and orchting. We design systems capable of meeting agricultural demands while remaining efficient for residential use.

Orchard and Vineyard Wells

The Descanso area includes small vineyards and apple orchards. We can design irrigation wells sized for these specialized agricultural applications.

Well Rehabilitation

Existing wells showing declining production may benefit from rehabilitation:

  • Hydrofracturing: Opening fractures in bedrock
  • Well Cleaning: Removing deposits and sediment
  • Pump Upgrades: Modern, efficient equipment
  • Deepening: Accessing additional water zones

Mountain Considerations

Freeze Protection

Descanso winters can bring temperatures below freezing. All installations include appropriate freeze protection—insulated wellheads, heat tape where needed, and proper component placement.

Fire Safety

Mountain fire risk makes water storage valuable. We can integrate storage tanks with your well system for firefighting reserves while maintaining normal household supply.

Power Reliability

Storm-related power outages occur in mountain areas. We can design systems with backup options to maintain water during outages.

FAQs

Is Descanso a good area for wells?

Generally yes. Descanso's valley setting and decomposed granite geology typically produce reliable wells. Valley floor properties often find water at moderate depths.

What about the drought?

Properly constructed, adequately deep wells have remained productive through recent droughts. We design wells to reach reliable aquifer zones.

How long does drilling take?

Typically 2-4 days depending on depth. Total project time including permits and completion is usually 2-3 weeks.

Can you work with my existing well?

Yes. We evaluate existing wells and can often improve production through rehabilitation, pump upgrades, or deepening.
