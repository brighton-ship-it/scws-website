Well Pump Troubleshooting Guide

Before calling for service, try these basic troubleshooting steps. Many well problems have simple causes you can check yourself.

No Water at All

  1. Check electrical panel for tripped breaker
  2. Check pressure switch (near tank) for power
  3. Listen for pump running
  4. Check pressure gauge reading

Low Water Pressure

  • Check pressure tank air charge
  • Inspect for leaks in plumbing
  • Check pressure switch settings
  • Look for clogged filters

Pump Runs But No Water

  • Check for major leaks
  • Foot valve may be stuck (jet pumps)
  • Pump may need priming
  • Well water level may be low

When to Call a Pro

If basic troubleshooting doesn't solve the problem, or if you smell burning or see sparks, call immediately.
