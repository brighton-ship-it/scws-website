Common Pump Problems in San Bernardino

The geological conditions in San Bernardino — the Peninsular Ranges batholith, primarily granitic and metamorphic rock — create specific challenges for well pumps. While moderate well depths are easier on pumps, local water chemistry and sediment conditions can still cause premature wear.

The most common pump repair calls we get from San Bernardino include: pumps running but producing low flow (often a failing impeller or dropped water level), circuit breakers tripping when the pump starts (bad capacitor or motor windings), and pressure tank waterlogging (failed bladder). We carry common parts on our trucks for same-day repair in most cases.
