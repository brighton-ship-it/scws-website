Well Drilling in Fallbrook

Fallbrook — the "Avocado Capital of the World" — is San Diego County's premier agricultural community. With thousands of acres of avocado and citrus groves, plus rural estates and equestrian properties, Fallbrook is prime well drilling country.

Southern California Well Service provides comprehensive well drilling services throughout Fallbrook and surrounding areas including De Luz, Rainbow, and Bonsall. We understand the agricultural water demands of this unique community.

Why Fallbrook Needs Wells

  • Agricultural economy — Avocados require reliable water
  • Limited municipal supply — Many properties off grid
  • Cost control — Well water far cheaper than alternatives
  • Drought protection — Independence from allocations
  • Rural lifestyle — Self-sufficient water supply

Well Applications

Fallbrook wells serve the community's diverse needs:

Agricultural

  • Avocado orchards — Fallbrook's signature industry
  • Citrus groves — Oranges, lemons, grapefruit
