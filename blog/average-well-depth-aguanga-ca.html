Geology and Groundwater in Aguanga

Aguanga sits in an area with deep bedrock, typically requiring wells to penetrate granite or metamorphic formations. The average depth of 411 feet reflects the need to drill through hard rock layers to reach productive aquifers. These deeper wells often require air rotary or DTH (down-the-hole) hammer drilling methods. Well depths vary significantly—from 20 to 1710 feet—reflecting diverse geological conditions across the area.

The average static water level (depth to water when the pump is off) in Aguanga wells is approximately 139 feet below ground surface.
