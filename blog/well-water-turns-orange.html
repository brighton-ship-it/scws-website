Why Your Well Water Is Turning Orange

Orange well water looks alarming, but it's usually a straightforward iron problem. When dissolved iron in your water contacts air, it oxidizes—turning from invisible to that rusty orange color. The same chemistry that rusts metal is coloring your water. Let's explore the causes and solutions.

Common Causes

  • High Iron Content: Iron is one of the most common minerals in groundwater. When it oxidizes, it turns orange.
  • Iron Bacteria: These bacteria feed on iron and create orange/rust-colored slime and deposits.
  • Corroding Casing or Pipes: Rusting steel well components shed iron particles into your water.
  • Sediment Disturbance: Iron-rich sediment at the well bottom can color water when disturbed.
  • Water Table Changes: Your well may now be drawing from a higher-iron zone of the aquifer.

Warning Signs to Watch For

  • Orange, red, or rust-colored water
  • Staining on fixtures and laundry
  • Metallic taste
  • Color worse after water sits in pipes
  • Orange slime in toilet tanks
  • Clogged filters or aerators

How to Fix This Problem

1. Test Your Water

Determine iron concentration. Low levels (under 3 ppm) can be handled by softeners; higher levels need dedicated treatment.

2. Install Iron Filter

Air injection, greensand, or birm filters oxidize and remove iron effectively.

3. Consider Chlorination

For iron bacteria, shock chlorination kills the bacteria. Ongoing chlorination may be needed.

4. Water Softener

For modest iron levels, a properly sized water softener can handle iron removal.

5. Address the Source

If rust is from corroding pipes or casing, filtration treats the symptom but replacement solves the problem.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is orange well water safe to drink?

Iron itself isn't harmful at typical levels. However, the discoloration may indicate other issues worth testing for.

Why is my well water suddenly orange?

Sudden orange water usually indicates sediment disturbance, new water source contact, or advancing corrosion in well components.

What removes orange from well water?

Iron filtration systems are most effective. Options include oxidizing filters, water softeners (for low iron), and whole-house filtration.

Will a water filter fix orange water?

A standard carbon filter won't remove dissolved iron. You need oxidizing filtration that converts dissolved iron to particles for removal.
