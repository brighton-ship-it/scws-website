Professional Well Drilling Services in Redlands, California

Redlands, the "Jewel of the Inland Empire," carries a rich heritage rooted in citrus cultivation and groundwater. From the 1880s when navel oranges first made this valley famous, local water wells have sustained the groves, estates, and homes that give Redlands its distinctive character. Today, Southern California Well Service (SCWS) continues that tradition, providing professional well drilling and service to Redlands property owners who value water independence and quality.

Whether you own a historic citrus property, a foothill estate with mountain views, or rural acreage beyond municipal water service, SCWS brings over three decades of local experience to your well project. Our knowledge of Redlands' unique geology—from the productive alluvial fans below the San Bernardino Mountains to the fractured bedrock of the higher elevations—ensures successful well development for every application.

Redlands' Exceptional Groundwater Heritage

Redlands owes its agricultural success to the exceptional groundwater resources that lie beneath the valley. The San Bernardino Mountains collect precipitation that percolates through the foothills, recharging aquifers that have supplied the region for over a century. This natural recharge system creates favorable conditions for well development throughout much of the Redlands area.

  • Alluvial Fan Deposits: The foothills contain deep deposits of sand, gravel, and sediments washed down from the mountains—excellent aquifer materials that often yield productive wells.
  • Mountain Front Recharge: Continuous recharge from the San Bernardino Mountains helps maintain groundwater levels and quality.
  • Historic Production: Generations of successful wells demonstrate the area's reliable groundwater potential.
  • Quality Water: Many Redlands wells produce excellent quality water with minimal treatment requirements.

Redlands' historic citrus estates represent some of California's most beautiful properties, with mature groves, Victorian homes, and established landscapes that speak to the region's agricultural heritage. Many of these properties have well water systems dating back decades, requiring professional evaluation and potential updating.

Historic Property Well Services

Foothill and Mountain View Properties

Redlands' foothill areas offer spectacular views and the natural beauty of the San Bernardino Mountain backdrop. These elevated properties often encounter different geological conditions than the valley floor, requiring specialized drilling approaches.

Higher elevation properties may encounter decomposed granite, fractured bedrock, or transitions between alluvial materials and underlying rock. SCWS has extensive experience drilling in these conditions, understanding how to successfully locate and develop wells where water may be found in fracture systems rather than traditional aquifer materials.

Our foothill drilling experience includes properties throughout the Live Oak Canyon area, the historic groves along Sunset Drive, and the scenic neighborhoods approaching Oak Glen. We understand the varied conditions these areas present and match our drilling approach accordingly.

Our Drilling Process

SCWS approaches every Redlands well project systematically, maximizing success while keeping you informed throughout the process.

Site Evaluation

We begin with thorough property evaluation, reviewing geological data, neighboring well records, and conducting on-site assessment. This information guides drilling location selection and helps establish realistic expectations for depth, production, and water quality.

Professional Drilling

Our experienced crews use air rotary drilling for most Redlands applications, efficiently penetrating varied formations while monitoring water production in real-time. Our drillers make informed decisions as conditions evolve, optimizing well construction for your specific property.

Well Completion

After drilling, we properly develop the well to maximize production, collect samples for water quality testing, and install pumping equipment sized for your needs. Complete documentation—well logs, test results, equipment specifications—becomes part of your property records.

Well Rehabilitation Services

Many Redlands properties have wells that may be decades old, requiring professional attention to maintain or restore production. Our rehabilitation services can often bring older wells back to excellent performance.

  • Video Inspection: Downhole camera surveys to assess casing condition and identify problems.
  • Well Cleaning: Removing mineral deposits, biological growth, and sediment restricting water flow.
  • Hydrofracturing: High-pressure treatment to open new fractures in bedrock wells.
  • Well Deepening: Extending existing wells to reach additional water-bearing zones.
  • System Upgrades: Modern pumps and controls for improved efficiency and reliability.

Water Quality

Redlands' groundwater is generally high quality, though some properties may benefit from treatment for hardness or other minerals. SCWS provides water testing services and can recommend appropriate treatment solutions based on actual test results.

We believe in honest recommendations—if your water tests well, we'll tell you treatment isn't necessary. When treatment would benefit you, we explain options clearly and help you choose appropriate solutions.
