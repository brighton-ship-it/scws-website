About Cardiff-by-the-Sea

Cardiff-by-the-Sea was developed in 1911 by J. Frank Cullen, who named all the streets after British cities in honor of his wife, who was from Wales. The community maintains its unique identity with the famous "Cardiff Kook" statue (officially titled "Magic Carpet Ride"), the San Elijo Lagoon Ecological Reserve, and some of the best surf breaks in San Diego County.

Properties near the San Elijo Lagoon and in the hillside areas east of Highway 101 are most likely to have or benefit from private wells, particularly for landscape irrigation.

Well Maintenance in Cardiff By The Sea

Regular well service in Cardiff By The Sea helps prevent costly emergency repairs. With local wells drilled through the Peninsular Ranges batholith, primarily granitic and metamorphic rock, we recommend annual checkups that include water level measurement, pump performance testing, pressure tank inspection, and water quality screening.

Cardiff By The Sea well owners should watch for signs of trouble: reduced water pressure, pump cycling frequently, discolored water, or unusual noises from the pressure tank. These often indicate issues that are much cheaper to fix early than after a complete system failure.
