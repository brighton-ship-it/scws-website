Well Not Producing Enough Water? Here's Why

When your well's water production drops, several factors could be responsible. Understanding the cause helps determine the right solution.

Common Causes of Low Well Yield

  • Dropping water table: Drought or increased local pumping
  • Clogged well screen: Mineral or bacterial buildup
  • Failing pump: Worn impellers reduce output
  • Sediment in well: Sand or debris blocking flow
  • Aquifer depletion: Long-term decline in groundwater

Solutions

  • Hydrofracturing: Opens new fractures in rock
  • Well rehabilitation: Cleaning and chemical treatment
  • Deeper pump setting: Access lower water levels
  • Well deepening: Drill deeper into aquifer
  • New well: When rehabilitation isn't viable
