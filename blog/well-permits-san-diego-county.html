Well Permits in San Diego County

All new wells in San Diego County require permits from the Department of Environmental Health. Here's what you need to know.

Permit Requirements

  • Well construction permit application
  • Site plan showing proposed well location
  • Setback compliance (from septic, property lines)
  • Licensed C-57 contractor for drilling

Typical Fees

  • New well permit: $500-$1,000
  • Inspection fees included
  • Destruction permit (for old wells): $300-$500

We Handle Permits

SCWS manages the entire permitting process. We prepare applications, coordinate inspections, and ensure your well meets all county requirements.
