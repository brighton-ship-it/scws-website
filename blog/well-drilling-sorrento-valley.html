Frequently Asked Questions

Can I drill a commercial well in Sorrento Valley?

Yes, Sorrento Valley's commercial zoning and larger properties are excellent for commercial well drilling.

How deep are wells in Sorrento Valley?

Wells typically range from 150-350 feet deep. The valley floor often produces at shallower depths.

Is the water quality suitable for biotech use?

Raw well water is suitable for irrigation, cooling, and as pre-feed for treatment systems. Lab-grade water requires DI/RO treatment.

Can you integrate with existing systems?

Yes, we routinely integrate wells with existing irrigation controllers, treatment systems, and building water systems.
