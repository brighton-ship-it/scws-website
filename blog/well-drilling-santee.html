Why Consider a Well in Santee?

Water Cost Savings

Padre Dam Municipal Water District rates include tiered pricing that makes high-volume irrigation expensive. Large properties with substantial landscape water needs can often achieve excellent returns on irrigation well investments.

Drought Protection

During drought restrictions, landscape allocations may be limited. Well owners maintain control over irrigation schedules, protecting valuable mature landscaping.

Horse Property Support

Santee's eastern areas include horse properties needing reliable water for pastures, arenas, and livestock. Wells provide water independence for these demands.

Santee's Groundwater

Santee's geology features primarily decomposed granite, which generally provides favorable conditions for wells.

Eastern Areas

The eastern portions of Santee, near Lakeside and the foothills, often have the most favorable conditions for wells—larger lots combined with accessible groundwater in decomposed granite.

San Diego River Corridor

Properties near the San Diego River corridor may access alluvial deposits that can provide good production at moderate depths.

FAQs

Is my Santee property a good candidate?

It depends on lot size, water usage, and location. We provide free site evaluations to assess feasibility and potential ROI.

Do I need permits?

Yes, San Diego County requires well permits. We handle permitting as part of our service.

Can I use well water for household use?

Yes, though many Santee homeowners prefer using well water for irrigation while keeping municipal water for household use.
