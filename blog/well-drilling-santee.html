Well Opportunities in Santee

Santee's development pattern means wells serve specific niches rather than being universally applicable. Understanding when wells make sense helps property owners make informed decisions.

  • Large Lot Irrigation: Properties with substantial landscaping save significantly over municipal rates.
  • Commercial Applications: Businesses with high water demands benefit from independent supply.
  • Development Projects: New construction may require wells where infrastructure is limited.
  • Agricultural Properties: Remaining agricultural parcels need economical water sources.
  • Backup Systems: Critical facilities benefit from redundant water supply.
  • Outlying Areas: Properties on city edges may lack practical municipal connections.

San Diego River Valley Aquifer

Santee's location along the San Diego River means portions of the city sit above significant alluvial aquifers. These unconsolidated deposits of sand and gravel can yield substantial water where they occur.

  • Alluvial Zones: Areas near the river channel may access productive unconsolidated aquifers.
  • Crystalline Bedrock: Hillside properties encounter fractured rock typical of San Diego County.
  • Variable Conditions: Geology changes significantly across relatively short distances.
  • Recharge Considerations: River proximity may enhance groundwater availability in some areas.

Well depths and production vary considerably based on location. Properties in valley areas may find excellent water at moderate depths, while hillside sites typically require deeper drilling.

Well Pump Systems

SCWS provides comprehensive pump services for all Santee well applications. Proper pump selection and installation is crucial for system performance and longevity.

  • Submersible Pumps: Standard choice for most deep well applications.
  • Line Shaft Turbines: High-capacity pumps for commercial applications.
  • Booster Pumps: Adding pressure for demanding situations.

Water Quality Testing and Treatment

Santee groundwater quality varies by location and aquifer source. Comprehensive testing reveals any issues requiring treatment.

  • Basic Testing: Essential parameters for all wells.
