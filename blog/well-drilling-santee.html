Based on California DWR well completion reports. Santee's average well depth is 382 feet shallower than the San Diego County average of 450 feet.

With 238 wells on record, Santee has a moderate well infrastructure. The wide depth range of 5 to 1315 feet reflects the varied terrain and geology across Santee's landscape. Shallower wells typically tap into alluvial aquifers near drainages, while deeper wells penetrate the Peninsular Ranges batholith, primarily granitic and metamorphic rock to reach more reliable water sources.

At an average depth of 68 feet, drilling in Santee typically costs between $3,060 and $5,100 depending on formation hardness and casing requirements. See detailed well depth data for Santee →

Drilling Conditions in Santee

Well drilling in Santee typically encounters the Peninsular Ranges batholith, primarily granitic and metamorphic rock. Most wells can be completed in 1-3 days under normal conditions. The relatively moderate depths keep drilling costs reasonable, though rocky formations can slow progress.

San Diego County requires a well permit from the Department of Environmental Health (DEH) before drilling can begin. The permit process typically takes 2-4 weeks and costs $800-$1,500 depending on the parcel. We handle the entire permit process for Santee properties.
