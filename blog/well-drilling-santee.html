Well Drilling in Santee

Santee — the "City of Progress" — offers a blend of suburban amenities and open space. While Padre Dam Municipal Water District serves most of the city, many Santee properties benefit from private irrigation wells to reduce water costs.

Southern California Well Service provides well drilling throughout Santee and surrounding areas. We help homeowners achieve water independence for landscaping, pools, and other non-potable uses.

Why Santee Properties Drill Wells

  • Cost savings — Reduce Padre Dam water bills
  • Large landscaping — Significant irrigation needs
  • Pool maintenance — Fill and top-up water
  • Drought protection — Maintain landscapes
  • Rural properties — Eastern Santee areas

Well Applications

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep are wells in Santee?

Wells typically range from 150-450 feet deep. Properties near the San Diego River often find water shallower.

Is Santee good for wells?

Yes, especially near the San Diego River corridor. Most properties can achieve irrigation wells.

Can I use a well for drinking water?

Yes, with proper testing and treatment. Many Santee residents use wells for all domestic needs.

How much will I save on water bills?

Typically $2,000-$6,000+ per year depending on usage. We can calculate projected savings for your property.
