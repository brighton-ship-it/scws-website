Regulatory Requirements

  • Public supply wells: Subject to EPA Safe Drinking Water Act
  • Testing frequency: Monthly to quarterly for regulated systems
  • Treatment requirements: Based on source water quality
  • Reporting: Annual consumer confidence reports required
  • Permits: More extensive than domestic well permits
