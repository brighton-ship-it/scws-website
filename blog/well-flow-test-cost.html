Flow Test Pricing by Type

Basic flow rate measurement costs $100-$200. Standard 4-hour pump test runs $200-$400. Extended 24-hour yield test ranges from $400-$800. Recovery rate testing is typically included in most tests. Written reports with GPM results are usually included in the price.

Understanding Flow Test Results

Minimum residential need is 3-5 GPM for a basic household. Comfortable supply is 8-12 GPM for an average home. Agricultural and irrigation needs vary by acreage. Static water level versus pumping level matters for assessing well health. Recovery rate indicates aquifer sustainability.

When Flow Testing Is Required

Flow tests are needed for pre-purchase property inspections and FHA/VA loan requirements (many lenders require them). Test before planning home additions or irrigation systems, after drilling a new well, and when investigating reduced water pressure complaints.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a 4-hour and 24-hour test?

A 4-hour test shows immediate production capacity. A 24-hour test reveals sustainable yield—whether the aquifer can keep up with extended pumping. Lenders often require 4-hour tests; serious buyers of rural property may want 24-hour results.

What if my flow test shows low GPM?

Low flow doesn't always mean a bad well. Options include storage tanks to buffer supply, hydrofracturing to improve production, or deepening the well. A contractor can recommend solutions based on your specific situation and water needs.

Can weather affect my flow test results?

Yes. Testing during drought or at the end of dry season shows worst-case capacity. Testing after heavy rains may show artificially high results. Consider seasonal timing when scheduling and interpreting results.
