What Does a Pressure Tank Do?

  • Stores pressurized water — Provides instant water at the tap
  • Reduces pump cycling — Extends pump life
  • Maintains steady pressure — Consistent flow throughout your home
  • Prevents water hammer — Absorbs pressure spikes

Signs of Tank Problems

  • Pump cycles on/off frequently (short cycling)
  • Pressure fluctuates wildly
  • Tank feels waterlogged (no air cushion)
  • Visible corrosion or leaks

FAQs: Pressure Tanks

How long do pressure tanks last?

Quality bladder tanks typically last 10-15 years. Older galvanized tanks may fail sooner.

What size tank do I need?

Tank size depends on pump GPM and household demand. We size tanks properly to minimize pump cycling.
