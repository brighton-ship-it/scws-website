Types of Well Pumps We Service

SCWS technicians service all pump types found in Murrieta:

  • Submersible pumps: The standard for most residential and agricultural wells.
  • Jet pumps: Above-ground pumps for shallower applications.
  • Constant pressure systems: Modern systems maintaining steady water pressure.
  • Variable speed pumps: Energy-efficient systems that match output to demand.
  • Booster pumps: Secondary pumps for pressure enhancement.
  • Irrigation systems: Dedicated pumps for landscape and pasture watering.

Common Well Pump Problems in Murrieta

Our Murrieta service calls frequently address these issues:

  • Motor failures: Heat and heavy use wear out pump motors.
  • Electrical problems: Control boxes, capacitors, and switches degrade.
  • Scale buildup: Hard water deposits affect pump efficiency.
  • Pressure tank issues: Failed bladders cause short cycling.
  • Impeller wear: Sediment gradually erodes pump components.
  • Check valve failures: Worn valves reduce system efficiency.
  • Low water conditions: Drought can affect some wells.

Signs Your Well Pump Needs Service

Murrieta well owners should watch for:

  • Reduced water pressure at fixtures
  • Pump running constantly or cycling rapidly
  • Air in water lines
  • Unusual equipment noises
  • Higher electricity costs
  • Changes in water quality
  • Irrigation underperformance
  • Complete water loss
