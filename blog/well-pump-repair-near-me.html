Common Well Pump Problems We Fix

  • No water – Pump failure, electrical issues, or low water table
  • Low pressure – Worn pump, pressure tank issues, or pipe leaks
  • Pump cycling frequently – Pressure switch or tank bladder problems
  • High electric bills – Inefficient or failing pump
  • Air in lines – Pump drawing air, drop pipe issues
  • Dirty water – Sand, sediment, or pump wear

What to Do While Waiting for Pump Repair

  1. Check the breaker – Make sure power is on to the pump
  2. Check the pressure gauge – Note the reading (or lack thereof)
  3. Turn off water – Prevent damage if there's a leak
  4. Call us – We'll prioritize based on urgency
