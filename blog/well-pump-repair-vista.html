Common Well Pump Problems in Vista

Our Vista service calls frequently address these issues:

  • Motor burnout: Heavy irrigation use can overheat pump motors.
  • Electrical failures: Control boxes and capacitors wear out.
  • Scale buildup: Hard water deposits accumulate on components.
  • Pressure tank problems: Failed bladders cause short cycling.
  • Impeller wear: Sediment erodes pump internals over time.
  • Check valve failures: Worn valves reduce system efficiency.
  • Pipe corrosion: Metal components deteriorate in hard water.

Signs of Well Pump Problems

Vista well owners should watch for these warning signs:

  • Reduced water pressure at fixtures
  • Pump running continuously or cycling rapidly
  • Air bubbles in water lines
  • Unusual noises from pump equipment
  • Increased electricity costs
  • Dirty, sandy, or discolored water
  • Complete loss of water
  • Irrigation system underperforming
