About Mount Helix

Mount Helix rises to 1,362 feet and is crowned by the famous Mount Helix Cross, a 34-foot cross built in 1929. The area features some of San Diego's most beautiful estate homes with spectacular views of the surrounding valleys, mountains, and ocean. The granitic geology typically requires well depths of 300-500 feet.
