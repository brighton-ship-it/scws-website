How to Find Water Well Companies Near You

Follow these steps to find reputable water well companies in your area:

Step 1: Search and Research

  • Search "water well companies near me" plus your city name
  • Check Google Maps for well service providers in your area
  • Ask neighbors with wells who they use
  • Check community Facebook groups for recommendations
  • Contact your county health department for licensed contractor lists

Step 2: Verify Credentials

Every water well company in California must have proper licensing:

  • C-57 License — Required for well drilling and major pump work
  • Insurance — Liability coverage and workers' compensation
  • Experience — Years in business and local track record

Verify licenses at the California Contractors State License Board.

⚠️ Warning: Never hire an unlicensed water well company. In California, it's illegal to drill wells or perform major pump work without a C-57 license. Unlicensed work may fail inspection, void warranties, and create liability for groundwater contamination.

Step 3: Check Reviews and References

  • Read Google reviews carefully—look for detailed experiences
  • Check Yelp for both positive and negative feedback
  • Look for Better Business Bureau accreditation
  • Ask for references from recent customers in your area
  • Contact references and ask about their experience

Step 4: Get Multiple Estimates

For major work like well drilling or pump replacement, get at least three written estimates including:

  • Detailed scope of work
  • Equipment specifications and brands
  • Timeline for completion
  • Total cost with itemized breakdown
  • Warranty terms
  • Payment terms
