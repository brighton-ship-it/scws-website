Troubleshooting Before You Call

Try these simple checks before calling for service. Sometimes the fix is easier than you think:

Check Your Electrical Panel

  • Look for a tripped breaker — Reset it once
  • If it trips again immediately, don't keep resetting — call us
  • Check for any burned smell or discoloration

Check the Pressure Switch

  • Located on or near the pressure tank (gray box with lever)
  • Listen for clicking when you turn on a faucet
  • If lever is stuck, a gentle tap may restore function temporarily

Check the Pressure Tank

  • Tap on tank — hollow sound = good, solid thud = waterlogged
  • Check air valve pressure (should be 2 PSI below cut-in)
  • Heavy tank with no water running = likely failed bladder

Check for Leaks

  • Inspect around wellhead for wet spots
  • Check hose bibs and irrigation valves
  • Look under all sinks
  • Check toilets — a running toilet can cycle your pump constantly

Call Us Immediately If:

  • Breaker trips repeatedly when reset
  • You smell burning from control box
  • Complete water loss with no obvious cause
  • Pump makes grinding or screeching sounds
