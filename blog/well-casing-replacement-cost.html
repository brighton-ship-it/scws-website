Casing Repair vs. Replacement Costs

Spot repairs and patches run $1,500-$4,000. Casing liner installation costs $3,000-$8,000. Partial casing replacement ranges from $5,000-$15,000. Full casing replacement costs $15,000-$30,000 or more. In some cases, new well drilling may be cheaper than major repairs.

Signs Your Casing Needs Attention

Watch for sand or sediment suddenly appearing in water, bacteria contamination that keeps returning, and surface water intrusion after rain events. Video inspection showing holes or corrosion indicates damage. Declining water production can also signal casing problems.

Casing Liner Installation Process

Camera inspection first identifies damage locations. A smaller diameter PVC or steel liner is inserted into the existing casing. The annular space is sealed with grout or cement. This reduces well diameter slightly but extends the well's life. Liner installation is less disruptive than full replacement.

When to Repair vs. Drill New

Repairs make sense if the well produces adequate water. Consider well age—rehabilitating a 50-year-old well has limits. Get quotes for both repair and new well drilling. New wells may qualify for better components and warranties. Factor in long-term reliability, not just upfront cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does well casing last?

Steel casing typically lasts 25-50 years depending on water chemistry and soil conditions. PVC casing can last 50+ years. Corrosive water or aggressive soils shorten lifespan significantly. Regular inspections catch problems early.

Can I just patch a hole in my casing?

Small holes can sometimes be patched with inflatable packers or localized sleeves, costing $1,500-$4,000. However, one hole often indicates more damage. A camera inspection should evaluate the entire casing before deciding on spot repair vs. full liner.

Will a casing liner reduce my water flow?

Liners reduce the internal diameter, which can slightly reduce maximum flow. For most residential wells, this isn't noticeable. Your contractor should calculate whether your pump and production will work within the smaller diameter.
