Sonoma County Well Overview

Sonoma County, California's premier wine region alongside Napa, has an extensive groundwater infrastructure. The county overlies multiple groundwater basins regulated under SGMA, including the Santa Rosa Plain (High Priority), Sonoma Valley, and Petaluma Valley basins.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many wells are in Sonoma County?

Sonoma County has approximately 24,500 registered wells, including 18,200 domestic and 4,800 agricultural wells.

What groundwater basins are in Sonoma County?

Three SGMA-regulated basins: Santa Rosa Plain (High Priority), Sonoma Valley (Medium), and Petaluma Valley (Medium).

Are groundwater levels declining?

Yes, portions of Santa Rosa Plain and Sonoma Valley show declining groundwater levels. GSAs are implementing sustainability plans.

What is the average well depth?

Domestic wells average 265 feet; coastal areas may require 300-500 feet.

How much does a well cost?

Average cost is $45,500 for a complete system (2026), ranging from $25,000 to $95,000.
