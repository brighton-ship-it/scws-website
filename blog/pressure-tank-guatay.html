Why Your Guatay Pressure Tank Matters

The pressure tank is the heart of your well system — it stores pressurized water so your pump doesn't have to run every time you turn on a faucet. A properly sized and functioning pressure tank in your Guatay home means consistent water pressure, longer pump life, and lower electricity costs.

Signs Your Pressure Tank Needs Attention

  • Pump short cycling: Turns on and off every few seconds (the #1 sign of a waterlogged tank)
  • Fluctuating pressure: Strong pressure that suddenly drops, then recovers
  • Water hammer: Banging pipes when fixtures turn on or off
  • Visible rust or corrosion: On the tank exterior, especially at seams or the base
  • Water from the air valve: Press the Schrader valve — if water comes out, the bladder has failed

Sizing a Pressure Tank for Guatay Homes

Tank size depends on your pump's flow rate and how many fixtures you run simultaneously. For typical Guatay residences:

  • 1-2 bathroom homes: 20-32 gallon tank (adequate for 5-10 GPM pumps)
  • 3-4 bathroom homes: 44-86 gallon tank (handles 10-20 GPM pumps and higher simultaneous demand)
  • Estates/ranch properties: 86-120 gallon tank or multiple tanks (for high-flow systems with irrigation)

Undersized tanks cause short cycling, which destroys pumps. We always size based on your actual pump output and peak demand — not just the number of bathrooms.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my pressure tank is bad?

Signs of a failing pressure tank include: pump cycling on/off rapidly, waterlogged tank (heavy when full), fluctuating water pressure, or visible rust/corrosion. We can diagnose the problem and recommend repair or replacement.

How long do pressure tanks last?

Quality pressure tanks last 10-15 years on average. Bladder tanks may fail sooner if water quality is poor. Regular maintenance and proper air charge can extend tank life.

What size pressure tank do I need?

Tank size depends on your pump's flow rate and household demand. Most homes use 20-50 gallon tanks. Larger tanks reduce pump cycling and extend pump life. We calculate the right size for your system.
