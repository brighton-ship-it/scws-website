Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best water treatment for well water?

The best treatment depends on your water quality. We test for hardness, iron, sulfur, bacteria, and pH to recommend the right system. Most San Diego County wells benefit from a water softener combined with sediment filtration.

How much does a whole-house water treatment system cost?

Whole-house systems range from $1,500 for basic filtration to $8,000+ for comprehensive treatment including softening, iron removal, and UV disinfection. We provide free water testing and quotes.

How often do water treatment systems need maintenance?

Most systems need annual maintenance. Water softeners need salt refills monthly. Filter cartridges typically last 6-12 months. We offer maintenance plans to keep your system running efficiently.
