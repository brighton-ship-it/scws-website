Mountain Well Challenges in Crestline

Granite Bedrock

The San Bernardino Mountains consist primarily of hard granite bedrock. Drilling through granite requires specialized air rotary equipment and experienced operators who understand fractured rock aquifers. Your well's production depends on intersecting water-bearing fractures.

Freeze Protection

Crestline sees genuine winter weather—freezing temperatures, snow accumulation, and ice. Any above-ground well components need proper freeze protection including insulated enclosures, heat tape, and protected piping. A frozen pressure tank or burst pipe during a mountain winter is a serious emergency.

Seasonal Water Table Fluctuations

Mountain aquifers respond to precipitation patterns. Snow melt recharges groundwater through spring, and wells typically show strongest production in late spring. By late summer, water tables may drop—wells need adequate depth and proper pump placement to handle these variations.

Crestline Winter Tip

Before winter, inspect your well house insulation, test heat tape circuits, and verify that any pump house heaters work properly. A $50 heat tape replacement prevents a $5,000 emergency repair during a January freeze.
