San Jacinto Valley Groundwater

Hemet benefits from its position in the San Jacinto Valley basin, with generally favorable groundwater conditions.

Valley Basin

The San Jacinto Valley basin contains productive aquifers that have supported agriculture for generations. Many properties can develop reliable wells at moderate depths.

Mountain Recharge

Surrounding mountains—San Jacinto to the east, San Bernardino to the north—provide natural watershed recharge, supporting the valley's groundwater.

Variable Quality

Water quality varies across the valley. Some areas have excellent water; others may have elevated minerals. We test every well and recommend treatment as needed.

FAQs

Variable across the valley. We test every well and recommend treatment as needed. Many wells produce good water; some need softening or filtration.
