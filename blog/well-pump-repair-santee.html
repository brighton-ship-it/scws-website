Santee's Well Water Landscape

Santee's position along the San Diego River has historically provided good groundwater resources. However, well conditions vary across the city based on proximity to the river, elevation, and underlying geology.

Key characteristics of Santee wells:

  • River valley locations: Properties near the San Diego River often have more favorable well conditions.
  • Hillside wells: Elevated areas may require deeper wells into fractured rock.
  • Variable depths: Well depths in Santee range from moderate to quite deep.
  • Water quality: Groundwater typically contains minerals that create hard water.
  • Development impact: Urban growth has affected groundwater levels in some areas.

Well Pump Types in Santee

SCWS services all pump types found in Santee homes and properties:

  • Submersible pumps: The most common type, installed inside the well to push water upward.
  • Jet pumps: Above-ground pumps suitable for shallower wells.
  • Constant pressure systems: Modern systems maintaining steady water pressure.
  • Booster pumps: Secondary pumps for pressure or distance challenges.
  • Irrigation systems: Dedicated pumps for landscape watering.

Common Well Pump Problems

Our Santee service calls typically address these issues:

  • Motor failure: Pump motors wear out over time, especially under heavy use.
  • Electrical component failures: Control boxes, capacitors, and switches degrade.
  • Pressure tank problems: Failed bladders cause short cycling and pump stress.
  • Scale accumulation: Hard water deposits build up on pump components.
  • Impeller wear: Sand and sediment erode pump internals.
  • Check valve issues: Failed valves cause backflow and efficiency loss.
  • Wiring problems: Damaged or corroded wiring affects pump operation.

Signs of Well Pump Trouble

Santee well owners should watch for these warning signs:

  • Decreased water pressure at fixtures
  • Pump running more often or longer
  • Frequent pressure tank cycling
  • Air in water lines
  • Strange sounds from pump equipment
  • Higher electricity bills
  • Changes in water appearance or taste
  • Complete water loss
