What sets Goulds apart from budget pump manufacturers is their commitment to heavy-duty construction. Every Goulds submersible pump features precision-engineered components designed for years of reliable service. Common Goulds Pump Problems

Even premium Goulds pumps can experience issues over time. Our technicians diagnose and repair these common problems daily:

Motor Failures

Submersible pump motors eventually wear out, typically after 15-25 years of service. Warning signs include:

  • Pump trips breaker repeatedly
  • Motor hums but won't start
  • Reduced water output over time
  • Increased electrical consumption
  • Motor runs hot (on jet pumps)

Goulds motors are typically Franklin Electric motors, which are serviceable and rebuildable in many cases. For submersible pumps, motor replacement usually requires pulling the entire pump assembly.

Impeller Wear

Sand and sediment gradually wear down pump impellers, reducing efficiency and output. Goulds stainless steel impellers resist wear better than plastic alternatives, but eventually need replacement. Signs of impeller wear include:

  • Gradual decrease in water pressure
  • Pump runs longer to fill pressure tank
  • Lower flow rates at fixtures
  • Increased energy consumption

Seal Failures

Mechanical seals protect the motor from water intrusion. When seals fail, water enters the motor housing causing catastrophic damage. Early detection can save costly motor replacement.

Control Box Issues

The control box contains capacitors and relays that can fail over time. Control box problems often mimic pump failure but are much cheaper to repair. Common symptoms include:

  • Pump won't start at all
  • Clicking sound from control box
  • Burnt smell or visual damage
  • Intermittent operation

Pressure Switch Problems

Pressure switches control pump cycling and can develop issues including:

  • Contacts burning or pitting
  • Diaphragm failure
  • Clogged sensing port
  • Short cycling due to misadjustment

Check Valve Failure

Failed check valves allow water to drain back into the well, causing the pump to work harder and cycle more frequently. This common issue is relatively inexpensive to repair.

Maintenance & Prevention

Regular maintenance extends Goulds pump life and prevents unexpected failures. Our maintenance services include:

Annual Inspection

A yearly checkup catches problems early. Our annual inspection includes:

  • Amp draw testing
  • Pressure tank pre-charge verification
  • Pressure switch cleaning and adjustment
  • Flow rate measurement
  • Visual inspection of all components
  • Water quality screening

Pressure Tank Maintenance

Proper pressure tank maintenance prevents short cycling that damages pumps. We check and adjust tank pre-charge to factory specifications.

Preventive Replacement

Some components should be replaced before failure:

  • Pressure switches: Every 5-7 years
  • Check valves: When showing signs of wear
  • Capacitors: When testing shows degradation
  • Pressure tanks: Every 10-15 years or when bladder fails

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do Goulds well pumps last?

Goulds submersible well pumps typically last 15-25 years with proper maintenance and good water quality. Their heavy-duty construction and stainless steel components contribute to exceptional longevity compared to budget brands. Some Goulds pumps in optimal conditions have operated for 30+ years.

What is the warranty on Goulds well pumps?

Goulds offers a 5-year limited warranty on most residential submersible pumps when installed by a licensed contractor. Extended warranties may be available through authorized dealers. The warranty covers manufacturing defects but not damage from sand, lightning, or improper installation.

Are Goulds pump parts readily available?

Yes, Goulds parts are widely available through authorized distributors. As a professional-grade brand owned by Xylem, parts supply is well-established. Most common parts can be sourced within 1-2 days in Southern California. We stock frequently needed components for same-day repairs.

How much does Goulds pump repair cost?

Goulds pump repair costs typically range from $300-$800 for minor repairs to $1,500-$4,000 for major overhauls including pump pulling. Control box repairs average $200-$500. Exact costs depend on the specific model, depth of well, and nature of the problem. Contact us for accurate estimates.

Can you repair my Goulds pump or does it need replacement?

Many Goulds pump problems are repairable. Control box issues, pressure switch failures, and minor component problems don't require pump replacement. Even worn impellers can sometimes be rebuilt. However, failed motors or severely worn pumps may be more cost-effective to replace. We'll provide honest recommendations based on your specific situation.

Is Goulds better than Grundfos?

Both are excellent professional-grade brands. Grundfos edges ahead on energy efficiency, while Goulds is known for rugged durability and slightly lower cost. Goulds has better parts availability in our area. For most residential applications, either brand will provide excellent, long-lasting service.

Do you install new Goulds pumps?

Yes, we're authorized installers for Goulds products. We can help you select the right Goulds pump for your well and provide professional installation with full warranty coverage.
