Well Water Turns Brown After Rain

If you've noticed your well water turning brown after a storm or rainfall, you're likely dealing with surface water intrusion.

Why Does This Happen?

This phenomenon occurs when rainwater infiltrates your well system, bringing with it sediment and other particulates. It might indicate issues such as poor well casing or a lack of proper seal.

Solutions to Clear Your Water

To rectify this problem, ensure your well casing is intact and consider sealing any gaps. You might also want to install additional filtration to clear the particulates from your water supply.
