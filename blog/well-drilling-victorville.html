Victor Valley Groundwater Conditions

Victorville sits along the Mojave River in San Bernardino County's High Desert. The area benefits from the Mojave River aquifer system, which provides groundwater recharge from mountain snowmelt. However, well depths and water availability vary significantly across the region.

Properties closer to the Mojave River corridor often find water at shallower depths (150-300 feet), while areas on the benches and mesas may require drilling to 400-600 feet. Our pre-drilling assessment includes reviewing local well data to give you realistic expectations.

Victorville Well Drilling Costs

A complete residential well system in Victorville typically costs $18,000 to $45,000. Key cost factors include:

  • Drilling depth: $35-$55 per foot depending on conditions
  • Casing type: Steel casing for deeper wells, PVC where appropriate
  • Pump equipment: Based on well depth and required flow rate
  • County permits: San Bernardino County well permit and inspections

Drilling Conditions in Victorville

Well drilling in Victorville typically encounters Mojave Desert alluvial basins and Transverse Ranges crystalline rock. Most wells can be completed in 1-3 days under normal conditions. The relatively moderate depths keep drilling costs reasonable, though rocky formations can slow progress.

San Bernardino County well permits are managed by the Land Use Services Department. Some areas have special requirements due to groundwater management plans. We handle the entire permit process for Victorville properties.
