Well Drilling Services in Lucerne Valley

Residential Well Drilling

For homeowners in Lucerne Valley seeking water independence, we design and install complete residential well systems. This includes drilling, casing, pump installation, pressure tank, and all electrical connections. Most homes need 5-10 GPM (gallons per minute) for comfortable water supply.

Agricultural & Irrigation Wells

San Bernardino County has a strong agricultural heritage. We drill high-capacity wells for farms, ranches, orchards, and vineyards. Agricultural wells often require higher flow rates (20-100+ GPM) and larger diameter casings to support irrigation demands.

Commercial Well Drilling

Businesses in Lucerne Valley requiring independent water supply can rely on SCWS for commercial well installation. We work with developers, contractors, and business owners to design systems that meet commercial demand requirements.

Well Drilling Cost in Lucerne Valley

Well drilling costs in Lucerne Valley typically range from $15,000 to $45,000 for a complete residential system. Key cost factors include:

  • Drilling depth: $35-$55 per foot depending on geology
  • Casing: Steel or PVC, based on well depth and conditions
  • Pump system: $1,500-$4,500 depending on depth and flow needs
  • Pressure tank: $500-$1,200 for quality bladder tanks
  • Electrical: $800-$1,500 for wiring and controls
  • Permits: San Bernardino County permit fees

We provide detailed written quotes before any work begins—no surprises, no hidden fees.
