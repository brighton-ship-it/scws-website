Well Permit Requirements for Riverside County, CA

Planning to drill a new well or make modifications in Riverside County? California requires permits for most well work. Southern California Well Service handles the entire permit process for you.

San Diego County Well Permit Requirements

In San Diego County, well permits are required for:

  • Drilling a new domestic or agricultural well
  • Deepening an existing well
  • Repairing or reconstructing well casing
  • Decommissioning an unused well
