Expert Well Drilling in Lemon Grove

Lemon Grove is a small city in San Diego County, situated between La Mesa and Spring Valley in the East County region. While served by the Helix Water District, Lemon Grove properties with larger lots often benefit from private wells for irrigation and backup water supply.

Southern California Well Service provides well drilling throughout Lemon Grove and neighboring communities. We specialize in residential wells, irrigation systems for larger properties, and services for existing wells.

Lemon Grove Service Area

  • Central Lemon Grove — Downtown and surrounding neighborhoods
  • Northern Lemon Grove — Near La Mesa border
  • Eastern Lemon Grove — Toward Spring Valley
  • Hillside properties — Larger lots with irrigation needs

Well Applications

Common reasons Lemon Grove property owners drill wells:

Residential Wells

  • Primary water supply — For properties outside water district
  • Backup water — Emergency supply supplement
  • Drought protection — Independent of water restrictions

Irrigation Wells

  • Landscape irrigation — Reduce water bills
  • Garden watering — Vegetable gardens, fruit trees
  • Pool/spa fill — Avoid high municipal costs

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep are wells in Lemon Grove?

Wells typically range from 150-350 feet deep. Valley locations often find water shallower, while hillside properties may require deeper drilling.

What does well drilling cost in Lemon Grove?

Complete well systems typically cost $18,000-$42,000 depending on depth and requirements.

Will a well save me money?

For properties using over 15,000 gallons monthly, wells often pay for themselves in 5-8 years through water cost savings.

Do I need a permit?

Yes, San Diego County DEH well permits are required. We handle the permitting process.
