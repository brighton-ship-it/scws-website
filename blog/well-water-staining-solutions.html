How to Remove Well Water Stains: Iron, Rust & Mineral Deposits

Updated February 2026 | By Southern California Well Service

Quick Answer: Iron stains (orange/rust): use oxalic acid cleaners like Bar Keepers Friend or commercial iron removers. Hard water (white deposits): vinegar or CLR. Manganese (brown/black): same as iron. For lasting fix, treat the water source with appropriate filtration.

Identify the Stain

Color Likely Cause
Orange, red, rust Iron
Brown, black Manganese
White, chalky Hard water (calcium/magnesium)
Blue, green Copper (acidic water corroding pipes)
Pink Bacteria (Serratia) - not water quality

Iron/Rust Stains

Where They Appear

  • Toilet bowls and tanks
  • Sinks and bathtubs
  • Dishwashers
  • Clothes and linens
  • Concrete and driveways

Best Cleaners

  • Bar Keepers Friend: Oxalic acid based, very effective
  • Iron OUT: Designed specifically for iron
  • Whink Rust Remover: Strong, use carefully
  • CLR: Works on light iron stains

DIY Options

  • Lemon juice + salt paste
  • White vinegar + baking soda
  • Cream of tartar paste

Step-by-Step

  1. Apply cleaner to dry surface
  2. Let sit 5-15 minutes (don't let dry)
  3. Scrub with non-scratching pad
  4. Rinse thoroughly
  5. Repeat if needed for stubborn stains

Tips

  • Don't use chlorine bleach—makes iron stains worse
  • Work in ventilated area
  • Wear gloves with strong cleaners
  • Test in inconspicuous area first

Hard Water Deposits

Where They Appear

  • Showerheads and faucets
  • Glass shower doors
  • Around faucet bases
  • Inside appliances
  • Dishes and glassware

Best Cleaners

  • White vinegar: Natural, effective, cheap
  • CLR: Calcium, Lime, Rust remover
  • Lime-A-Way: Similar to CLR
  • Citric acid: Natural option

For Showerheads

  1. Fill bag with vinegar
  2. Tie around showerhead submerged
  3. Leave overnight
  4. Scrub and rinse

For Glass Shower Doors

  1. Spray with vinegar solution
  2. Let sit 15-30 minutes
  3. Scrub with non-scratch pad
  4. Rinse and squeegee
  5. Heavy buildup may need multiple treatments

For Appliances

  • Dishwasher: Run empty with vinegar or citric acid
  • Coffee maker: Run vinegar through brew cycle
  • Water heater: Flush tank annually

Manganese Stains

Characteristics

  • Brown to black color
  • Often with iron stains
  • Can be harder to remove than iron

Best Cleaners

  • Oxalic acid based (Bar Keepers Friend)
  • Iron OUT (works on manganese too)
  • May need stronger products for heavy stains

Tips

  • Treat promptly—set-in stains harder to remove
  • May need multiple applications
  • Professional cleaning for severe cases

Copper Stains

Characteristics

  • Blue or green color
  • Usually under faucets or drains
  • Indicates acidic water corroding copper pipes

Best Cleaners

  • Lemon juice + salt
  • Ammonia-based cleaners
  • Commercial copper cleaners

Address the Source

Copper stains indicate pH problem. Neutralizer can stop the corrosion and prevent more staining.

Laundry Stains

Iron-Stained Clothes

  • Don't use chlorine bleach (sets stain)
  • Use Iron OUT or Rust Remover products
  • Soak before washing
  • Check before drying (heat sets stain)

For White Fabrics

  1. Soak in Iron OUT solution
  2. Wash with regular detergent
  3. Add 1/2 cup white vinegar to rinse
  4. Air dry and check before machine drying

Prevention

  • Run water before starting washer
  • Use detergent with water softening agents
  • Install whole-house iron filter

Prevention: Treat the Source

Maintenance Tips

  • Wipe fixtures after use
  • Squeegee shower doors
  • Regular cleaning prevents buildup
  • Address water quality for long-term solution

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does bleach make rust stains worse?

Chlorine bleach oxidizes iron further, intensifying the orange color. Use iron-specific cleaners instead.

How do I remove stains from toilet bowl?

Drain bowl (shut off valve, flush), apply iron remover or CLR to dry surface, let sit, scrub, and rinse.

Can I remove stains from concrete?

Yes—oxalic acid products or commercial rust removers work. May need pressure washing for severe cases.

How often should I clean to prevent buildup?

Weekly light cleaning is easier than monthly heavy cleaning. Treating your water eliminates the problem.

Will a water softener stop all staining?

Softeners handle hard water stains and low iron. High iron needs dedicated iron filter.
