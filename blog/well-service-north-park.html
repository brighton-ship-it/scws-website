About North Park

North Park was developed primarily in the 1910s-1930s and is named for its location north of Balboa Park. The neighborhood is now a designated Historic District with protected architecture and a thriving commercial corridor along 30th Street and University Avenue. The area is home to the iconic North Park Theatre marquee sign and hosts regular community events.

Properties from the original development era may have wells that were common before municipal water infrastructure was complete.
