Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my pressure tank is bad?

Signs of a failing pressure tank include: pump cycling on/off rapidly, waterlogged tank (heavy when full), fluctuating water pressure, or visible rust/corrosion. We can diagnose the problem and recommend repair or replacement.

How long do pressure tanks last?

Quality pressure tanks last 10-15 years on average. Bladder tanks may fail sooner if water quality is poor. Regular maintenance and proper air charge can extend tank life.

What size pressure tank do I need?

Tank size depends on your pump's flow rate and household demand. Most homes use 20-50 gallon tanks. Larger tanks reduce pump cycling and extend pump life. We calculate the right size for your system.
