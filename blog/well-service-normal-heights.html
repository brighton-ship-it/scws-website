About Normal Heights

Normal Heights got its name from the San Diego Normal School (a teachers' college) that once occupied nearby land. Today, Adams Avenue is the heart of the neighborhood, hosting the annual Adams Avenue Street Fair—one of San Diego's largest free music festivals. The neighborhood features beautiful 1920s-era Craftsman homes and an active, walkable community atmosphere.

Older bungalows, particularly those dating from the 1920s-1940s, may have original wells that served properties before municipal water became universal.
