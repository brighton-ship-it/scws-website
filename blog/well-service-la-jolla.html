About La Jolla

La Jolla is one of California's most affluent communities, featuring spectacular oceanfront homes, the Village shopping district, and world-renowned institutions including UC San Diego, Scripps Institution of Oceanography, and the Salk Institute. The community is known for its stunning coves, tide pools, and the La Jolla Underwater Park.

Larger estate properties, particularly in La Jolla Farms, Muirlands, Hidden Valley, and the Country Club area, often maintain private wells for irrigation. The coastal geology requires expertise to navigate potential saltwater intrusion while accessing quality groundwater.
