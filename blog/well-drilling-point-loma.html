Well Drilling in Point Loma

Point Loma — San Diego's prestigious peninsula — features historic estates, ocean views, and established neighborhoods. While most properties use City water, some larger estates and properties with significant irrigation needs may benefit from wells.

Southern California Well Service provides well drilling evaluations for Point Loma properties. Peninsula geology requires expert assessment.

Point Loma Well Considerations

  • Peninsula geology — Unique conditions
  • Saltwater interface — Must be evaluated
  • Estate properties — Large irrigation needs
  • Historic wells — Some properties have existing wells
  • Supplemental water — Reduce city bills
