Groundwater

Owens Valley Basin: Managed under agreement between Inyo County and LA DWP. Groundwater levels monitored to protect vegetation and environment.

  • Basin type: Alluvial valley fill
  • Recharge: Snowmelt from Sierra Nevada
  • Water quality: Excellent; low TDS
  • LADWP pumping: Regulated by long-term agreement
