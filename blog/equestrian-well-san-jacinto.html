Well Services for San Jacinto Horse Properties

Own a horse property in San Jacinto? Equestrian properties have unique water demands—from stock watering to arena maintenance. Southern California Well Service understands the water needs of Riverside County horse ranches.

Water Demands for Horse Properties

Horses and equestrian facilities require significant water:

  • Drinking: 5-15 gallons per horse per day
  • Stock tanks: Continuous fill to maintain levels
  • Arena watering: 1,000-3,000 gallons per watering
  • Wash racks: High-volume intermittent use
  • Pasture irrigation: If applicable
