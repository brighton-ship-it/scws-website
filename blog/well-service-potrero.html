Understanding Potrero's Groundwater

Potrero Valley enjoys relatively favorable groundwater conditions compared to some neighboring backcountry areas. The valley's geology and drainage patterns create conditions that support productive wells for most properties.

Potrero's Hydrogeology

Key characteristics of Potrero's groundwater:

  • Valley floor alluvium: Properties in the main valley often have shallower, more productive wells
  • Fractured granite: Hillside properties rely on fracture zones in bedrock
  • Seasonal recharge: Winter rains replenish aquifers through Potrero Creek drainage
  • Moderate depths: Generally shallower than more remote backcountry areas
  • Good yields: Many wells produce adequate flow for residential and small agricultural use

Factors Affecting Well Success

Well productivity in Potrero depends on:

  • Location in valley: Valley floor vs. hillside properties
  • Proximity to drainage: Closer to Potrero Creek often means better recharge
  • Fracture zones: Granite fractures are the primary water source for hillside wells
  • Drilling depth: Reaching productive zones is key
  • Well development: Proper development maximizes yield

Water Quality in Potrero

Potrero generally has good quality groundwater, though some properties experience common issues that can be easily treated.

Common Water Quality Characteristics

Hard Water

Most common issue. Causes scale buildup in pipes and appliances, soap residue on fixtures.

Iron/Manganese

Some wells have iron that causes orange staining. Easily treatable with proper filtration.

Generally Safe

Potrero wells typically test clean for bacteria and harmful contaminants.

Good Taste

Natural filtration through rock provides clean-tasting water for most wells.

Water Testing Services

We recommend regular water testing for all Potrero wells:

  • Annual bacterial testing: Coliform and E. coli screening
  • Mineral analysis: Hardness, iron, manganese, pH
  • Nitrate testing: Important for drinking water safety
  • Comprehensive panel: Full analysis for new wells or property sales

Treatment Solutions

Based on your water test results, we can recommend and install:

  • Water softeners: For hard water treatment
  • Iron filters: Remove iron and manganese
  • UV disinfection: Additional bacterial protection
  • Sediment filtration: For sandy or silty water
  • Whole-house systems: Comprehensive treatment

Warning Signs to Watch For

⚠️ Call for Service If You Notice:

  • • Declining water pressure
  • • Pump running more frequently or longer
  • • Air sputtering from faucets
  • • Changes in water taste or color
  • • Sediment in water
  • • Higher than normal electric bills
  • • Unusual pump noises
  • • Water running out during high use
