Constant Pressure System Pricing

VFD controller units cost $800-$1,500. Professional installation runs $500-$1,500. Total system cost is typically $2,000-$4,000. Premium systems with monitoring range from $3,500-$5,500. Electrical panel upgrades may be required, adding $300-$800.

How Constant Pressure Works

A Variable Frequency Drive adjusts pump speed to match demand, maintaining steady pressure (typically 50-60 PSI). This eliminates pressure fluctuations when multiple fixtures run simultaneously. The pump ramps up and down smoothly versus cycling on and off. A smaller pressure tank is needed, saving space.

Benefits Over Standard Systems

Constant pressure delivers consistent shower pressure regardless of other water use. Reduced pump cycling extends motor life. Energy costs typically drop 20-30%. Soft start reduces electrical stress and water hammer. These systems are especially beneficial for low-yield wells, as the pump can match available supply.

Is Constant Pressure Right for You?

Constant pressure is best for homes with multiple bathrooms and excellent for irrigation systems requiring steady flow. It's ideal for low-producing wells under 10 GPM. However, it may be overkill for simple, single-bathroom applications. The system requires a compatible submersible pump.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will a constant pressure system work with my existing pump?

Most 3-wire submersible pumps are compatible with VFD controllers. 2-wire pumps typically are not. Your contractor should evaluate your current pump before installation. If a new pump is needed, budget an additional $800-$2,000.

How much will I save on electricity?

Most homeowners see 20-30% reduction in pump energy costs. The savings come from running the pump at lower speeds most of the time instead of full-power cycling. Payback period is typically 3-7 years depending on usage.

What maintenance does a VFD system need?

VFD systems require minimal maintenance. Keep the controller clean and dry, check connections annually, and monitor for error codes. Most quality units last 10-15 years. Extended warranties are available and recommended given the electronics involved.
