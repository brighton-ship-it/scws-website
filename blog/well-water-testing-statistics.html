Testing Frequency Recommendations

  • Annual: Coliform bacteria, nitrate
  • Every 3 years: pH, hardness, TDS, iron, manganese
  • One-time baseline: Heavy metals, radon, VOCs
  • After events: Flooding, nearby construction, illness

Frequently Asked Questions

What percentage of wells have contamination?

Studies show 20-25% of private wells have at least one contaminant exceeding EPA guidelines.

How much does testing cost?

Basic testing costs $25-75; comprehensive panels $200-500.

How often should I test?

EPA recommends annual testing for bacteria and nitrates.

Are private wells regulated?

No, private wells are not covered by EPA's Safe Drinking Water Act. Owners are responsible for their own testing.
