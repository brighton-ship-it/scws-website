About Point Loma

Point Loma has been inhabited for over 9,000 years and is where European explorer Juan Rodríguez Cabrillo first landed in California in 1542. Today, the peninsula is home to a mix of historic neighborhoods including La Playa, Roseville-Fleetridge, and Sunset Cliffs. The area also includes Naval Base Point Loma, Liberty Station, and Point Loma Nazarene University.

Larger properties in Fleetridge, La Playa, and the hillside areas often have private wells for landscape irrigation, particularly for maintaining the mature gardens characteristic of this established community.
