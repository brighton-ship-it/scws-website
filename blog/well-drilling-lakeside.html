Why Lakeside Property Owners Choose Wells

While Lakeside Water District provides municipal water service throughout much of the community, many property owners find significant advantages in private well ownership. The benefits are particularly compelling for larger properties with substantial water needs.

  • Irrigation Savings: Properties with extensive landscaping or agricultural use save substantially over municipal rates.
  • No Tiered Pricing: Avoid escalating rates as usage increases during summer months.
  • Water Independence: Freedom from mandatory conservation restrictions during drought.
  • Property Enhancement: Productive wells add significant value to rural properties.
  • Backup Supply: Maintain water availability during municipal system disruptions.
  • Water Quality: Many prefer the taste of untreated groundwater.

Lakeside's Groundwater Geology

Lakeside occupies the San Diego River valley and surrounding hillsides, featuring varied geological conditions that affect well development. Understanding these conditions is essential for successful drilling.

  • Alluvial Deposits: Properties near the San Diego River may access productive sand and gravel aquifers.
  • Weathered Granite: Decomposed granitic rock can yield substantial water from storage zones.
  • Fractured Bedrock: Deeper granite stores water in fracture networks.
  • Stadium Conglomerate: This distinctive formation underlies portions of Lakeside with unique characteristics.

Well depths in Lakeside typically range from 150 to 500 feet, varying with location and target aquifer. Properties in valley bottoms may find productive water at shallower depths, while hillside locations often require deeper drilling.

Water Quality Services

Lakeside groundwater quality varies by location. Common considerations include:

  • Hardness: Elevated calcium and magnesium levels in many areas.
  • Total Dissolved Solids: Some locations have higher mineral content.
  • Iron and Manganese: Can cause staining and taste issues.
  • Nitrates: May be elevated in agricultural areas.

We provide comprehensive water testing and design treatment systems addressing any quality concerns. From simple filtration to complete treatment, we ensure your water meets your standards.
