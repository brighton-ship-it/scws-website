Lakeside's Groundwater

Lakeside benefits from its position along the San Diego River, with generally favorable groundwater conditions throughout much of the community.

River Basin Advantage

Properties near the San Diego River and its floodplain often have excellent water access. The alluvial deposits store substantial groundwater, and wells in these areas frequently produce 20-50+ GPM.

Surrounding Hills

Properties on the hills surrounding the valley encounter decomposed granite and fractured rock—still productive but typically requiring deeper drilling than valley-floor locations.

Good Quality

Lakeside wells generally produce good water quality. Some wells may have moderate hardness, but most require minimal treatment for household use.

Rural Property Considerations

FAQs

Is Lakeside good for well drilling?

Generally yes, especially near the San Diego River valley. The area has historically favorable groundwater conditions. Conditions vary by location, which is why we provide free site evaluations.

How much water do horses need?

Horses drink 5-10 gallons daily, more in summer. Add arena irrigation, pasture watering, and wash rack use, and equestrian properties often need 15-25+ GPM systems. We design for actual horse property demands.

Can I water my arena with well water?

Absolutely. Arena irrigation is one of the main uses for Lakeside equestrian wells. A good well eliminates metered water costs for this water-intensive need.

What about fire protection?

Critical for Lakeside. We regularly design systems with 10,000+ gallon fire reserves separate from daily supply. This can also benefit insurance rates.
