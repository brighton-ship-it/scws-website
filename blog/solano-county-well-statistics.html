Solano County Well Overview

Solano County sits at the confluence of the Sacramento Valley and San Francisco Bay region. The county relies on a mix of surface water (Solano Project) and groundwater, with the Solano Subbasin providing important agricultural and domestic supply.

  • Primary aquifer: Alluvial deposits, Tehama Formation

  • Recharge: Precipitation, stream seepage, irrigation return

Frequently Asked Questions

How many wells are in Solano County?

Solano County has approximately 7,850 registered wells, including 4,650 domestic and 2,400 agricultural wells.

What is the average well depth?

Domestic wells average 225 feet; agricultural wells average 380 feet.

How much does a well cost?

Average cost is $32,500 for a complete domestic system (2026).

Is groundwater regulated?

Yes, the Solano Subbasin is Medium Priority under SGMA with sustainability planning in progress.
