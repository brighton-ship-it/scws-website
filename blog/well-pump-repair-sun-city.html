Common Pump Problems in Sun City

Problem Common Causes Solution
No water Pump failure, tripped breaker Diagnosis and repair
Pump runs constantly Waterlogged tank, leak Tank replacement, leak repair
Low pressure Worn impellers, tank issue Pump service or upgrade
Rapid cycling Waterlogged tank Tank replacement
Breaker tripping Seized pump, bad capacitor Electrical diagnosis

Quick Troubleshooting

  • Check breaker — Reset once
  • Tap pressure switch — May restore function
  • Listen to tank — Hollow = good, solid = waterlogged
  • Check for leaks — Toilets, irrigation
