Understanding San Bernardino County's Diverse Hydrogeology

San Bernardino County's groundwater systems are as diverse as its landscape. From the San Bernardino Mountains to the Mojave Desert floor, each region presents unique drilling challenges and opportunities that require specialized knowledge and experience.

  • Mountain Communities (Big Bear, Lake Arrowhead, Crestline): Fractured granite aquifers with variable production; wells typically 300-800 feet deep. Cold temperatures require freeze protection for wellheads and piping.
  • Foothill Communities (Yucaipa, Highland, Redlands): Alluvial fans and fractured bedrock transitions; excellent water quality potential with depths ranging 200-500 feet.
  • High Desert (Apple Valley, Victorville, Hesperia): Deep alluvial basins with productive aquifers; wells commonly 400-1000+ feet to reach quality water below mineral-laden shallow zones.
  • Rural Valley Areas: Mixed geology requiring site-specific evaluation; often excellent production from properly constructed wells.

Communities We Serve Throughout San Bernardino County

Big Bear and Mountain Communities

The Big Bear area presents unique challenges including hard granite formations, seasonal access limitations, and cold weather considerations. Our crews have extensive experience working in mountain conditions, understanding the importance of proper well construction for freeze protection. We've drilled successful wells throughout Big Bear City, Big Bear Lake, Fawnskin, and the surrounding forest areas, providing reliable water for year-round residents and vacation properties alike.

Yucaipa and the Foothills

Yucaipa's location at the base of the San Bernardino Mountains creates favorable conditions for groundwater. The alluvial fans sweeping down from the mountains often contain productive aquifers, while the area's agricultural heritage—particularly the famous Yucaipa apple orchards—demonstrates the region's water availability. We serve properties throughout Yucaipa, Oak Glen, and the surrounding communities.

Redlands and Highland

Historic Redlands, once the heart of Southern California's citrus industry, sits atop valuable groundwater resources. The combination of mountain runoff recharge and quality alluvial deposits creates excellent well potential. Highland, nestled against the San Bernardino Mountains, shares similar favorable hydrogeology. SCWS has drilled countless residential and agricultural wells throughout both communities.

Victor Valley: Apple Valley, Victorville, and Hesperia

The high desert communities of Victor Valley have experienced tremendous growth, and many properties rely on private wells for their water supply. While wells in this region often run deeper than in other areas, the regional aquifers can provide excellent, long-term water supplies when properly developed. Our extensive experience in Apple Valley, Victorville, and Hesperia ensures we understand the specific drilling requirements of high desert conditions.

Well Rehabilitation and Repair Services

Many San Bernardino County properties have existing wells that may be decades old. Before committing to a new well, SCWS can evaluate your existing well to determine if rehabilitation might restore production at a fraction of the cost of drilling.

  • Video Inspection: Downhole camera surveys reveal casing condition, perforations, and potential problems.
  • Well Cleaning: Removing mineral scale, biological growth, and sediment that restrict water flow.
  • Hydrofracturing: High-pressure treatment to open new fractures and improve production in bedrock wells.
  • Well Deepening: Drilling deeper to access additional water-bearing zones below the current well bottom.
  • Pump System Upgrades: Modern, efficient pumping equipment to optimize your well's performance.
