Common Well Pump Problems in Beaumont

No Water from the Well

Complete water loss requires immediate attention. In Beaumont, common causes include:

  • Electrical failures: Control box, capacitor, or wiring problems
  • Pump motor burnout: Often heat-related during summer
  • Pressure switch failure: Not signaling pump to run
  • Dropped water table: Especially during drought years
  • Pipe or fitting failure: Down-hole equipment problems

Declining Water Pressure

Gradual pressure loss often indicates developing problems:

  • Waterlogged pressure tank: Lost air charge affecting performance
  • Wearing pump: Reduced efficiency from impeller or bearing wear
  • Scale buildup: Beaumont's harder water can cause mineral deposits
  • Partially clogged pipes: Sediment or mineral restrictions

Pump Cycling Issues

If your pump turns on and off frequently (short-cycling), you're wearing it out prematurely:

  • Failed pressure tank bladder: Most common cause
  • Pressure switch problems: Incorrect settings or worn contacts
  • Check valve failure: Water draining back down well
  • Small leaks: Constant water loss preventing stable pressure
