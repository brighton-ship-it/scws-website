DIY Troubleshooting Before You Call

Check Your Electrical Panel

  • Look for a tripped breaker — Reset it once
  • If it trips again immediately, don't keep resetting — call us

Check the Pressure Switch

  • Located on or near the pressure tank
  • Listen for clicking when water runs
  • A gentle tap may restore function temporarily

Check the Pressure Tank

  • Tap on tank — hollow = good, solid thud = waterlogged
  • Check air valve pressure

Check for Leaks

  • Inspect around wellhead
  • Check hose bibs and irrigation
  • Look under sinks
  • Check toilets for running water

Call Immediately If:

  • Breaker trips repeatedly
  • Burning smell from control box
  • Complete water loss
  • Grinding or screeching sounds

Redlands sits at the base of the San Bernardino Mountains, with a history shaped by citrus agriculture and artesian wells. While the city center is largely on municipal water, many areas still use private wells.

Well pump repair in Redlands typically costs $300-$1,500 for most repairs. Pressure switch replacement runs $150-$400. Full submersible pump replacement costs $1,500-$4,000 depending on depth.

Are there still private wells in Redlands?

Yes, many Redlands properties still use private wells, especially in the foothills, rural areas near Mentone, and older properties with citrus groves. We service wells throughout the Redlands area.

What causes hard water from Redlands wells?

Redlands groundwater often has elevated mineral content due to local geology. While safe to drink, it can affect appliances. We can refer you to water treatment specialists.

Yes, we have experience with older well systems common on historic Redlands properties. We can repair, upgrade, or advise on replacement options.
