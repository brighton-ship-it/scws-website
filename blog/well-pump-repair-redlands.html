DIY Troubleshooting Before You Call

Check Your Electrical Panel

  • Look for a tripped breaker — Reset it once
  • If it trips again immediately, don't keep resetting — call us

Check the Pressure Switch

  • Located on or near the pressure tank
  • Listen for clicking when water runs
  • A gentle tap may restore function temporarily

Check the Pressure Tank

  • Tap on tank — hollow = good, solid thud = waterlogged
  • Check air valve pressure

Check for Leaks

  • Inspect around wellhead
  • Check hose bibs and irrigation
  • Look under sinks
  • Check toilets for running water

Call Immediately If:

  • Breaker trips repeatedly
  • Burning smell from control box
  • Complete water loss
  • Grinding or screeching sounds

Common Pump Problems in Redlands

The geological conditions in Redlands — mixed alluvial deposits and crystalline basement rock of the Peninsular Ranges — create specific challenges for well pumps. While moderate well depths are easier on pumps, local water chemistry and sediment conditions can still cause premature wear.

The most common pump repair calls we get from Redlands include: pumps running but producing low flow (often a failing impeller or dropped water level), circuit breakers tripping when the pump starts (bad capacitor or motor windings), and pressure tank waterlogging (failed bladder). We carry common parts on our trucks for same-day repair in most cases.
