Well Water Pressure Drops When Toilet Flushes

Experiencing a drop in well water pressure when you flush the toilet is a common issue that many homeowners face. This typically occurs due to an undersized pressure tank or a poorly functioning pump. In this article, we will guide you through the possible causes and how to resolve them.

Common Causes

The most frequent reason for a pressure drop is an undersized pressure tank. If the tank is too small, it cannot maintain the necessary pressure when multiple water outlets are in use. Another potential issue is the water pump itself; if it is not powerful enough, it cannot cope with the demand.

Diagnosis and Fixes

To determine the cause, start by checking the pressure tank’s size and condition. If it appears undersized, consider installing a larger tank. Alternatively, evaluate the pump; it may be time to upgrade to a more robust model. Regular maintenance of both the pump and pressure tank can prevent such issues from arising.

FAQs

  • Can I fix low water pressure myself? Yes, for minor issues like adjusting the tank size or checking the pump. However, more complex problems should be handled by professionals.
  • How do I know if my pressure tank is too small? If you frequently experience pressure drops, especially during multiple uses, your tank might be undersized.
