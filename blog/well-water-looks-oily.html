Well Water Looks Oily or Has Sheen

Seeing an oily film on your well water can be alarming. It may be due to bacterial action, decaying matter, or even actual contamination.

Identification and Solutions

First, determine if the sheen is rainbow-colored (oil) or white (bacteria) to identify the problem. Regular testing is essential to ensure safety and proper corrective measures.
