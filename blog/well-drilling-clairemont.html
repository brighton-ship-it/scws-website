Well Services in Clairemont

Clairemont is one of San Diego's largest communities — a planned development from the 1950s-60s that includes residential neighborhoods, shopping centers, and significant commercial areas along Clairemont Mesa Boulevard and Balboa Avenue. The area is served by City of San Diego municipal water.

Southern California Well Service provides well services for Clairemont properties, focusing on commercial irrigation, industrial applications, existing well maintenance, and backup water systems for larger facilities.

Clairemont Area

  • Clairemont Mesa — Commercial/industrial zones
  • North Clairemont — Residential with some commercial
  • Bay Park — Adjacent residential community
  • Linda Vista border — Near USD campus area

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I drill a residential well in Clairemont?

New residential wells in Clairemont are uncommon due to urban density and lot sizes. However, larger residential properties may be evaluated for feasibility.

Do you drill commercial wells in Clairemont Mesa?

Yes, commercial and industrial wells are practical in Clairemont Mesa's larger commercial lots. We evaluate sites for irrigation, process water, and backup supply applications.

How much does a commercial well cost?

Commercial wells in Clairemont typically cost $30,000-70,000 depending on depth, capacity, and system requirements.

What about existing wells from the 1950s-60s?

Some Clairemont properties have wells from original development. We can assess, rehabilitate, or properly abandon these older wells.
