Well Rehabilitation Services

Is your well producing less water than it used to? Well rehabilitation can restore production at a fraction of the cost of drilling a new well.

Rehabilitation Methods

  • Hydrofracturing: High-pressure water opens new fractures
  • Chemical treatment: Dissolves mineral deposits
  • Mechanical cleaning: Removes sediment and debris
  • Acidizing: For limestone and calcium deposits

When to Consider Rehabilitation

  • Gradual decline in water production
  • Increased sediment in water
  • Pump runtime increasing
  • Well is less than 30 years old

Cost

Well rehabilitation typically costs $3,000-$15,000 depending on method and well condition—often much less than a new well.
