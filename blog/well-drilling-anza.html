Well Drilling in Anza

Anza Valley — the high desert plateau at 4,000 feet elevation — is entirely dependent on private wells. With no municipal water service, every property in Anza needs a well. As your local Anza well company, we know this valley better than anyone.

Southern California Well Service maintains an office at 57174 US Highway 79, Anza. We've drilled hundreds of wells throughout Anza Valley, Aguanga, Terwilliger, and surrounding mountain communities.

Well Depths in Anza

Anza's high desert presents unique conditions:

Typical Depth Ranges

Area Typical Depth
Valley floor 200-400 ft
Central Anza 250-450 ft
Higher elevations 350-600 ft
Mountain areas 300-700+ ft

Yield Expectations

  • Valley floor: 15-50 GPM
  • Central areas: 10-35 GPM
  • Mountain: 5-25 GPM

High Desert Geology

  • Decomposed granite: Surface layers
  • Fractured granite: Primary water source
  • Alluvium: Valley floor pockets
  • Weathered bedrock: Variable yields

Storage Solutions

Many Anza properties use storage tanks:

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep are wells in Anza?

Wells typically range from 200-700 feet deep. Valley floor is usually shallower; mountain properties need deeper wells.

Is Anza hard to drill?

Anza has unique challenges but achievable. We know where the water is — we've drilled here for years.

Do I need a storage tank?

Commonly recommended in Anza. Storage tanks provide fire protection, peak capacity, and backup.
