Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a well inspection cost?

Basic inspections cost $300-500; comprehensive inspections $500-1,000.

What percentage of wells fail inspection?

About 25-35% have at least one deficiency; serious issues occur in about 10%.

What does inspection include?

Visual wellhead inspection, pump performance testing, flow rate measurement, and water quality sampling.
