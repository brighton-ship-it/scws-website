Well Pump Overheating

Overheating in well pumps is a common problem that can lead to reduced efficiency or even mechanical failure. Identifying and addressing the cause is crucial for continued performance.

Causes

Common causes include a lack of adequate ventilation, excessive cycling, or issues with the motor bearings. Each can result in a well pump generating excessive heat.

Symptoms

Symptoms of overheating may include unusual smells, such as a burning odor, and the pump frequently cutting out due to built-in thermal protection.

Solutions

Ensure the area around the pump has proper ventilation, and inspect and maintain components like motor bearings. Reduce cycling by adjusting pressure settings accordingly.
