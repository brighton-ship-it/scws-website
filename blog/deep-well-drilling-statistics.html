Frequently Asked Questions

What is a deep well?

Wells over 300 feet are generally considered deep for domestic use.

How much does a deep well cost?

Deep wells (400-600 ft) cost $50,000-95,000; very deep wells can exceed $150,000.

Why are wells getting deeper?

Declining water tables from overdraft, drought, and increased demand require deeper drilling.
