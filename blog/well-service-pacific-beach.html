About Pacific Beach

Pacific Beach is bounded by Mission Bay to the south, La Jolla to the north, and features miles of sandy beaches along the Pacific Ocean. The community is known for Crystal Pier, the lively Garnet Avenue corridor, and the Mission Bay Boardwalk. PB attracts a mix of young professionals, students, and beach enthusiasts.

While most PB properties connect to city water, some older single-family homes and commercial properties near Mission Bay may have legacy wells that require maintenance or could benefit from rehabilitation.
