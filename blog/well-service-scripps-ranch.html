About Scripps Ranch

Scripps Ranch takes its name from the Scripps family, who were prominent San Diego newspaper publishers. The community was developed beginning in the 1970s around the historic Scripps Ranch, which dates to the 1860s. The neighborhood is distinguished by its signature eucalyptus trees—many of which were damaged in the 2003 Cedar Fire but have been restored.

Lake Miramar provides recreational opportunities, and the community is known for its top-rated schools in the Poway Unified School District. Properties in the older, larger-lot areas and those near the MCAS Miramar boundary are most likely to have or benefit from private wells.
