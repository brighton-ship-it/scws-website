Carlsbad Well Applications

Agricultural & Nursery Wells

Carlsbad's famous flower fields and numerous nurseries often rely on irrigation wells. These high-demand applications require efficient pump systems and proper maintenance to meet peak season water needs.

Estate Irrigation

Larger estate properties in La Costa and eastern Carlsbad sometimes maintain wells for landscape irrigation—reducing water bills and providing independence from water restrictions.

Backup Water Supply

Some properties maintain wells as supplemental or backup water sources, valuable during drought restrictions or emergencies.

Carlsbad Area Well Characteristics

  • Typical depths: 150-400 feet
  • Primary uses: Irrigation, agricultural, backup supply
  • Geology: Coastal sedimentary formations
  • Water quality: Variable; some areas have salinity concerns
