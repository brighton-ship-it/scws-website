About Hillcrest

Hillcrest was developed primarily in the 1910s-1920s and has been the heart of San Diego's LGBTQ+ community since the 1970s. The neighborhood borders Balboa Park and features the historic Hillcrest sign, installed in 1940 and now a San Diego landmark. The area hosts the annual San Diego Pride parade and numerous community events.

Properties from the original development era may have wells that predate municipal water service to the neighborhood.
