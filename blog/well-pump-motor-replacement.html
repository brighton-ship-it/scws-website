Well Pump Motor Replacement

When a well pump motor fails, you'll need to decide between motor replacement and full pump replacement. Here's what to consider.

Signs of Motor Failure

  • Pump won't start (no sound at all)
  • Breaker trips when pump tries to start
  • Humming but not running
  • Burning smell from control box

Motor Replacement Cost

  • 1/2 HP motor: $300-$500
  • 1 HP motor: $400-$700
  • 2+ HP motor: $600-$1,200
  • Labor: $500-$1,500 depending on well depth

Repair vs Replace?

If your pump is over 10 years old, full replacement is often more cost-effective than motor-only replacement. The labor to pull the pump is the same either way.
