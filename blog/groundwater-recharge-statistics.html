Frequently Asked Questions

What is natural recharge rate?

Natural recharge ranges from 0.5-3 in/year in arid areas to 10-25 in/year in humid regions.

How much is recharged through MAR?

US MAR projects add approximately 3-4 million acre-feet annually.

Is recharge keeping up with pumping?

No, extraction exceeds recharge by 20-25 million acre-feet annually in many basins.
