Well Drilling in Temecula

Temecula — Southern California's wine country — features world-class vineyards, estate properties, and equestrian facilities. Many Temecula properties, especially in the wine country and rural areas, rely on private wells.

Southern California Well Service provides well drilling throughout Temecula and surrounding areas. From our nearby Anza location, we serve wine country, De Luz, and all of Temecula Valley.

Why Temecula Properties Need Wells

  • Wine country vineyards — Irrigation needs
  • Estate properties — Large landscape requirements
  • Rural areas — Beyond EMWD service
  • Cost savings — Reduce water bills
  • Drought protection — Reliable supply

Area Typical Depth

Local Geology

  • Temecula Formation: Some areas

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep are wells in Temecula?

Wells typically range from 200-600 feet deep. Valley floor properties often find water at 200-350 feet.

Is Temecula wine country good for wells?

Yes, wine country has productive aquifers. Many wineries depend entirely on well water.

Do you serve De Luz?

Yes, we serve De Luz, Rainbow, and all surrounding areas.

What about winery wells?

We specialize in vineyard and winery wells with high-capacity systems.
