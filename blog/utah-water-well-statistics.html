Frequently Asked Questions

How many water wells are in Utah?

Utah has approximately 145,000 registered water wells.

What is the average well depth in Utah?

Average is 300-450 feet. Wasatch Front wells average 350-600 feet.

How much does it cost to drill a well in Utah?

A complete residential well costs $18,000-$38,000. Salt Lake Valley typically $28,000-$45,000.

Do you need a permit for a well in Utah?

Yes, all wells require Division of Water Rights approval. Most areas are over-appropriated.
