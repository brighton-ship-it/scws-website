Water Treatment in Glen Avon

Well water quality varies by location. Southern California Well Service provides water testing and treatment solutions for Glen Avon homes and businesses throughout Riverside County.

Common Water Quality Issues

  • Hard water — Scale buildup, dry skin, spotted dishes
  • Iron and manganese — Staining, metallic taste
  • Sulfur — Rotten egg smell
  • Bacteria — Coliform contamination
  • Nitrates — Agricultural runoff
  • TDS (Total Dissolved Solids) — Mineral content

Treatment Solutions

  • Water softeners — Remove hardness minerals
  • Iron filters — Eliminate iron and manganese
  • Reverse osmosis — Drinking water purification
  • UV disinfection — Kill bacteria and viruses
  • Chemical feed systems — Chlorination for bacteria
  • Sediment filters — Remove particles and sand

Water Testing

We can help you get your well water tested through certified labs. Testing identifies exactly what treatment you need — no guessing, no overselling.

FAQs: Water Treatment

Do I need treatment?

A water test reveals what's in your water. Many wells need no treatment; others benefit from softening or filtration.

How much does treatment cost?

Systems range from $500 for basic sediment filters to $5,000+ for whole-house treatment. We recommend only what you need.
