Well Pressure Tank Sizing Guide

Updated February 2026 | By Southern California Well Service

Quick Answer: Minimum: 1 gallon drawdown per GPM of pump. Common residential: 32-50 gallon tanks. Bigger = fewer pump cycles = longer pump life. Don't undersize. Tank gallons ≠ drawdown gallons (only ~1/3 is usable water).

Pressure Tank Basics

What It Does

  • Stores water under pressure
  • Provides water between pump cycles
  • Reduces pump starts
  • Maintains consistent pressure

How It Works

  • Air bladder separates air and water
  • Compressed air pushes water out
  • As water drains, pressure drops
  • At low pressure, pump starts

Key Term: Drawdown

  • Drawdown = usable water between pump cycles
  • Not the same as tank size
  • Depends on tank size and pressure range
  • Typically about 1/3 of tank volume

Example

  • 32-gallon tank
  • Drawdown: ~10 gallons
  • Pump fills tank, then waits
  • You use 10 gallons before pump starts again

How to Size Your Tank

Rule of Thumb

1 gallon drawdown per GPM of pump

  • 10 GPM pump = 10 gallons drawdown minimum
  • This ensures 1 minute run time per cycle
  • Longer run times are better for pump

Calculating Tank Size

  1. Determine pump GPM
  2. That's your minimum drawdown
  3. Drawdown ≈ 1/3 of tank size
  4. Tank size = drawdown × 3

Example Calculation

  • Pump: 12 GPM
  • Minimum drawdown: 12 gallons
  • Tank size: 12 × 3 = 36 gallons
  • Choose 40-52 gallon tank

Bigger Is Better

  • Oversizing has no downside
  • Fewer pump cycles
  • Longer pump life
  • More water during power outage

Undersizing Problems

Short Cycling

  • Pump turns on/off rapidly
  • Under 1 minute run time
  • Stresses motor
  • Wears pump faster

Consequences

  • Premature pump failure
  • Higher electric bills
  • Pressure switch wear
  • More frequent repairs

Signs Tank Is Too Small

  • Pump cycles frequently
  • Very short run times
  • Clicking pressure switch

Solution

  • Upgrade to larger tank
  • Or add second tank in parallel
  • Cost: $300-$800 installed

Frequently Asked Questions

What size pressure tank do I need?

1 gallon drawdown per GPM of pump. 10 GPM pump needs ~32 gallon tank minimum. Bigger is better.

What happens if pressure tank is too small?

Short cycling—pump wears out faster, higher bills, more repairs. Size up if cycling too much.

Can a pressure tank be too big?

Not really. Bigger tanks reduce cycling. Only concern is space and cost.

What size tank for a 3/4 HP pump?

Depends on GPM output, not HP. Typically 10-12 GPM, so 32-44 gallon tank recommended.

Should I upgrade my tank when replacing pump?

Good opportunity to upsize. Bigger tank protects your new pump investment.
