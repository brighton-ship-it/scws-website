Common Well Pump Problems

Our Oceanside service calls frequently address these issues:

  • Motor failures: Pump motors wear out from continuous use.
  • Electrical problems: Control boxes, capacitors, and switches degrade.
  • Scale and corrosion: Mineral deposits and corrosion affect components.
  • Pressure tank issues: Failed bladders cause pump cycling problems.
  • Impeller wear: Sand and sediment erode pump internals.
  • Check valve failures: Worn valves reduce efficiency.
  • Salt-related issues: Coastal proximity can create unique corrosion challenges.

Signs Your Well Pump Needs Service

Watch for these warning signs:

  • Decreasing water pressure
  • Pump running constantly or cycling frequently
  • Air in water lines
  • Strange sounds from equipment
  • Higher electricity bills
  • Changes in water quality
  • Complete loss of water
  • Irrigation system underperforming
