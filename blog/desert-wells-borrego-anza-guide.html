Desert Groundwater Challenges

Borrego Valley — Critical Overdraft

The Borrego Valley groundwater basin is critically overdrafted — more water is pumped out than nature recharges. This means:

  • Declining water levels: Wells that worked 20 years ago may be inadequate now
  • Groundwater Sustainability Agency: Pumping restrictions are being implemented
  • Deeper drilling: May be necessary to reach water
  • Water quality changes: As levels drop, water chemistry can change

Anza Valley

Anza sits in a high desert valley with better groundwater conditions than Borrego:

  • Alluvial aquifer with generally good yields
  • Typical depths: 150-400 feet
  • Less regulatory pressure than Borrego
  • Cold winters require freeze protection

Water Quality Issues

Desert groundwater often has quality challenges:

High TDS (Total Dissolved Solids)

  • Desert water often runs 500-1,500+ ppm TDS
  • Tastes minerally or salty
  • May need reverse osmosis for drinking
  • Adequate for irrigation and most household uses

Hardness

  • Very hard water common (200-400+ ppm)
  • Scale buildup in pipes, water heaters
  • Water softener recommended for indoor use

Sulfate

  • Some desert wells have elevated sulfate
  • Laxative effect at high levels
  • Test before drinking

Arsenic

  • Natural arsenic occurs in some desert aquifers
  • EPA limit is 10 ppb
  • Treatment available if needed
  • Always test new desert wells for arsenic

Heat Considerations

Desert temperatures exceeding 110°F affect well systems:

Pump and Motor Stress

  • Hot groundwater (80-90°F) reduces motor cooling
  • Quality motors rated for high temperatures essential
  • Avoid running pump during hottest hours if possible

Above-Ground Equipment

  • Shade pressure tanks and control boxes
  • UV-resistant materials for exposed components
  • Check pressure tank regularly (heat accelerates bladder wear)

Water Demand

  • Summer water use can triple
  • Landscaping irrigation is major demand
  • Evaporative coolers use significant water
  • Size systems for peak summer demand

Regulatory Considerations

Borrego Valley GSA

The Borrego Valley Groundwater Sustainability Agency is implementing pumping restrictions to address overdraft. New wells may face:

  • Registration requirements
  • Metering mandates
  • Potential pumping allocations

Check current regulations before drilling in Borrego Springs.

County Permits

Standard San Diego or Riverside County well permits apply. Desert locations may have additional setback requirements near washes or conservation areas.
