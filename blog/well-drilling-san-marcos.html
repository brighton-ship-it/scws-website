Well Drilling in San Marcos

San Marcos is a growing North County city that blends suburban development with rural character. From the Lake San Marcos community to Twin Oaks Valley estates, San Marcos properties benefit significantly from private wells.

Southern California Well Service provides well drilling throughout San Marcos. We serve residential properties, agricultural operations, nurseries, and commercial facilities.

Why San Marcos Properties Need Wells

  • Estate properties — Lake San Marcos and larger lots
  • Agricultural operations — Twin Oaks Valley farming
  • Nurseries — Commercial plant production
  • Cost savings — Reduce Vallecitos Water District bills
  • Drought protection — Maintain landscapes during restrictions

San Marcos property owners drill wells for various purposes:

Well Depths in San Marcos

Well depths vary across San Marcos:

Typical Depth Ranges

Area Typical Depth
Valley/lowland 150-280 ft
Lake San Marcos 200-350 ft
Twin Oaks Valley 180-320 ft
Hills/elevated 250-450 ft

Yield Expectations

  • Lower producers: 10-25 GPM
  • Average: 30-75 GPM
  • Good producers: 80-200+ GPM

Local Geology

  • Alluvium: Twin Oaks Valley, San Marcos Creek
  • Santiago Formation: Sandstone/conglomerate
  • Granite: Some hillside areas
