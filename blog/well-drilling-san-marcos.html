San Marcos Hydrogeology

San Marcos geology includes weathered and fractured crystalline bedrock typical of inland North County, with some areas featuring sedimentary formations. Groundwater occurrence varies with these geological conditions.

  • Fractured Granite: Water stored in rock fracture systems throughout much of the area.
  • Weathered Zones: Decomposed rock near the surface providing storage capacity.
  • Alluvial Deposits: Valley bottoms may access productive unconsolidated aquifers.
  • Variable Depths: Wells range from 200 to over 600 feet depending on location.
