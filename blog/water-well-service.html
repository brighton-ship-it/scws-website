Types of Water Well Service

Emergency Well Service

When your well stops producing water, you need help fast. Emergency water well service addresses urgent situations:

  • Complete loss of water — pump failure or electrical problems
  • Sudden pressure loss — pressure tank or switch failure
  • Pump running constantly — leak or check valve failure
  • Contamination events — requiring immediate testing and treatment
  • Lightning strikes — damaging pump motors or controls

For more information on emergency situations, see our emergency well repair guide.

Preventive Maintenance Service

Regular maintenance prevents emergencies and extends equipment life. Annual water well service should include:

  • Flow rate testing — verifying adequate water production
  • Pressure system check — testing tank pre-charge and switch operation
  • Electrical inspection — checking connections, amp draw, and safety devices
  • Wellhead inspection — ensuring cap seal and sanitary conditions
  • Water quality sampling — testing for bacteria and basic chemistry

Learn more in our complete well maintenance guide.

Pump Service

Your well pump is the heart of your water system. Professional pump service includes:

  • Performance diagnosis — testing flow rate and pressure
  • Pump pulling — removing pumps for inspection or replacement
  • Motor testing — checking windings and amp draw
  • Pump replacement — installing new pumps when needed
  • System optimization — matching pump to well capacity

For signs you need pump service, see signs your well pump is failing.

Water Quality Service

Safe water is the ultimate goal. Water quality services include:

  • Bacteria testing — annual coliform and E. coli testing
  • Chemical analysis — testing for nitrates, arsenic, and minerals
  • Shock chlorination — treating bacterial contamination
  • Treatment system installation — filtration, softening, UV, RO systems
  • Treatment system maintenance — filter changes and system checks

Learn more in our water testing guide.

Signs You Need Water Well Service

Don't wait for a complete failure. These warning signs indicate you should call for water well service:

Pressure Problems

  • ⚠️ Low water pressure throughout the house
  • ⚠️ Pressure fluctuates while using water
  • ⚠️ Pump cycles on and off frequently (short cycling)
  • ⚠️ Pressure gauge shows abnormal readings

Water Quality Issues

  • ⚠️ Discolored or cloudy water
  • ⚠️ Rotten egg smell (sulfur)
  • ⚠️ Metallic taste or rusty color
  • ⚠️ Sediment or particles in water
  • ⚠️ Air sputtering from faucets

Pump and System Issues

  • ⚠️ Pump runs continuously
  • ⚠️ Higher than normal electric bills
  • ⚠️ Strange noises from pump or pressure tank
  • ⚠️ Breaker trips when pump runs
  • ⚠️ No water at all

For troubleshooting, see our guides on low water pressure, no water from well, and pump runs constantly.
