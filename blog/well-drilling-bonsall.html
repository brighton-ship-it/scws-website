Well Drilling in Bonsall

Bonsall — a rural community along the San Luis Rey River corridor — is prime agricultural and equestrian country. With citrus and avocado orchards, horse properties, and estate homes, Bonsall properties frequently rely on private wells.

Southern California Well Service provides comprehensive well drilling services throughout Bonsall and surrounding areas. We understand the agricultural water demands of this community.

Why Bonsall Needs Wells

  • Agricultural economy — Citrus and avocado orchards
  • Limited municipal water — Many properties off grid
  • Equestrian lifestyle — Horse properties throughout
  • Cost savings — Well water far cheaper
  • Drought protection — Reliable supply

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep are wells in Bonsall?

Wells typically range from 100-500 feet deep. River valley properties often find water at 100-250 feet.

Is Bonsall good for wells?

Yes, especially along the San Luis Rey River corridor. The alluvial aquifer is very productive.

What yields can I expect?

River valley: 50-250+ GPM. Hillsides: typically 10-50 GPM.

Do you handle orchard wells?

Yes, we specialize in agricultural wells with high-capacity pump systems.
