Understanding Nuevo's Groundwater

Nuevo sits in the eastern portion of the San Jacinto Valley groundwater basin, an important water resource for Riverside County. Understanding local groundwater conditions helps property owners make informed decisions about well drilling and maintenance.

San Jacinto Valley Basin

The San Jacinto Valley basin underlies much of the area from San Jacinto through Nuevo and toward Perris. Recharged by mountain runoff and the San Jacinto River system, this basin has provided water for agricultural and residential use for over a century. Basin management by local water agencies helps maintain long-term availability.

Local Geology

Nuevo's geology consists of alluvial deposits from ancient river and stream systems. Sand, gravel, and clay layers create the aquifer system. Well yields vary based on which formations are encountered, making professional site assessment valuable before drilling.

Typical Nuevo Well Depths

  • Central Nuevo: 200-350 feet
  • Lakeview area: 250-400 feet
  • East toward Homeland: 200-350 feet
  • North toward San Jacinto: 300-450 feet
  • Near Lakeview Mountains: 350-500 feet
