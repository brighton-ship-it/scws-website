Banging Pipes When Well Pump Runs

Do your pipes bang loudly when the well pump is running? This noise, known as water hammer, can be quite disruptive.

What Causes Water Hammer?

Water hammer is typically caused by rapid changes in water flow or pressure within the plumbing system. This can occur when a pump starts or stops suddenly, causing the water to change direction abruptly.

Installing Arrestors

One solution to prevent banging pipes is to install water hammer arrestors. These devices absorb the shock of sudden water movement, reducing noise and protecting your plumbing system.

FAQs

  • Can water hammer damage my pipes? Yes, over time it can cause significant damage to the plumbing.
  • Where should arrestors be installed? They are best installed near the source of the water hammer, such as the well pump.
