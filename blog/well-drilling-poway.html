Drilling Conditions in Poway

Well drilling in Poway typically encounters Poway conglomerate and underlying granodiorite. The deeper average depth means drilling often takes 3-5 days depending on formation hardness. Hard granite formations may require air rotary or mud rotary methods to maintain borehole stability.

San Diego County requires a well permit from the Department of Environmental Health (DEH) before drilling can begin. The permit process typically takes 2-4 weeks and costs $800-$1,500 depending on the parcel. We handle the entire permit process for Poway properties.
