Why Poway Properties Benefit from Wells

Substantial Cost Savings

City of Poway water rates include tiered pricing that makes high-volume irrigation expensive. Large properties spending $400-1,200+ monthly on landscape water can often recoup well installation costs within a few years, with ongoing savings thereafter.

Drought Protection

During drought restrictions, landscape allocations may be limited. Well owners maintain control over irrigation schedules, protecting valuable mature landscaping through dry periods.

Horse Property Water

Equestrian properties need water for pastures, arenas, and livestock. A dedicated well provides water independence for these substantial demands.

Property Value

A functioning irrigation well demonstrates water security and reduced operating costs—valuable features for Poway's premium properties.

Poway's Groundwater

Poway's geology generally provides favorable conditions for wells, with decomposed granite being the predominant formation.

Decomposed Granite

Most of Poway sits on weathered granitic rock that stores groundwater in fractures and weathered zones. This geology typically produces reliable wells with proper site selection.

Valley Areas

Properties along Poway Valley and near Poway Creek may access alluvial deposits that supplement fractured rock storage, sometimes allowing shallower wells with good production.

Higher Elevations

Properties at higher elevations toward Mount Woodson or Green Valley may require deeper drilling to reach adequate water zones.

Well Services for Poway

Estate Irrigation Wells

Large properties with extensive landscaping can dramatically reduce water costs with dedicated irrigation wells. We design systems that integrate seamlessly with existing landscape controllers and provide the pressure your system requires.

Equestrian Property Wells

Horse properties need reliable water for pastures, arenas, stables, and livestock. We design systems capable of meeting these substantial and varied demands.

Pool and Water Feature Supply

Pools and water features consume significant water. Using well water for filling and top-offs avoids premium municipal rates for these high-volume uses.

Well Rehabilitation

Existing wells showing declining production may benefit from rehabilitation:

  • Hydrofracturing: Opening new fractures
  • Well Cleaning: Removing mineral deposits
  • Pump Upgrades: Modern, efficient equipment
  • Deepening: Accessing additional water zones

FAQs

Do I need permits for a well in Poway?

Yes, San Diego County requires well permits. We handle permitting as part of our service.

Can I use the well for household water too?

Yes, though many Poway homeowners prefer using well water for irrigation while keeping municipal water for household use. Treatment may be needed for household use.

What's the payback period for an irrigation well?

It depends on your current water bills. Properties spending $500+ monthly on irrigation typically see payback in 3-5 years, with decades of savings thereafter.

How long does installation take?

Drilling takes 3-5 days typically. Total project time including permits and completion is usually 4-6 weeks.
