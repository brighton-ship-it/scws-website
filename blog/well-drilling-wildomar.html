Understanding Wildomar's Hydrogeology

Wildomar sits in a transitional zone between the Santa Rosa Plateau highlands and the Temescal Valley, creating varied geological conditions that require experienced drillers to navigate successfully. The area's groundwater characteristics vary significantly based on property location.

Properties in the valley areas often access water from alluvial deposits—sands and gravels deposited by ancient waterways that now contain productive aquifers. These wells may reach water at relatively shallow depths, though production rates vary based on aquifer thickness and quality.

Hillside properties and areas toward the plateau encounter different conditions, often requiring drilling through decomposed granite or fractured bedrock to reach water-bearing zones. These wells typically run deeper and may produce from fracture systems rather than traditional aquifer materials.

SCWS brings the experience to succeed in both environments, properly selecting drilling methods and well construction approaches based on each property's specific conditions.

Horse Property Well Solutions

Wildomar's equestrian community has specific water requirements that go beyond typical residential needs. Horses consume 10-15 gallons per day each—more in hot weather—and horse properties often include wash racks, irrigated pastures, and arena watering systems that significantly increase water demand.

SCWS designs well systems for horse properties with adequate capacity for peak demand periods. We consider factors like the number of horses, pasture irrigation needs, and facility requirements to properly size wells and pumping equipment. Our goal is a system that never leaves you short during summer months when water consumption peaks.

For existing horse properties experiencing water limitations, we offer solutions including well rehabilitation, pump upgrades, and storage tank additions to meet expanding needs without necessarily drilling a new well.

Well Rehabilitation Services

Wildomar properties with existing wells often have systems dating back years or decades. These wells may be experiencing declining production, water quality changes, or aging equipment that's becoming unreliable. SCWS provides comprehensive rehabilitation services that can restore performance at a fraction of new well costs.

  • Video Inspection: Downhole camera surveys reveal casing condition, perforations, and potential problems.
  • Well Cleaning: Removing mineral deposits, biological growth, and sediment that restrict water flow.
  • Hydrofracturing: High-pressure water treatment to open new fractures and improve bedrock well production.
  • Well Deepening: Drilling deeper to access additional water-bearing zones.
  • Pump System Upgrades: Modern, efficient equipment to optimize performance and reduce operating costs.

Our Drilling Process

SCWS follows a systematic approach to well drilling that maximizes success while minimizing disruption to your property. Every project begins with thorough planning and ends with a properly documented, fully functional water system.

We start with a comprehensive site evaluation, reviewing geological data, neighboring well records, and conducting an on-site assessment. This information guides drilling location selection and helps us set appropriate expectations for depth, production, and project timeline.

Our drilling crews arrive with properly maintained, professional equipment suited to Wildomar's varied conditions. We use air rotary methods for most applications, allowing us to efficiently penetrate hard formations while monitoring water production in real-time.

After drilling, we develop the well to maximize production, collect water samples for testing, and install appropriately sized pumping equipment. You receive complete documentation including well logs, test results, and equipment specifications.
