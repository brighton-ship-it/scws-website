What to Do Before We Arrive

While waiting for your technician, these steps can protect your equipment and speed up the diagnosis:

  • Turn off the pump breaker immediately — A pump running without water can burn out the motor in minutes. Flip the breaker labeled "well pump" in your electrical panel and leave it off until the technician arrives.
  • Check for simple causes — Is a valve closed? Did a breaker trip? Was there a recent power outage? These are the first things we'll check on arrival, and if you've already ruled them out, we can move straight to deeper diagnosis.
  • Note the timeline — When did the problem start? Was it sudden or gradual? Were you running a lot of water (irrigation, laundry, etc.) when it happened? This information helps the technician narrow down the cause.

Common Well Problems in Glen Avon and Jurupa Valley

The Glen Avon area has specific well characteristics that influence the types of problems we see most frequently:

  • Aging well infrastructure — Many Glen Avon properties have wells that were drilled 30, 40, or even 50+ years ago for agricultural use and later converted to residential service. These older wells may have deteriorating steel casings, outdated pump equipment, and piping that's reaching the end of its service life. Proactive inspection and upgrades can prevent emergency failures.
  • Hard water and scale buildup — The alluvial aquifers in the Santa Ana River corridor produce water with moderate to high hardness. Over years, calcium and magnesium scale builds up inside pipes, pump intakes, and pressure tanks, gradually restricting flow until the system can no longer deliver adequate pressure. Regular water softener maintenance and periodic well flushing help prevent scale-related failures.
  • Nitrate contamination risk — The Jurupa Valley area has a history of agricultural and dairy operations, which can contribute nitrates to groundwater. Shallow wells (under 100 feet) in this area should be tested annually for nitrates, especially if there are or were agricultural operations nearby.
  • Power quality issues — Rural electrical service in parts of Glen Avon can experience voltage drops and surges, particularly during high-demand periods or severe weather. These electrical fluctuations damage control boxes, capacitors, and pump motors over time. Installing a surge protector and motor protection relay can prevent many electrical-related well failures.

We service all major pump brands including Franklin Electric, Grundfos, Goulds (Xylem), and Sta-Rite (Pentair). Our trucks carry common parts and components for same-day repairs.

Preventing Well Emergencies in Glen Avon

While we're always here for emergencies, most well failures are preventable with basic maintenance. For Glen Avon properties with well water, we recommend this annual maintenance checklist:

  • Annual water quality testing — Test for bacteria (coliform and E. coli), nitrates, and general mineral content. The agricultural history of the Jurupa Valley area makes nitrate testing especially important for wells under 150 feet.
  • Pressure tank inspection — Check the air charge in your pressure tank with a tire gauge at the Schrader valve. It should match your cut-in pressure (usually 28-38 PSI). A waterlogged tank means the bladder has failed and replacement is needed before it damages your pump.
  • Electrical system check — Visually inspect the pressure switch contacts for pitting or burning, check wire connections for corrosion, and verify the control box isn't overheating. These components degrade gradually and can be caught before they cause a complete failure.
  • Well cap inspection — Ensure the sanitary well cap is intact and properly sealed. A damaged cap allows insects, rodents, and surface water to enter the well, leading to contamination and potential damage to the pump wiring.
  • Flow rate monitoring — If you notice your water pressure gradually declining or the pump running longer than it used to, don't ignore it. Gradual performance decline usually indicates a developing problem (wearing pump, clogging screen, or dropping water level) that's much cheaper to address before it becomes a complete failure.

We offer scheduled maintenance visits for Glen Avon properties that include a comprehensive system check, water level measurement, pump performance test, and basic water quality screening. Catching problems early saves you the stress and expense of emergency repairs.
