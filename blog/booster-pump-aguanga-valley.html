Frequently Asked Questions

What size well pump do I need?

Pump sizing depends on your well depth, water demand, and pipe diameter. A typical residential home needs 10-20 GPM. Our technicians calculate the exact Total Dynamic Head (TDH) to match the right pump — usually a Franklin or Grundfos submersible for wells over 25 feet.

How long does a well pump last?

Quality submersible pumps (Franklin Electric, Grundfos) typically last 8-15 years. Lifespan depends on water quality, usage patterns, and whether a proper pressure tank is installed to reduce cycling.

What brands do you install?

We primarily install Franklin Electric and Grundfos submersible pumps — the two most reliable brands in the industry. For specialized applications, we also work with Goulds (Xylem) and Sta-Rite (Pentair).
