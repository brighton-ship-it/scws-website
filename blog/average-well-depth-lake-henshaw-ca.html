Geology and Groundwater in Lake Henshaw

Wells in Lake Henshaw reach moderate depths, averaging 295 feet. The area features a mix of alluvial deposits and bedrock formations, with depth varying based on specific location and proximity to fracture zones. San Diego County geology in this region typically involves decomposed granite overlaying harder rock, with groundwater found in fractures and weathered zones.

The average static water level (depth to water when the pump is off) in Lake Henshaw wells is approximately 54 feet below ground surface.
