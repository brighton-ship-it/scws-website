Los Angeles County Well Overview

28,000 Total registered water wells in Los Angeles County

Source: LA County Environmental Health, California DWR

While Los Angeles County is best known for its massive imported water infrastructure, private water wells serve significant populations in Antelope Valley, Santa Clarita Valley, and unincorporated foothill communities.

Key LA County Well Facts

  • 28,000+ registered wells serving outlying communities
  • 175,000+ residents rely on private wells
  • 17 groundwater basins in county
  • Antelope Valley: Largest private well population
  • Santa Clarita Valley: Mix of private wells and municipal
  • 98% of urban LA relies on imported/municipal water
  • SGMA basins: Antelope Valley critically overdrafted

Frequently Asked Questions

How many wells are in Los Angeles County?

LA County has approximately 28,000 registered water wells, mostly in Antelope Valley and Santa Clarita.

What is the average well depth?

Residential wells average 300-450 feet. Antelope Valley typically 350-600 feet.

How much does it cost to drill a well in LA County?

A complete residential well costs $28,000-$48,000.

Where are private wells used in LA County?

Private wells serve Antelope Valley (Lancaster, Palmdale), Santa Clarita Valley, Acton, Agua Dulce, and mountain communities.
