Yolo County Well Overview

Yolo County, located in California's Sacramento Valley, relies heavily on groundwater for agriculture. The county overlies the Yolo Subbasin, a significant groundwater resource with an estimated 14 million acre-feet of storage capacity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many wells are in Yolo County?

Yolo County has approximately 8,450 registered wells, with 4,200 domestic and 3,150 agricultural wells.

What is the Yolo Subbasin storage capacity?

The Yolo Subbasin has an estimated 14 million acre-feet of storage capacity between 20 and 420 feet depth.

Is Yolo County groundwater sustainable?

The Yolo Subbasin is classified as High Priority under SGMA. The Yolo Subbasin Groundwater Agency manages sustainability with a 2042 deadline.

What is the average well depth?

Domestic wells average 245 feet; agricultural wells average 485 feet.
