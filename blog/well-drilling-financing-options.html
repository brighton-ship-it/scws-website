Drilling Conditions in Financing Options

Well drilling in Financing Options typically encounters the Peninsular Ranges batholith, primarily granitic and metamorphic rock. Most wells can be completed in 1-3 days under normal conditions. The relatively moderate depths keep drilling costs reasonable, though rocky formations can slow progress.

San Diego County requires a well permit from the Department of Environmental Health (DEH) before drilling can begin. The permit process typically takes 2-4 weeks and costs $800-$1,500 depending on the parcel. We handle the entire permit process for Financing Options properties.
