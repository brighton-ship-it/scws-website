Booster Pump Installation in Glen Avon

Low water pressure? A booster pump system can solve pressure problems throughout your home or property. Southern California Well Service installs and services booster pumps in Glen Avon and all of Riverside County.

Benefits of a Booster Pump

  • Consistent pressure — Even flow to all fixtures
  • Multi-story homes — Adequate pressure on upper floors
  • Irrigation systems — Proper pressure for sprinklers
  • Long distances — Maintain pressure over long pipe runs

Booster Pump Options

  • Constant pressure systems — Variable frequency drive (VFD) for steady pressure
  • Single-stage boosters — Simple pressure increase
  • Multi-stage systems — For significant pressure gains

FAQs: Booster Pumps

Do I need a booster pump?

If you have low pressure at fixtures, inconsistent flow, or pressure drops when multiple taps run, a booster pump may help.

How much does a booster pump cost?

Booster pump systems typically range from $1,500-$5,000 installed, depending on capacity and features.
