Frequently Asked Questions

How many permits are issued annually?

Approximately 500,000 new permits are issued annually in the US.

What are typical permit fees?

Fees range from $50-300 to $1,500+, averaging $350-500 nationally.

Which state issues the most permits?

Texas leads with ~65,000 permits annually, followed by California (~45,000).

How long does processing take?

Average is 7-14 business days; some states process in 1-3 days.
