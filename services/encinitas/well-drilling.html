Why Encinitas Geology Matters

Coastal beach town. Almost entirely on municipal water. Private wells mainly in Olivenhain (rural eastern area). High property values. Surf culture.

This geological context means Encinitas wells require: 200-350ft depths to reach productive water-bearing zones in coastal sandstone and limited alluvial.

New Well Drilling Process

  1. Site evaluation: Review property, existing wells nearby, geology maps
  2. Permits: Pull San Diego County well permits
  3. Drilling: Rotary or cable tool drilling through coastal sandstone and limited alluvial to 200-350ft
  4. Well development: Pump and clean well to maximize yield (target: 5-12 GPM)
  5. Pump installation: Size and install submersible pump based on actual yield
  6. Water testing: Test for bacteria, minerals, and very limited private wells - mostly municipal water
  7. System completion: Pressure tank, controls, final inspection
