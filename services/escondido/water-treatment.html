Water Treatment for Escondido Wells

Escondido well water comes from Santiago Peak volcanics and Escondido Formation sedimentary, which creates specific water quality challenges that require targeted treatment solutions.

What's in Escondido Well Water?

  • Hardness: 10-20 grains — causes scale buildup, soap scum, appliance damage
  • TDS: 400-700 ppm — affects taste and indicates mineral content
  • Common contaminants: harder water (10-20 grains), iron in volcanic areas, sulfate in sedimentary

Recommended Treatment Systems for Escondido

Water Softener (Essential)

With 10-20 grains hardness, a softener is critical for Escondido homes. Benefits:

  • Eliminates scale buildup on fixtures, water heater, appliances
  • Softer skin and hair, better soap lathering
  • Extends lifespan of well pump and plumbing
  • Reduces water heater energy costs by 25-30%
Iron Filtration (Highly Recommended)

Escondido wells in Santiago Peak volcanics and Escondido Formation sedimentary commonly have iron problems. Our iron filters remove:

  • Ferrous iron (dissolved, clear water iron)
  • Ferric iron (rust stains, orange water)
  • Iron bacteria (slimy buildup in toilets and pipes)
Sulfur/Odor Filtration

Escondido wells in Santiago Peak volcanics and Escondido Formation sedimentary can produce sulfur odor ("rotten egg smell"). Our systems eliminate it completely.
