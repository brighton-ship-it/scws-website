Well Inspections in Twentynine Palms

Wells in Twentynine Palms face unique challenges due to high desert alluvial and granite and local conditions. A thorough inspection identifies problems before they become emergencies.

What We Inspect in Twentynine Palms Wells

Well Casing & Construction

  • Casing integrity: Check for cracks, corrosion, or damage (critical in high desert alluvial and granite)
  • Well seal: Ensure sanitary seal is intact (prevents surface contamination)
  • Depth verification: Confirm well depth matches records (Twentynine Palms wells typically 200-600ft)
  • Screen condition: Check for sand infiltration (common in high desert alluvial and granite)

Pump System

  • Pump performance: Flow rate test (should be 8-25 GPM)
  • Motor condition: Amp draw, voltage, running temperature
  • Pump depth: Verify setting is appropriate for Twentynine Palms water table
  • Drop pipe: Check for leaks, corrosion, or age-related failure

Pressure & Storage

  • Pressure tank: Check pre-charge, waterlogging, bladder condition
  • Pressure switch: Test cut-in/cut-out settings
  • Storage tanks: Inspect condition if applicable (common in Julian, Anza for low-yield wells)

Water Quality

  • Visual inspection: Check for sediment, rust, odor
  • Testing: Bacteria, hardness, TDS, specific contaminants (hard water (14-22 grains), high TDS, variable yields)
  • Flow rate: Measure actual GPM vs. expected (8-25 GPM)

Twentynine Palms-Specific Inspection Focus

Because Twentynine Palms wells are in high desert alluvial and granite, we pay special attention to:

  • Hard water, high TDS (often >1000), variable yields (alluvial better than granite), extreme heat damaging equipment, very remote location, aging infrastructure
  • Geological factors: high desert alluvial and granite can cause specific wear patterns
  • Water quality: hard water (14-22 grains), high TDS, variable yields

When to Get a Twentynine Palms Well Inspection

  • Real estate transactions: Required for buying/selling homes in Twentynine Palms proper, Joshua Tree border, Wonder Valley, Amboy Road area, Marine Corps base
  • Annual maintenance: Recommended for all Twentynine Palms wells
  • After problems: Following pump failure, contamination, or major repairs
  • Pre-winter: Especially for high-elevation Twentynine Palms areas (elevation: 1,900ft)
  • Drought assessment: Check well capacity during low water table periods

Our Inspection Report Includes

  • Water quality test results
