Water Treatment for Julian Wells

Julian well water comes from decomposed granite, deep fractured bedrock, which creates specific water quality challenges that require targeted treatment solutions.

What's in Julian Well Water?

  • Hardness: 6-12 grains — causes scale buildup, soap scum, appliance damage
  • TDS: 200-400 ppm — affects taste and indicates mineral content
  • Common contaminants: low yield common, iron, moderate hardness

Recommended Treatment Systems for Julian

Iron Filtration (Highly Recommended)

Julian wells in decomposed granite, deep fractured bedrock commonly have iron problems. Our iron filters remove:

  • Ferrous iron (dissolved, clear water iron)
  • Ferric iron (rust stains, orange water)
  • Iron bacteria (slimy buildup in toilets and pipes)

System Sizing for Julian Wells

Your well produces 2-5 GPM. We size treatment systems based on:

  • Well flow rate (2-5 GPM)
  • Household size and peak demand
  • Specific contaminants in your water test
  • Water usage patterns (irrigation, livestock, residential)
