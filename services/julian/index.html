Julian's Mountain Well Challenges

Julian isn't like other San Diego County communities when it comes to wells. At 4,200 feet elevation with hard granite bedrock, freezing winters, low-yield fracture wells, and naturally occurring uranium in the geological formation, Julian well systems need a contractor who understands mountain-specific conditions — not just standard valley well work.

We're 30 minutes from Julian via Highway 78. We service wells across Julian proper, Wynola, Santa Ysabel, Harrison Park, Pine Hills, and the surrounding mountain communities. Whether you're a full-time resident, vacation homeowner, or managing a rental property, we understand what Julian wells need.
