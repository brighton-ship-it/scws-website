Julian's Challenging Mountain Well Conditions

Julian's extreme geology: Decomposed granite everywhere at 4,200ft elevation. Wells drill 300-600ft+ to find water in deep fractured zones. Low yield common (2-5 GPM)—enough for a home, not a gusher. This is hard rock mountain drilling in some of San Diego County's toughest terrain.

Freezing temps: Julian drops below 32°F regularly in winter. Above-ground well components need freeze protection (insulated wellheads, heat tape on pressure tanks). We design for mountain conditions.

❄️ Freeze Protection is Mandatory in Julian

Julian regularly drops to 20-30°F in winter. Above-ground well components freeze and rupture if not protected:

  • Pressure tanks: Must be indoors or in insulated, heated pumphouse.
  • Wellheads: Insulated caps, buried pipes below frost line (18-24 inches in Julian).
  • Piping: Heat tape on exposed runs, foam pipe insulation.
  • Pump controls: Indoor installation preferred (control boxes can freeze).

We design Julian well systems for mountain winters. Valley contractors often overlook freeze protection—big mistake at 4,200ft elevation.
