Water Treatment for Fallbrook Wells

Fallbrook well water comes from decomposed granite and weathered gabbroic rock, which creates specific water quality challenges that require targeted treatment solutions.

What's in Fallbrook Well Water?

  • Hardness: 8-14 grains — causes scale buildup, soap scum, appliance damage
  • TDS: 300-500 ppm — affects taste and indicates mineral content
  • Common contaminants: moderate hardness, boron in some areas (bad for avocados)
