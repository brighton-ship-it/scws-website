What to Test For in Fallbrook Wells

The geology in Fallbrook — decomposed granite and weathered gabbroic rock — directly determines what contaminants you're likely to find in your well water.

Common Contaminants in Fallbrook

Based on local geology and decades of testing Fallbrook wells, here's what we typically find:

  • Water hardness: 8-14 grains — this causes scale buildup on fixtures, water heaters, and appliances
  • Total Dissolved Solids (TDS): 300-500 ppm — affects taste and can indicate other contaminants
  • Specific to decomposed granite and weathered gabbroic rock: moderate hardness, boron in some areas (bad for avocados)
