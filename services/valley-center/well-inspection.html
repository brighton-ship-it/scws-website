Well Inspections in Valley Center

Wells in Valley Center face unique challenges due to alluvial valley floor with granite hillsides and local conditions. A thorough inspection identifies problems before they become emergencies.

What We Inspect in Valley Center Wells

Well Casing & Construction

  • Casing integrity: Check for cracks, corrosion, or damage (critical in alluvial valley floor with granite hillsides)
  • Well seal: Ensure sanitary seal is intact (prevents surface contamination)
  • Depth verification: Confirm well depth matches records (Valley Center wells typically 180-500ft)
  • Screen condition: Check for sand infiltration (common in alluvial valley floor with granite hillsides)

Pump System

  • Pump performance: Flow rate test (should be 8-25 GPM)
  • Motor condition: Amp draw, voltage, running temperature
  • Pump depth: Verify setting is appropriate for Valley Center water table
  • Drop pipe: Check for leaks, corrosion, or age-related failure

Pressure & Storage

  • Pressure tank: Check pre-charge, waterlogging, bladder condition
  • Pressure switch: Test cut-in/cut-out settings
  • Storage tanks: Inspect condition if applicable (common in Julian, Anza for low-yield wells)

Water Quality

  • Visual inspection: Check for sediment, rust, odor
  • Testing: Bacteria, hardness, TDS, specific contaminants (hard water in valley (12-20 grains), iron in hillside wells)
  • Flow rate: Measure actual GPM vs. expected (8-25 GPM)

Valley Center-Specific Inspection Focus

Because Valley Center wells are in alluvial valley floor with granite hillsides, we pay special attention to:
