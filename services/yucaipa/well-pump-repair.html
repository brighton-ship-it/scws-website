Common Pump Issues in Yucaipa

Wells in Yucaipa typically range from 200-500ft with geology consisting of foothill alluvial and granite. This means pumps here face unique challenges:

  • Pump depth settings: Most Yucaipa wells require pump settings between 200 and 500ft, which means your pump is working hard to lift water from deep underground.
  • Typical yields: Yucaipa wells produce 8-20 GPM, which affects pump sizing and horsepower requirements.
  • Local water quality: hard water (10-18 grains), moderate TDS, variable yields — these contaminants can cause premature pump wear and motor failure.

Pump Types for Yucaipa Geology

Because of the foothill alluvial and granite in Yucaipa, we typically install:

  • Submersible pumps for depths over 250 feet (most common in Yucaipa)
  • Specific horsepower: Based on your well's yield (8-20 GPM) and total dynamic head
  • Stainless steel construction to handle hard water (10-18 grains), moderate TDS, variable yields

Signs Your Yucaipa Well Pump is Failing

  • Pressure drops below 40 PSI
  • Pump runs continuously or cycles frequently
  • Rusty or sandy water (common with foothill alluvial and granite)
  • High electric bills (pump working too hard)
  • No water at all (complete failure)
