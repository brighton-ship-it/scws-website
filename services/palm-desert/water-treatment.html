Water Treatment for Palm Desert Wells

Palm Desert well water comes from deep Coachella Valley alluvium, which creates specific water quality challenges that require targeted treatment solutions.

What's in Palm Desert Well Water?

  • Hardness: 20-30 grains — causes scale buildup, soap scum, appliance damage
  • TDS: 900-1800 ppm — affects taste and indicates mineral content
  • Common contaminants: very hard water (20-30 grains), extremely high TDS, arsenic in some wells

Recommended Treatment Systems for Palm Desert

Water Softener (Essential)

With 20-30 grains hardness, a softener is critical for Palm Desert homes. Benefits:

  • Eliminates scale buildup on fixtures, water heater, appliances
  • Softer skin and hair, better soap lathering
  • Extends lifespan of well pump and plumbing
  • Reduces water heater energy costs by 25-30%
Reverse Osmosis (RO) System

With TDS levels up to 1800 ppm in Palm Desert, an RO system provides:

  • Ultra-pure drinking water (removes 95-99% of dissolved solids)
  • Better taste (removes minerals, sulfates, nitrates)
  • Typically installed under kitchen sink

System Sizing for Palm Desert Wells

Your well produces 20-50 GPM. We size treatment systems based on:

  • Well flow rate (20-50 GPM)
  • Household size and peak demand
  • Specific contaminants in your water test
  • Water usage patterns (irrigation, livestock, residential)

Water treatment systems need regular maintenance:
