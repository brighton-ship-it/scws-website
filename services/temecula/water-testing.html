Common Contaminants in Temecula

Based on local geology and decades of testing Temecula wells, here's what we typically find:

  • Water hardness: 10-18 grains — this causes scale buildup on fixtures, water heaters, and appliances
  • Total Dissolved Solids (TDS): 350-600 ppm — affects taste and can indicate other contaminants
  • Specific to mixed granite, alluvial, and sedimentary formations: variable by location, moderate hardness, some iron
