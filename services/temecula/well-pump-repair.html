Common Pump Issues in Temecula

Wells in Temecula typically range from 200-500ft with geology consisting of mixed granite, alluvial, and sedimentary formations. This means pumps here face unique challenges:

  • Pump depth settings: Most Temecula wells require pump settings between 200 and 500ft, which means your pump is working hard to lift water from deep underground.
  • Typical yields: Temecula wells produce 10-30 GPM, which affects pump sizing and horsepower requirements.
  • Local water quality: variable by location, moderate hardness, some iron — these contaminants can cause premature pump wear and motor failure.

Signs Your Temecula Well Pump is Failing

  • Pressure drops below 40 PSI
  • Pump runs continuously or cycles frequently
  • Rusty or sandy water (common with mixed granite, alluvial, and sedimentary formations)
  • High electric bills (pump working too hard)
  • No water at all (complete failure)
