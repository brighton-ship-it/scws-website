🍇 Vineyard Irrigation Well Expertise

Temecula wineries and vineyards have different well demands than residential:

  • Higher flow rates: 15-40 GPM for drip irrigation systems.
  • Water quality matters: High sodium (SAR), boron, or chloride damage grapevines. Test before planting.
  • Seasonal demand: Irrigation pumps run 6-10 hrs/day April-October. Higher duty cycle = more maintenance.
  • Reliability critical: Missing irrigation windows during growing season costs thousands in crop stress.

We work with vineyard managers and ag consultants to design irrigation well systems that meet grape water quality requirements.
