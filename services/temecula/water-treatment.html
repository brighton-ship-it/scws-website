Water Treatment for Temecula Wells

Temecula well water comes from mixed granite, alluvial, and sedimentary formations, which creates specific water quality challenges that require targeted treatment solutions.

What's in Temecula Well Water?

  • Hardness: 10-18 grains — causes scale buildup, soap scum, appliance damage
  • TDS: 350-600 ppm — affects taste and indicates mineral content
  • Common contaminants: variable by location, moderate hardness, some iron

Recommended Treatment Systems for Temecula

Water Softener (Essential)

With 10-18 grains hardness, a softener is critical for Temecula homes. Benefits:

  • Eliminates scale buildup on fixtures, water heater, appliances
  • Softer skin and hair, better soap lathering
  • Extends lifespan of well pump and plumbing
  • Reduces water heater energy costs by 25-30%
Iron Filtration (Highly Recommended)

Temecula wells in mixed granite, alluvial, and sedimentary formations commonly have iron problems. Our iron filters remove:

  • Ferrous iron (dissolved, clear water iron)
  • Ferric iron (rust stains, orange water)
  • Iron bacteria (slimy buildup in toilets and pipes)

System Sizing for Temecula Wells

  • Household size and peak demand
  • Specific contaminants in your water test
