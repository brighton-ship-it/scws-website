What To Do While Waiting

  1. Check Your Breaker: Go to your electrical panel and look for a tripped breaker. If it's tripped, reset it ONCE. If it trips again immediately, leave it off and call us — there's an electrical fault.
  2. Check the Pressure Tank: Look at the gauge on your pressure tank. Normal is 40–60 PSI. If it reads zero or very low, the pump may not be running.
  3. Listen for the Pump: Go to your wellhead or pump house. You should hear the pump running when water is called for. No sound = pump not running.
  4. Don't Run Faucets: If you have partial pressure, don't drain it by running water. You may need that water until we arrive.
  5. Note Any Symptoms: When did it fail? Any unusual sounds? Recent power outages? This helps us diagnose faster.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is considered a well emergency?

A well emergency includes: complete loss of water, pump failure, electrical burning smell from well equipment, continuously running pump, very low water pressure, contaminated water, or any situation where you have livestock or medical needs requiring water.

How fast can you respond to a well emergency?

For emergencies in our primary service area (San Diego and western Riverside County), we typically respond within 2–4 hours. Same-day service is available for most emergency calls received before noon.

What should I do if my well suddenly has no water?

First, check your electrical panel for a tripped breaker—this fixes about 30% of no-water calls. If the breaker is fine, check your pressure tank gauge. If you hear the pump running but have no water, or if the breaker keeps tripping, call us immediately.

Can a drilled well run out of water?

Yes, wells can run dry temporarily or permanently. During droughts or after heavy usage, the water level may drop below the pump intake. Often, letting the well rest for 4–8 hours allows recovery. We can diagnose whether your well is recoverable.

Do you charge extra for emergency service?

We have a modest $75 after-hours fee for calls outside normal business hours. However, we never price-gouge during emergencies. The diagnostic visit is $200, and we'll give you a full quote before any repairs begin.
