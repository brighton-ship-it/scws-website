About Southern California Well Service

Family-owned. Locally operated. Here when you need us.

Our Story

Southern California Well Service started with a simple idea: provide honest, reliable well service to our neighbors. After working in the industry and seeing how some companies operated — hidden fees, unnecessary repairs, slow response times — we knew there had to be a better way.

Today, we're proud to be the well service company that San Diego, Riverside, and San Bernardino County residents trust. When you call us, you're not getting a call center — you're getting real people who live and work in your community.

We answer our phones. We show up when we say we will. We explain what's wrong in plain English and give you options — not just the most expensive one. And we stand behind our work.

Our Heritage

When we say "60+ years of combined family heritage," we mean it. SCWS was founded in 2020 by Brighton Scala and Travis Sego, but our roots in well drilling run much deeper.

Brighton learned the trade working alongside Joe Fain, founder of Fain Drilling—a company with over 60 years of history serving Southern California. Travis started at age 14 with his uncle at Sam Crum Well Drilling, gaining hands-on experience across every type of terrain and well system in the region.

In 2024, we were honored to acquire two trusted names in San Diego's well service industry: Heritage Well Service and Ransom Pump (Ransom Brothers). These acquisitions weren't just business transactions—they were a commitment to continue the legacy of quality service these companies built over decades.

What This Means for You

  • If you're a former Heritage or Ransom customer: Your service records are safe, your warranties are honored, and we're here to help.
  • Combined expertise: We've brought together the best practices from multiple generations of well professionals.
  • The Ransom line still works: Former Ransom Pump customers can call (760) 789-5955 — it forwards to us.

Meet the Founders

Brighton Scala

Co-Founder & Operations • Valley Center Resident

Brighton got his start in the well industry through Joe Fain — founder of Fain Drilling with over 60 years of history in Southern California — after Joe married Brighton's grandmother. That hands-on mentorship shaped everything about how we run this company: show up on time, be honest about what's wrong, and never cut corners on quality. As a Valley Center resident, Brighton takes pride in serving his neighbors throughout San Diego County.

Travis Sego

Co-Founder & Field Operations

Travis started working at age 14 for his uncle at Sam Crum Well Drilling, learning the trade from the ground up. He's drilled and serviced wells across every type of terrain Southern California has to offer — from the granite of Julian to the clay of Ramona to the desert rock of Anza.

When you call SCWS, there's a good chance you'll talk to one of us directly. That's how we like it.
