Vista's Well Water Needs

Vista is known for its agriculture — particularly avocado groves that have thrived here for generations. Many of these operations depend on private wells, especially in the rural eastern areas near Buena Creek and along Gopher Canyon Road.

The geology around Vista varies from alluvial deposits near Buena Vista Creek to harder formations in the hills. Water depths can range from 150 to 500+ feet depending on location. We've worked throughout Vista and understand what it takes to hit good water here.
