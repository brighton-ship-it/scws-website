Desert Well Experts for Jacumba

Jacumba Hot Springs sits at the edge of the Anza-Borrego Desert, right along the Mexican border. Once a resort destination famous for its natural hot springs, today it's a quiet community where residents rely entirely on private wells for water.

We understand desert wells. Jacumba's unique geology — including geothermal activity that creates those famous hot springs — means water quality and temperature can vary significantly. Some wells produce warm water year-round. Mineral content requires consideration for both domestic use and pump longevity.

At San Diego County's eastern edge, Jacumba is far from everything. When you need well service, you need a company willing to make the drive and equipped to handle desert conditions. That's us.
