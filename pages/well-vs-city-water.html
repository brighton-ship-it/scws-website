Well Water: Pros & Cons

✓ Advantages

  • No monthly water bill
  • No chlorine or fluoride
  • Water independence
  • Often better taste
  • Unaffected by droughts/restrictions
  • Property value increase
  • Unlimited water usage

✗ Disadvantages

  • Upfront drilling cost
  • Pump replacement every 15-20 years
  • You're responsible for maintenance
  • May need treatment for hardness/iron
  • No water if power goes out (unless generator)
  • Annual testing recommended

The Verdict

Consider a well if: You're building on rural land, value water independence, use a lot of water (irrigation, livestock), or hate monthly bills.

Stick with city water if: You prefer zero maintenance responsibility, live in an established neighborhood, or can't afford the upfront drilling cost.

For most rural properties in San Diego County, a well pays for itself in 15-20 years — and you own your water forever after that.
