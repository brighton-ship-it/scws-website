Featured Case Study: Ranch Well System

The Challenge

50-acre horse ranch in Valley Center with declining well output. Original well drilled in 1985 producing only 3 GPM — not enough for livestock and irrigation.

Our Solution

Video inspection revealed collapsed casing. Drilled new well 200 feet from original, hit excellent water at 340 feet. Installed 3HP pump with 1,000-gallon storage tank.

The Result

25 GPM sustained yield — 8x the previous output. Ranch now has abundant water for horses, arena dust control, and pasture irrigation.
