Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 19, 2026

Information We Collect

Southern California Well Service ("we", "us", or "our") collects information in the following ways:

  • Information You Provide Directly: This includes name, address, phone number, and email when you request services, submit quotes, or contact us.
  • Automatically Collected Information: We use technologies like cookies and analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics) to collect data about your interactions with our website, such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and actions taken (e.g., call clicks or form submissions). This helps us improve our services and track website performance.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide well drilling, pump repair, and related services
  • Communicate about appointments and service updates
  • Send invoices and process payments
  • Improve our services and website functionality through analytics
  • Track user engagement and conversions (e.g., call tracking for marketing effectiveness)

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience and analyze website usage. Specifically:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function properly (e.g., navigation and form submissions).
  • Analytics Cookies: Set by Google Analytics to track user behavior, helping us understand how visitors use our site. This includes data like page views, time spent on pages, and conversion events.

You can manage cookie preferences through the consent banner displayed on your first visit to our site, where you can accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can also adjust cookie settings in your browser at any time.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Third parties who assist our operations (e.g., payment processors, scheduling software, analytics providers like Google) under confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

For analytics, data is shared with Google Analytics under their data processing terms. You can opt out of analytics tracking via our cookie consent tool or by using browser extensions like the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on.

Your Privacy Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and other applicable laws, you have rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of the personal information we collect, use, or share about you.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information we hold.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of the sale of your personal information. While we do not sell data directly, you can opt out of analytics tracking through our cookie consent tool.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below. We will respond within the timeframes required by law (e.g., 45 days under CCPA). For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) apply, including the right to withdraw consent for data processing.
