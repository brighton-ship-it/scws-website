## Executive Summary

### Overall SEO Health: **B+ (85/100)**

**Overall Assessment:** SCWS has a strong technical foundation with excellent content depth (4,150+ blog pages), clean technical implementation, and solid schema markup. The site ranks well for many informational queries but suffers from **low click-through rates** on high-impression pages and **minimal local traffic** (only 3.6% of organic sessions are from the service area). The primary opportunities lie in optimizing meta descriptions/titles for higher CTR, improving local SEO signals, and reducing keyword cannibalization.

### Top 3 Priorities

1. **🎯 Improve CTR on High-Impression Pages** (Quick Win, High Impact)
   - Pages like "well-drilling-cost-statistics-by-state.html" get 12,228 impressions but only 0.1% CTR at position 3.9
   - Homepage gets 5,121 impressions with 1.1% CTR at position 23.9 (should be ranking #1 for brand)
   - Estimated impact: +150-300 clicks/month with better meta descriptions and titles

2. **🏠 Fix Local Traffic Problem** (Critical, High Impact)
   - Only 6 of 166 daily organic sessions are from San Diego/Riverside/San Bernardino counties (3.6%)
   - Site ranks nationally but not capturing local intent effectively
   - Need stronger local schema, GMB integration, and local keyword optimization
   - Estimated impact: +50-100 local sessions/day within 90 days

3. **🔄 Resolve Keyword Cannibalization** (Medium Effort, Medium Impact)
   - "Southern California Well Service" splits across 9 URLs (homepage, contact, blog, anchors)
   - "Well pump replacement cost" cannibalizes across 2 strong pages
   - Consolidate signals via canonical tags and internal linking
   - Estimated impact: +5-15 positions on key commercial terms
