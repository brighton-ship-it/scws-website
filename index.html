About Southern California Well Service

Founded in 2020 by Brighton and Travis Sego, Southern California Well Service brings generations of well drilling heritage to San Diego, Riverside, and San Bernardino Counties.

Travis started working at age 14 for his uncle at Sam Crum Well Drilling, learning the trade from the ground up. Brighton got his start from Joe Fain—founder of Fain Drilling with over 60 years of history in Southern California—after Joe married Brighton's grandmother.

That multi-generational experience, combined with modern technology and genuine care for our customers, is what sets us apart. When your well has a problem, you need someone who shows up fast, diagnoses accurately, and fixes it right the first time. That's what we do.
