Common Problems We Fix:

  • No water from well — pump failure, dropped water table, electrical issues
  • Pump runs constantly — pressure switch, leak in system, waterlogged tank
  • Pump won't turn on — electrical, control box, breaker issues
  • Low water pressure — worn impellers, pressure tank, clogged pipes
  • Pump making noise — bearings, cavitation, loose components
  • Pump cycling on/off — pressure tank, switch, small leaks
  • High electric bills — inefficient pump, constantly running
