Common Well Emergencies We Handle

💧 No Water At All

Complete loss of water supply. Could be pump failure, electrical issue, or depleted well. We diagnose and fix fast.

⚡ Pump Won't Start

Pump motor failure, blown capacitor, or control box issue. We carry common parts for same-day repair.

🔄 Pump Running Non-Stop

Continuous running means pressure tank failure or leak. This burns out pumps fast — call immediately.

📉 Sudden Low Pressure

Dramatic pressure drop could indicate pump wear, clogged screen, or failing pressure tank.

🟤 Dirty or Muddy Water

Sediment, sand, or discoloration. Could be well collapse, screen failure, or aquifer issue.

💥 Strange Noises

Banging pipes, clicking at the pressure switch, or grinding sounds. Early warning signs of bigger problems.

What To Do While You Wait

Check Breaker

2

Check Pressure

Look at your pressure tank gauge. Normal is 40-60 PSI. Zero means no pressure.

3

Save Water

Conserve what's in your pressure tank. Don't run faucets or flush unnecessarily.
